Build it this way — every Turkish word above stands exactly as written; each <…> is yours to fill:
- One table, the identity card: exactly these six rows, in this order, their `Alan` cells written as above.
- Each `Değer` says what the rows say and ends with the ids it stands on.
- A field no row speaks to is `—` and cites nothing: zero is an answer.
- Nothing is estimated: a number is one a row states.
