## Şekil — Pazar levhası

| Ölçü | Değer | Yıl/tarih | Kaynak |
|---|---|---|---|
| <what is measured> | <the number, as a row states it> | <its year or date> | <who says it> [L0042] |
| <another measure> | — | — | — |

Build it this way — every Turkish word above stands exactly as written; each <…> is yours to fill:
- One table, the number board: one row per number the rows bear on the question — a market's size, a price,
  a demand, a growth rate, a count of buyers or sellers —, in the order the answer needs them.
- `Değer` is the number exactly as a row states it, with its unit and currency. Nothing is estimated,
  converted, averaged or summed by you; two rows that give two numbers for one measure are two rows.
- `Yıl/tarih`: the year or date the row gives the number for; none given: `—`.
- `Kaynak`: who says it — a report, a company, a user — in at most four Turkish words, never an address or
  a domain, then its ids.
- A measure the question asks for and no row gives still stands, `—` in every other cell: zero is an answer.
- No `Toplam` row. A total a row itself states is an ordinary row, named for what it measures (e.g.
  `Pazar büyüklüğü`), with its ids.
