## Prompt

Photograph {device} on {setting}, framed for {aspect}, the screen the clear focus, facing the camera within 20 degrees so its four corners are all visible and sharp. The screen is switched on but shows {screen} edge to edge, perfectly even, with no interface, icons, reflections of the room or glare across it, so a real screenshot can be laid onto it in post. The device is real but anonymous: thin bezels, brushed aluminium or matte glass back, keycaps blank, no maker's mark, a faint fingerprint on the frame, tiny dust on the surface. Around it, one or two quiet objects, a ceramic cup and a closed notebook with a plain cover, kept small and soft. Lighting: soft window daylight from camera-left, a white bounce right, physically plausible shadows under the device. Full-frame camera, 50 mm lens at f/4, camera slightly above the screen's centre. Restrained grade, true colour, fine grain. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere, on the device or around it.

## Notes

- media-still or codex-image: text-only; no input needed. Lay the real screenshot in post with a corner-pin, then add a faint glass reflection over it.
- Chroma green spills on glossy edges; choose mid-grey when the device is close to the lens.
