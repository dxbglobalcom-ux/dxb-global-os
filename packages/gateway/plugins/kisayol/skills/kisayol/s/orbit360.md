## Prompt

Camera: a full 360-degree orbit. The camera circles the product clockwise, seen from above, at a constant radius and a constant height slightly above its centre, tilted down about ten degrees, at one steady speed for the whole clip. It opens on the product's front three-quarter view and ends exactly where it began, so the clip loops. The product stays centred and the same size; the background wheels around it, and the fixed set lights travel across its surfaces as the angle turns. Sides the product photo does not show are completed plainly and conservatively, with no invented features or decoration. No zoom, no drift in height or radius. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- h3 only, from a scene base: a single still cannot show the back. Check the 360view sheet against the real product before this runs for a client.
- For a turntable feel with a locked camera, the 360view note (h3-from-still spin) is the better path.
