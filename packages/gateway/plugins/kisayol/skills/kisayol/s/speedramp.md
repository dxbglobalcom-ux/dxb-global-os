## Prompt

Effect: a speed ramp on the main action. The action runs at real speed, then at its most telling instant slows smoothly to about one fifth of real speed for roughly a second, so every detail reads (a drop hanging in the air, fibres lifting, a reflection sliding across the product), then ramps smoothly back to real speed. The change of speed is eased, never a jump. Physics stays true at every speed: motion blur shrinks as the action slows and returns as it speeds up, liquids and fabric keep their weight, the camera's own move slows in step. The product keeps its exact shape, colours and label throughout.
