## Prompt

Camera: a slow dolly in. The camera travels straight forward on a level track toward the product, lens at the product's centre height, at an even, unhurried pace with a gentle start and a gentle stop. It opens on a medium shot with the product filling about a third of the frame and ends on a close shot with the product filling about two thirds, still centred, its whole silhouette in frame. Height, lens and direction stay locked; only the distance closes, so near and far layers shift with real parallax. Focus stays on the product throughout. No zoom, no speed change. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- h3-from-still: the still is the opening frame; the closing frame is read from it, so start from a still with room around the product.
- The safest move in the shelf: the one to try first when a base has no move named.
