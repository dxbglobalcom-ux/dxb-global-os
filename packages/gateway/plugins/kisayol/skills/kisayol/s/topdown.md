## Prompt

Composition: top-down: the camera directly overhead, sensor parallel to the surface, so the product and a few props lie flat in true plan view with no perspective lean. Props are spaced deliberately on a loose grid, the product the largest and most central object; soft shadows fall to one side from a single high source. The frame edges cut nothing important.

## Notes

- Fits overhead bases: flat lays, set tables, ingredient spreads; in ingredientspread and backtoschool keep the straight-overhead view.
