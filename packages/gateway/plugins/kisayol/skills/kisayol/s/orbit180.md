## Prompt

Camera: a half orbit of 180 degrees. The camera circles the product at a constant radius and a constant height slightly above its centre, tilted down about ten degrees, at a steady, unhurried speed with soft ease in and out. It opens on the product's left side in profile, passes its front face at the midpoint and ends on its right side in profile. The product stays centred and the same size throughout, while the background wheels slowly behind it. The lights stay fixed in the set, so highlights travel naturally across its surfaces as the angle changes. No zoom, no wobble in the radius. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

- h3 only: half the product's sides are not in a single still; with h3-from-still the engine invents them. Use the 360view sheet to see those sides first.
