## Prompt

Camera: a slow push-in to macro. The camera opens on a close shot of the whole product and moves straight in at a slow, even pace until one real detail fills the frame: the material's surface, an edge, a seam, a cap or a closure. Focus follows smoothly as the distance closes, and the depth of field thins to a few millimetres, so at the end only the detail is sharp. The final frame shows real texture: grain, fibres, brushed lines, fine scratches. The detail is one the product has in the photo; nothing is added to it. No zoom, no change of light. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- Say which detail in the job: the camera must land on something the photo really shows.
- Pairs naturally with a macro or material base.
