## Prompt

Camera: an FPV drone flight, seen from the drone itself. A wide lens, flying fast but smooth at waist height through the set: it enters through an opening in the space, glides along it, banks gently through one curve and slows into a steady hover on the product, ending on a close-medium shot with the product centred. The horizon stays level except in the gentle banks; the flight path never passes through solid objects and every surface keeps its real scale. The speed falls continuously from fast to still; the last part of the clip is calm. No shadow of the drone, no lens warp beyond a real wide lens. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- h3 only, from a scene base that names a space (store, workshop, room): a single product still has no space to fly through.
