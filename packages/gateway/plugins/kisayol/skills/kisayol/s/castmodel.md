## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the garment: keep its exact cut, proportions, colour, fabric, stitching and trims as photographed; do not redraw, restyle or recolour it. The wearer is the person in the attached cast photo, an adult man or an elderly woman from our own cast, with exactly that face, hair and build. Photograph them {framing} in a {setting}, framed for {aspect}. The garment is worn as designed and fits as it would on that real body: true drape, the fabric pulling at the shoulder when an arm moves, a natural crease at the elbow. The styling is modest and complete, the pose calm and unposed: weight on one leg, one hand resting at the side or adjusting a cuff. Real skin with pores and fine lines, natural hair, no makeup sheen. Light: a large soft source from the front left at head height, a gentle fill on the right, a faint rim from behind separating the figure from the background. Full-frame camera, 85 mm lens at f/2.8, camera at chest height, the garment and the eyes sharp. True colour, restrained grade, fine grain; no airbrushed skin, no warped hands, no changing facial features. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
