## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw or restyle it. Create a wide marketplace detail module, {module}, as one photograph: {setting}. The product stands in the right 40% of the frame, three-quarter view, sharp, large enough to read on a phone at full module width; the left 60% continues the same surface and wall in soft, even, quiet light, uncluttered, with gentle tonal falloff, so the client's copy and feature points can be laid there in post. The module must sit calmly beside other modules on a white page: the frame's outer edges are clean and evenly lit, no vignetting. Lighting: a large soft key from camera left that crosses the open area and lands on the product, a narrow rim from behind right, a soft contact shadow. If a hand helps show use, it is an adult man's hand from our own cast, relaxed, real skin texture. Real materials with small imperfections, true reflections. Full-frame camera, 85 mm lens at f/5.6, camera at product height, perfectly level. Restrained grade, accurate colour, fine grain; no fake HDR, no glowing edges, no infographic shapes or icons. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
