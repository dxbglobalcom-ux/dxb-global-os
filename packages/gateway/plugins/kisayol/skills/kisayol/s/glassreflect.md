## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw or restyle it. Place it on {surface} in front of a {backdrop}, framed for {aspect}, so the product and its reflection make one tall, calm form. The reflection is physically true: a mirror image directly beneath, slightly darker and softer than the product, fading with distance from the contact line, showing the underside the camera would really see; on water, a faint ripple breaks it gently. Lighting: a long vertical strip softbox behind and to each side drawing clean edge highlights that repeat in the reflection, a small soft front fill for the product's face, the backdrop kept dark so the surface reads as deep. Real detail: a few specks of dust and a hairline scratch on the surface catching light. Medium-format camera, 100 mm lens at f/11, camera just above the surface plane, low and level. Restrained grade, true colour, fine grain; no impossible reflections, no glowing edges, no fake HDR. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
