## Prompt

Transition: between two shots, a whip pan. The camera starts on a clear, readable framing, then swings fast to one side; for about a third of a second the scene smears into horizontal motion blur, and the move lands with a short deceleration on the new framing, in the same direction of travel, perfectly readable again. The blur is real optical motion blur along the pan direction only, never a digital wipe or a ghosted double image. The product is sharp before the swing and sharp after it, its shape, colours and label unchanged; the light direction stays consistent on both sides.
