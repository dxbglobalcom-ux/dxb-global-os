## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed, the same in every frame; do not redraw or restyle it. A six-second video in three moments within one continuous shot, framed for {aspect}, on {background}. 0 to 2 seconds: the product is revealed as the camera glides in from a soft-focus edge to a sharp three-quarter view. 2 to 4 seconds: {feature}. 4 to 6 seconds: the camera settles and holds on a calm hero view, the product slightly off-centre, leaving one plain, evenly lit third of the frame empty for the client's mark laid in post. Lighting: a large softbox high camera-left as key, a white bounce camera-right, a thin strip light behind for a clean rim. Real materials with micro-texture and true reflections; a soft contact shadow grounds the product. Full-frame cinema camera, 85 mm lens at f/5.6, camera at product height, smooth operated motion with a gentle start and stop. Any hand is an adult man's hand from our own cast; no face in frame. Restrained grade, fine grain; no morphing, no flicker, no label drift. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
