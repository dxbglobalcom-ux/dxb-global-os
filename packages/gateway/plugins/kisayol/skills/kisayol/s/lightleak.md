## Prompt

Effect: a single light leak passes through the frame. From one edge a soft band of warm light, pale yellow at its core and deepening to orange and red at its edges, drifts across the image over about one second, lifting the blacks and lowering the contrast where it passes, then leaves by the opposite edge. It behaves like real light fogging film: soft-edged, uneven, slightly flickering in density, never a hard shape or a graphic overlay. When it covers a cut it peaks over the cut and clears on the new shot. The product stays readable under it and keeps its true colours and label once the leak has passed.
