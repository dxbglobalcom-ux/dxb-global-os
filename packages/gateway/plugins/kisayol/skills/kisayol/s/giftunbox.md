## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw or restyle it. A gift being opened on a wooden table: {wrap}, at the moment {moment}. The paper is really torn or folded back, with creases and one rough edge; the ribbon lies in a loose curl; a small blank card, face down. Two hands from our own cast, an adult man's or an elderly woman's, open it carefully, real skin texture, nails short and clean, faces out of frame. The product is the brightest, sharpest thing in the frame, label toward the lens. Light: warm daylight from a window on camera-left, the tissue paper glowing slightly where the light passes through it, soft shadows inside the box, a true highlight on the product's gloss. Full-frame camera, 50 mm lens at f/2.8, slightly above, looking into the box, framed for {aspect}. Real materials: paper fibre at the tear, satin sheen on silk, rigid board with a pressed edge. Restrained warm grade, fine grain; no confetti, no sparkles, no glow, no plastic. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere; any box or card stays blank for the client's mark laid in post.
