## Prompt

Start from the attached approved still as the first frame: the product resting in {box} on {surface} in a home in {market}, the room behind softly out of focus with the everyday things of a home there. The product stays exactly as in the still for the whole clip: shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; the packaging keeps its real form. A seven-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}. The hands of one adult man from our own cast enter from the bottom of frame: clean, short nails, natural skin with visible knuckle lines and fine hair, plain dark sleeve cuffs. He folds back the flaps and the tissue with two fingers, the tissue crinkling and settling, lifts the product out by its sides with care, turns it a quarter turn so its front faces the lens, and holds it steady at chest height for the last two seconds. Only hands and forearms are in frame; no face. Light: {light}, with a white card opposite for gentle fill; real soft shadows from the fingers fall on the box. Camera: full-frame cinema camera, 50 mm lens at f/2.8, slightly above the table looking down at 30 degrees, steady with the faintest handheld breathing unless a camera move is named. Real paper behaviour: cardboard creases, tape torn unevenly, paper fibres at the tissue edge, crisp card folds; the product's true surface, unchanged the moment it appears. Natural motion blur on the hands only, consistent anatomy, five fingers each, no warping, no flicker, no cuts. Restrained, natural grade, fine grain. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
