## Prompt

Lens: 85 mm at f/2, camera at subject height about two to three metres back: gently compressed perspective, the subject crisp at the focus plane and the background melting into smooth, round out-of-focus highlights that turn slightly oval toward the corners. Focus falls off gradually and believably. No cutout halo, no extreme bokeh, no lens flares, no digitally sharpened edges.
