## Prompt

Use the attached product photos as the only source for every product in the set: keep each one's exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw, restyle, merge or add products. Re-photograph the whole set together on {surface}, framed for {aspect}. Arrange them as a confident group: the hero product tallest and at the back centre, the others stepping down and out on both sides in a gentle triangle, each label facing camera, small even gaps so every silhouette reads, no product hiding another. One shared light: a large soft key from high camera left, a white fill card right, a thin rim from behind, soft contact shadows that agree in direction across the group. Real materials with small honest imperfections, true reflections of neighbours on glossy surfaces. Medium-format camera, 80 mm lens at f/11 so the whole group is sharp, camera slightly above, looking a few degrees down. The upper fifth of the frame is calm background for the client's offer copy and mark laid in post. Restrained grade, accurate colour, fine grain; no oversharpening, no glowing edges, no ribbons, badges or price stickers. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
