## Prompt

Lens: a modern smartphone's main rear camera from about one metre: the mild wide-angle perspective of a phone, deep focus with the background only slightly soft because of distance, a faint softness toward the corners. Auto exposure and auto white balance, the phone's own mild sharpening, fine sensor noise in the shadows; framing a little imperfect, as a real phone photo is. No cinematic bokeh, no portrait-mode cutout halo, no lens flares, no fisheye look.
