## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw or restyle it. Re-photograph it as a square 1:1 feed image on a built set of {background}: a painted wall meeting a matte floor with a soft curve, and one low cylindrical plinth the product stands on, centred slightly below the middle. Light like hard late sun: one small hard source high from camera right throws a crisp, long, clean-edged shadow across the floor that becomes the second shape of the composition; a white bounce from the left keeps the shadow side readable. Real materials: the plinth's painted edge with a tiny chip, faint roller texture on the wall, true specular highlights on gloss and soft falloff on matte. Medium-format camera, 80 mm lens at f/8, camera slightly above the product looking a few degrees down. One idea, nothing else in frame: no props, no clutter. The product reads at thumbnail size: clear silhouette, strong value contrast against the ground. Restrained grade, accurate colour, fine grain; no fake HDR, no glowing edges. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere; keep the top quarter of the frame plain wall for the client's copy and mark laid in post.
