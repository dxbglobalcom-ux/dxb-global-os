## Prompt

Camera: a slow dolly out. The camera travels straight backward on a level track away from the product, lens at the product's centre height, at an even pace with a gentle start and a gentle stop. It opens on a close shot with the product filling about two thirds of the frame and ends on a wide shot with the product filling about a fifth, centred, the setting around it now readable. Height, lens and direction stay locked; only the distance grows. Whatever enters the frame at the edges belongs to the same set, the same light and the same time of day. No zoom, no speed change. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.
