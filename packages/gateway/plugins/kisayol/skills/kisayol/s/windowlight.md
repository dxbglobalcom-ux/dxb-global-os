## Prompt

Light: a single window camera-left is the only source, its daylight diffused by a sheer curtain and falling across the product at about 45 degrees. The lit side carries a soft, broad highlight; the shadow side falls off gradually and is lifted only by light bounced from a pale wall opposite. Shadows lie to the right, soft-edged but directional; the window itself, if in frame, may blow out slightly. Do not add a ring light, softbox catchlights or any glow without a source.
