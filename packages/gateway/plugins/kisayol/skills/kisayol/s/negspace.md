## Prompt

Composition: negative space: the product small to medium in frame, about a quarter of its width, placed low or to one side, with a large, quiet field of even background around it. The empty area is deliberate: smooth tone, gentle light falloff, no props or busy texture in it, so the product reads at a glance and the client's mark can sit there in post.

## Notes

- Stack with thirds only if both agree on the side left empty.
