## Prompt

Lens: 24 mm on a full-frame camera at about 1.2 m, levelled so every vertical stays vertical, showing the whole room around the product. The product sits in the near third, large enough to lead, the room receding behind it; deep focus at f/8, a mild natural stretch only at the far edges of the frame, which nothing important touches. No fisheye bend, no tilted verticals, no warped furniture.

## Notes

- Needs a product-in-a-room base shot wide; the room bases here fix their own 35 mm or rectilinear lens.
