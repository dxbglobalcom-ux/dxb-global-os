## Prompt

Camera: a whip pan. The camera stands still in one place and opens on a calm framing of the setting beside the product, holds it briefly, then turns hard and fast horizontally to the right. The frame streaks with motion blur only during the swing, which lasts a fraction of a second, and lands on the product in a medium shot with a small natural settle. Both the opening and the landing frames are sharp and steady; the camera does not travel, it only turns. The rest of the clip holds still on the product. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

- h3 only, from a scene base: the opening framing is a part of the set the product still does not show.
