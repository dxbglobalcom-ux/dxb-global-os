## Prompt

Camera: handheld on a phone. Filmed on a modern phone by one of our own cast (an adult man or an elderly woman), who stays out of frame, holding it at chest height. The camera has small natural sway and micro-corrections, a slight reframing once as the operator settles, the product a little off-centre the way a person frames it. Autofocus finds the product once and stays; exposure stays even, without pumping. The phone's own look: mild sharpening, slight sensor noise in the shadows. No stabiliser glide, no random shake, no tracking a person could not make on foot. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.
