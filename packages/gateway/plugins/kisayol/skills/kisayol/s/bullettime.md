## Prompt

Camera: a bullet-time freeze. The moment in the scene is frozen at its peak: the splash, the scattered ingredients, the falling petals or the cloth in mid-air hang perfectly still. Through this frozen moment the camera arcs about 90 degrees around the product at a steady speed, at the product's centre height, opening on its front three-quarter view and ending on the other side's three-quarter view. Every suspended droplet and fragment stays crisp and in place, and the layers slide past each other with real parallax. Nothing in the scene moves; only the camera does. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- Needs a base with a moment to freeze (a splash, a drop, a scatter); on a still-life base it is only an arc.
- h3-from-still: start from the frozen-splash still itself.
