## Prompt

Light: hard midday sun almost overhead and slightly behind camera-right, a cloudless sky as the only fill. Short, crisp, dark shadows pool directly under objects with sharp edges; bright specular points on gloss and metal; the sky's blue is faintly reflected in the shadow side. Exposure held for the product's highlights so whites keep detail. No fill flash, no lifted shadows, no haze, no oversaturated greens and blues.
