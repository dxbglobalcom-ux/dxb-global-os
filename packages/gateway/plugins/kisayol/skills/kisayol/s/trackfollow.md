## Prompt

Camera: a tracking follow. The subject moves through the scene (one of our own cast, an adult man or an elderly woman, carrying the product, or the product itself on a moving surface), and the camera travels beside it, parallel to its path at shoulder height, matching its speed exactly. The subject and the product stay at the same place in the frame, a medium shot in three-quarter profile, faces not readable, while the background streams past with natural motion blur. The camera keeps an even distance and does not overtake or fall behind. No zoom. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- The base must give the subject a path (a corridor, a street, a workshop floor).
- A readable face needs a cast portrait base and a cast photo.
