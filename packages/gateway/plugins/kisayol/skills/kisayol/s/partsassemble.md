## Prompt

Start from the attached approved still as the first frame: the assembled product floating at the centre of a {backdrop}. A seven-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}, that ends on the identical product of the first frame: same shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed. In the first three seconds the product's real outer parts separate calmly along a single axis into a tidy exploded view, each part travelling a short, straight distance and stopping with a soft ease; parts the product does not visibly have are never invented, and the separation stays shallow so the outer shell is the main thing seen. One second of stillness shows the parts in line. In the last three seconds every part glides back along the same path and seats into place with a precise, satisfying stop, the seams closing cleanly. Light: a large softbox high camera-left as the key, a hard rim from behind-right that traces each edge, a faint bounce below. Camera: full-frame cinema camera, 70 mm lens at f/8, slightly below product height, a slow 15-degree orbit to the right across the whole shot. Real materials: machined edges, brushed metal grain, moulded plastic with fine mould lines. Restrained grade, fine grain. No sparks, no holograms, no glowing lines, no flicker, no cuts. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
