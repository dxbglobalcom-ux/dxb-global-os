## Prompt

Start from the attached approved still as the first frame. The product stays exactly as in it for the whole clip: its shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; it never moves, bends or redraws. A six-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}. The product stands on a {backdrop}, almost entirely in shadow at the start, only a faint rim along its silhouette. A narrow, hard-edged band of white light travels slowly across it {sweep}, like a strip softbox on a motorised rail just out of frame: as the band passes, true specular highlights run along curved edges, matte areas brighten with soft falloff, and the material's micro-texture shows for a moment before falling back into shade. By the last second a low, wide key from camera-left settles and holds the product fully lit and readable. Camera: full-frame cinema camera, 90 mm lens at f/5.6, at product height, locked off with a barely perceptible push-in of a few centimetres. Physically plausible light: the band lights the surface in front of the product too, and the contact shadow moves with it. Constant exposure, restrained grade, deep but not crushed blacks, fine grain. No lens flares, no glowing edges, no sparkles, no flicker, no cuts. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere; the last second leaves calm dark space above the product for the client's mark laid in post.
