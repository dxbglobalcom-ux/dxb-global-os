## Prompt

Use the attached still as the exact first frame and the only source for the product: its shape, proportions, colours, materials, position and its own label stay exactly as photographed for the whole clip; the product never moves, morphs, flickers or redraws. A six-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}, that loops cleanly. Only the steam moves: thin, translucent wisps rise from the hot surface already in the photo, curl slowly, thin out and vanish a hand's height above it, new wisps replacing them in an irregular rhythm. The steam is lit by the still's own light: brightest where it crosses the main light, nearly invisible against bright areas, clearest against darker background. Real steam behaviour: soft edges, uneven density, a slight drift with the room's air, never thick smoke, never a perfect spiral. Everything else in the frame is still; the camera is locked off. Real materials as in the still, fine grain over the whole frame, restrained grade matching the still. No glow, no flicker, no cuts, no added objects. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
