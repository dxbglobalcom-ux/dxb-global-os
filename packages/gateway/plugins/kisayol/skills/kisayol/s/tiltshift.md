## Prompt

Lens: a tilt-shift lens tilted to lay a thin, slanted plane of focus across the scene, shot from a high vantage looking down at about 45 degrees: the product and a narrow band around it are sharp, everything nearer and farther blurs smoothly, so the scene reads like a miniature scale set. Clean daylight, slightly lifted colour. The blur grows gradually with distance from the focus band, as real optics do; no flat digital blur, no blur halos around edges.
