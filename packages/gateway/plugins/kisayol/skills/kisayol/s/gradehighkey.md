## Prompt

Grade: high-key: bright, airy exposure with the background rising to near-white, very soft and faint shadows, an overall light tonal range. The product is exposed for its own detail, so white and pale surfaces keep their texture and edges and never dissolve into the background; a faint contact shadow still grounds it. Neutral whites; no blown-out highlights on the product, no grey haze, no glow.
