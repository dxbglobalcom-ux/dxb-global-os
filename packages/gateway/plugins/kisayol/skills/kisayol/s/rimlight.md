## Prompt

Light: strengthen the back light. The source behind the product and slightly off-axis (the strip light in a studio, the window in a room) draws a thin, even bright edge along its silhouette and separates it cleanly from the background. The front stays readable from the existing key, the rim about one stop brighter than the front's highlight; on glass the rim travels through the edge as a clean line. No haloing, no outline drawn around the shape, no edge brighter than its source could make it.

## Notes

- In a room it places the window behind the product; do not stack it with windowlight, which puts the window camera-left.
