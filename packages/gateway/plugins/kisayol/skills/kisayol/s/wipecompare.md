## Prompt

Start from the attached approved still as the first frame: a split view of {scene}, one side still in its before state and the other in its after state, with the product standing on the after side. The product stays exactly as in the still for the whole clip: shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; it does not move. A five-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}. A clean vertical boundary travels slowly {direction} across the frame, carried by a physical element: the edge of a sheet of frosted glass sliding just in front of the lens, softly blurred. Everything behind the passing edge becomes the after state; the surface details stay in exactly the same place, so only their condition changes. The after state is true to what the product really does: honest, modest, no gleam beyond what a clean surface shows. Light: soft daylight from a window camera-left, unchanged across both states so the comparison is fair. Camera: full-frame cinema camera, 35 mm lens at f/5.6, slightly above the surface looking down at 40 degrees, locked off. Real materials with imperfection: residue, fine scratches, true reflections. Restrained grade, fine grain. No sparkle, no shine stars, no glow, no flicker, no cuts. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
