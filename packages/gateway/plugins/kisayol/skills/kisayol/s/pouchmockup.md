## Prompt

Use the attached image, a photo of the client's pouch or its flat artwork, as the only source of the design: keep its exact layout, colours, graphics and its own label as supplied; do not redraw, translate or restyle it. Show it printed on a filled stand-up pouch in {finish}, standing on {setting}, framed for {aspect}, at a slight three-quarter turn. The pouch is real: a gusseted base that spreads under the weight of the contents, a heat-sealed top with fine crimp texture and a tear notch, a press-to-close zip ridge, soft wrinkles where the film bends, and the design following every bulge without stretching wrongly. Lighting: a large softbox high camera-left giving a broad, soft highlight that rolls across the curved front, a white card camera-right, a soft contact shadow. Medium-format camera, 80 mm lens at f/8, camera at mid-pouch height. Restrained grade, accurate colour, fine grain. No people. Add no text, letters or logos beyond what the supplied design already carries.
