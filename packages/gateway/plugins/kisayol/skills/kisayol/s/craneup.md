## Prompt

Camera: a slow crane up. The camera starts at the product's own height on a medium shot, then rises smoothly straight up, tilting down as it climbs so the product stays in frame. The pace is slow and weighty, with a soft start and a soft settle at the top. It ends high, looking down at about 50 degrees, the product smaller near the centre and the surrounding set laid out around it. The camera does not travel sideways; only height and tilt change. No zoom. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.
