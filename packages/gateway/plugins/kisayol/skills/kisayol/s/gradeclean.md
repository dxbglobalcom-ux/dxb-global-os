## Prompt

Grade: clean commercial colour: neutral whites, the product's colours matched to the attached photo, moderate saturation, a gentle S-curve with highlights held and shadows open but not grey. Wood, paper, stone and skin keep their true hues; no colour cast in the whites. Fine, barely visible grain. No fake HDR, no oversaturation, no crushed blacks, no teal-orange split.
