## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw or restyle it. Compose a dynamic hero image framed for {aspect}: the product tilted about 25 degrees, its long axis {direction}, in front of {backdrop}, as if caught mid-movement by a flash. A strong diagonal line of light crosses the background along the same axis and the product's shadow echoes it, so the whole frame leans one way. The product stays sharp; only a faint streak of fine particles or dust trails behind it to suggest speed. The opposite corner of the frame stays calm and even for the message laid in post. Lighting: a hard strip light along the diagonal as the key, carving a bright edge down the product, a soft fill from camera-left keeping its label and colours readable, a thin rim light behind. Full-frame camera, 35 mm lens at f/8, camera slightly below and rolled 5 degrees. Punchy, honest grade, product colour untouched, fine grain; no speed-line graphics, no glow. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
