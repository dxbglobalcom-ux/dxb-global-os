## Prompt

Camera: a crash zoom in. The camera stays fixed in one place; only the focal length changes. It opens on a wide shot with the product small at the centre, holds for a moment, then zooms in very fast to a tight framing of the product, landing crisp with no overshoot. Perspective stays that of a zoom, not a dolly: the background magnifies with the product and does not shift around it. A brief motion blur during the zoom only. The remaining part of the clip holds still on the tight framing. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- h3-from-still: the still is the wide opening frame, so the product must be sharp enough in it to survive the magnification.
