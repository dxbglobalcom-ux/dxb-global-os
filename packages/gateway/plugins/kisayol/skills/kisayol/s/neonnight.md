Light: night, lit only by what is there: shopfront neon tubes in two or three colours (magenta, cyan, warm white), sodium-orange street lamps and the screen itself. Mixed colour temperatures pool on the wet asphalt as long, rippled reflections; light falls off quickly into deep but detailed shadows. The neon tubes are abstract shapes with nothing legible in them. Controlled highlights; no excessive bloom, no glowing outlines, no noise reduction smearing the surfaces.
