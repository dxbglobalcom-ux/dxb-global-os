## Prompt

Camera: a rack focus on a locked-off camera. A long lens, around 85 mm at a wide aperture, from the product's centre height. The clip opens with focus on a foreground element close to the lens (an ingredient, a leaf, the near edge of the surface) and the product soft behind it. Near the middle of the clip the focus shifts smoothly over about a second to the product, which ends crisp while the foreground falls soft. The camera does not move, the framing does not change; only the plane of focus travels, with the slight natural focus breathing of a real lens. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.
