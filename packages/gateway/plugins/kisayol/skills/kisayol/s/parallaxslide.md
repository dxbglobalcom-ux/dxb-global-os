## Prompt

Camera: a parallax slide. The camera trucks sideways from left to right on a level track, parallel to the product, at a steady pace with a soft start and stop. Out-of-focus objects of the same set pass close in front of the lens and briefly cross the frame, while the background moves slowly, so the layers separate clearly by depth. The product is partly hidden at the opening and fully revealed by the end, centred in a medium shot. Distance and height stay locked; the camera does not turn. No zoom. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- Needs foreground layers: a base with shelves, plants, glassware or props near the camera.
- Longer and more layered than sliderglide; take one or the other.
