## Prompt

Start from the attached approved still as the first frame: the product standing on a matte surface in front of a {backdrop}. A six-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}, that ends exactly where it began. In the first two seconds the product gently loosens from its top downward into {material}, which lifts and circles slowly around the spot where it stood, catching the light; for one second only the material is in frame, hanging in a soft spiral; in the last three seconds it gathers back, settles and re-forms the product from its base upward, finishing as the identical product of the first frame: same shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed, perfectly still for the final second. Material behaviour is physically believable: gravity-true arcs, real particle size, translucent where thin, no glitter. Light: one large softbox high camera-left as the key, a rim from behind-right that makes the material glow at its edges. Camera: full-frame cinema camera, 85 mm lens at f/8, at product height, locked off. Restrained grade, fine grain. No glowing edges, no neon, no CGI gloss, no flicker, no cuts. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
