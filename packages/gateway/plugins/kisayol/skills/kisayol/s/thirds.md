## Prompt

Composition: rule of thirds: the product's centre of interest on one third-line, slightly off-centre, its lean or front facing into the frame. The opposite two thirds stay calm and uncluttered: an even, low-detail area with no objects, no strong edges and no lettering, kept clean for the client's mark and copy laid in post. Horizon and surface lines level.

## Notes

- The empty area is for copy and the mark added in post from the real files (rule f).
