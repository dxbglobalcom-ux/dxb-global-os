## Prompt

Effect: at the key instant the action freezes into a perfectly still frame for about one second, and within that frozen frame the view pushes in quickly but smoothly toward the product, ending on a tight close-up of its most telling detail with its label sharp. The frozen frame stays physically consistent while zooming: no new detail appears, no part of the scene moves, grain stays fine and steady. Then the frame releases, the view eases back out to the original framing and the action continues at real speed from exactly where it stopped. The product's shape, colours and label never change.
