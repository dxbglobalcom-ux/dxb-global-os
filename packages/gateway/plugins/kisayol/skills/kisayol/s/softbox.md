## Prompt

Light: one large softbox, about 120 cm across, high camera-left at 45 degrees as the key; a white bounce card camera-right lifts the shadow side to about one stop under the key; a narrow strip box behind gives a clean edge. Highlights on gloss take the softbox's rectangular shape with softened corners; shadows are soft-edged but clearly directional, falling down and to the right. Even, controlled exposure across the product; no hot spots, no light without a source.

## Notes

- Fits studio bases whose key already sits high camera-left; it sharpens their description, it does not move the light.
- Bases lit by a window or the sun do not take it.
