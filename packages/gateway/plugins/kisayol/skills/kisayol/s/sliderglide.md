## Prompt

Camera: a slider glide. The camera slides a short distance, about the width of the product, sideways on a slider at the product's centre height, while turning very slightly to keep the product at the same place in the frame. The pace is slow and perfectly even, with soft ease in and out, the kind of move a premium product film breathes with. The background shifts gently behind the product with real parallax; the framing stays a medium close shot from start to end. No zoom, no height change. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- The gentlest move here: right for luxury, jewellery and still-life bases; it suits a seamless loop.
