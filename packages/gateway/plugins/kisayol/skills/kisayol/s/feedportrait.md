## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw or restyle it. Re-photograph it as a vertical 4:5 feed image: the product stands on {surface} in the lower third of the frame, against a {background} wall that falls into soft shadow. A single shaft of window light enters from high camera left, cuts diagonally across the wall and lands on the product, giving it a bright key face, a crisp edge and a long shadow toward camera right; a little bounce from the surface lifts the shadow side. Fine dust motes drift faintly in the shaft. Real materials: the surface's pores, small chips and natural variation, the wall's plaster texture, true reflections on the product. Full-frame camera, 90 mm lens at f/5.6, camera at product height, horizon level. The upper half of the frame is calm wall in soft graded light, holding nothing: it carries the client's copy and mark laid in post. Composition built for a phone: the product readable at small size, the brightest point on the product. Restrained grade, accurate colour, fine film grain; no fake HDR, no glow halos, no plastic surfaces. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
