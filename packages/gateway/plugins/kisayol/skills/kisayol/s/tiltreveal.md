## Prompt

Camera: a slow tilt up. The camera stays fixed in place at a little below the product's centre height and only rotates upward. It opens on the surface just below the product's base and tilts up at a slow, constant speed, travelling the product from its base to its top, and ends with the whole product framed with a little headroom above it. A soft start and a soft stop; the camera does not rise or move. No zoom. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- Strongest on tall products; on a flat product the tilt reveals almost nothing.
- h3-from-still: the still is the opening frame, so frame it low, the product's base near the top edge; a centred packshot still leaves the tilt nowhere to go.
