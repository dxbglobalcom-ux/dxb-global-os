## Prompt

Use the attached still as the exact first frame and the only source for the product: its cut, colours, pattern, weave, stitching and its own label stay exactly as photographed for the whole clip; the garment or textile only moves as real cloth moves and never changes. A six-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}, that loops cleanly. A soft breeze from camera-left lifts the free edges of the fabric: hems, corners and loose folds ripple and settle, the movement travelling through the cloth from the edge inward, with the weight of its real material: linen stiff and crisp, silk light and fluid, wool slow and heavy. Folds deepen and open, light shifts across them, the pattern follows every fold without sliding or redrawing. Fixed parts such as seams, hangers and pinned areas stay still. Everything else in the frame is still; the camera is locked off. Real materials, fine grain over the whole frame, restrained grade matching the still. No warping, no melting folds, no glow, no flicker, no cuts, no added objects. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
