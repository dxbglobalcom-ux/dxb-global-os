## Prompt

Lens: 100 mm macro at f/8 from a working distance that keeps the whole product in frame: flat, distortion-free perspective, straight edges stay straight, the product's front face parallel to the sensor and in critical focus. Fine surface detail reads clearly: the print grain of the packaging, brushed or moulded texture, tiny seams. The background falls off gently. Real lens behaviour; no focus-stacking artefacts, no exaggerated bokeh, no oversharpened edges.

## Notes

- Fits studio bases already shot on a 100 mm lens with the whole product in frame; extreme-macro bases fix their own magnification.
