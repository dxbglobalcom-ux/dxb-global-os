## Prompt

Start from the attached approved still as the first frame: the product nested in its own open packaging on a {surface}, soft tissue folded loosely over its upper half, its front face just visible. The product stays exactly as in the still for the whole clip: shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; the packaging keeps its real form and finish. A six-second single continuous shot, framed for {aspect}. With no one touching it, the tissue lifts slowly at its folded edges as if on a gentle current of air and slides back over the box walls, one leaf after the other; then the product's inner tray rises a few millimetres and settles, presenting the product fully. The last two seconds are still. Light: a large softbox high camera-left as the key, a narrow rim from behind-right catching the tissue's edge so it glows faintly, a low bounce in front. Camera: full-frame cinema camera, 65 mm lens at f/5.6, above the box looking down at 45 degrees, a slow push-in of a few centimetres across the shot unless a camera move is named. Real materials: paper fibres and soft creases in the tissue, crisp board edges, a fine emboss on the box catching the light. Physically plausible paper behaviour, no floating debris. Restrained grade, fine grain. No sparkle, no magic dust, no glow, no flicker, no cuts. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
