## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the piece: keep its exact design, metal colour and finish, stone colour, cut, size, number of stones and setting as photographed; do not add stones, change the cut or restyle it. Make an extreme macro of the main stone and its setting against a {background}, framed for {aspect}. Show what a jeweller would want seen: the facets' sharp junctions, real light return with small flashes of spectral colour only where a true facet would throw them, the claws or bezel gripping the stone with tiny tool marks, the polish of the metal and the faint grain of its finish. Light: one small hard source high to the right for sparkle, a large soft source on the left shaping the metal with a long, smooth highlight, black flags to keep strong dark edges in the facets. Full-frame camera, 100 mm macro lens with focus stacking at f/11, camera at 30 degrees above the piece, sharp from the table of the stone to the shank. No people. Accurate colour, deep clean blacks, restrained grade, fine grain; no glow halos, no starburst filters, no impossible reflections, no plastic metal. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.

## Notes

- Inclusions and colour must match the real stone; a sharper or cleaner stone than the client's is a false claim.
- h3-from-still: four seconds, the piece turns a few degrees on a hidden turntable, the facets flashing; 8 steps.
