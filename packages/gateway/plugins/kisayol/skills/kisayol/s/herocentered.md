## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the product: keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and its own label as photographed; do not redraw or restyle it. Compose a strictly symmetrical hero image, framed for {aspect}: the product dead centre, front-on, standing on a low surface with {frame}, in a {palette} palette. Everything mirrors across the vertical centre line, so the eye goes straight to the product; the area above the frame stays plain and even for the message laid in post. Real built materials: plaster with trowel marks, stone with pores and chipped arrises, a little dust. Lighting: a broad overhead soft source centred above the product, two matching strip lights left and right for symmetrical rim lines, a soft contact shadow directly beneath. Medium-format camera, 80 mm lens at f/11, lens exactly on the centre line at the product's mid-height, verticals and horizon perfectly straight. Restrained grade, accurate colour, fine grain; no tilt, no glow, no CGI smoothness. No people. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
