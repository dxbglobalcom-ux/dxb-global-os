## Prompt

Camera: a top-down descend. The lens points straight down at the product from high above. The camera lowers vertically along that axis at a slow, even pace with a gentle settle, from a wide overhead view that shows the whole arrangement around the product to a tight overhead view with the product filling about two thirds of the frame, centred. The lens stays exactly vertical and the frame does not rotate. Shadows keep their direction from a light fixed in the set. No zoom. One continuous shot from the first frame to the last, no cuts. The product stays exactly as photographed: shape, proportions, colours and its own label unchanged in every frame, no morphing. Exposure and white balance stay constant; background geometry stays stable, with natural motion blur only where the move makes it.

## Notes

- Pairs with an overhead base (flat lay, table); on an eye-level base the opening view is invented.
