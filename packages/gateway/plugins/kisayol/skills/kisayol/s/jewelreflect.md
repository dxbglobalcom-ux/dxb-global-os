## Prompt

Use the attached product photo as the only source for the piece: keep its exact design, metal colour and finish, stones, size and proportions as photographed; do not add or restyle anything. Set it on {surface} in a dark studio, framed for {aspect} with wide negative space. The surface gives a crisp, true reflection directly beneath it that fades downward and slightly darker than the piece itself, with a real thin seam of contact where the two meet. Light: two tall narrow strip softboxes, one at each side slightly behind, drawing long clean highlights along the metal's curves and their mirrored twins in the reflection; a small hard spot from above for the stones' sparkle. The rest of the frame falls to deep black with a faint gradient near the horizon line. Full-frame camera, 90 mm lens at f/11, camera low, only a few degrees above the surface, perfectly level. Real details: a hairline scratch in the acrylic or a micro ripple in the water, one speck of dust. No people. Restrained, cool-neutral grade, fine grain; no glow halos, no impossible reflections, no starbursts. Add no text, letters or logos anywhere.
