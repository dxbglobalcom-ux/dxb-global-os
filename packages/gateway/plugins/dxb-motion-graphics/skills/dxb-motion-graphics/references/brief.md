# The brief — what must be known before a board is written

A film is as good as what it was told. The same model that returns a generic spot from "make a motion graphic
for our app" returns a strong one from a brief that says what the film is for and gives it real things to move.

## The form

| | |
|---|---|
| **The one action** | What should the viewer do when it ends — download, visit, book, remember a name? One. |
| **Who and where** | Who watches, on what (phone feed, site hero, store listing, a screen in a shop, a billboard), with or without sound. |
| **Length and frames** | Seconds; 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 — each named, because each is composed. |
| **Delivery** | Resolution, frame rate, loudness target, transparent master or not, the file the next step needs. From where it runs and from any footage it joins. |
| **The promise** | The one thing the product does for the viewer, in the brand's own words. Three supporting facts at most, each true and checkable. |
| **The material** | Logo files, real screens, product photographs or model, illustrations, the brand's colours and typeface, footage it must join. What is missing and who supplies it. |
| **References** | Two or three films or frames, each with what is wanted from it — the pacing, a transition, the light. Never "like this" without saying what. |
| **Tone** | Three adjectives the brand would sign, and one it would refuse. |
| **Sound** | Effects only, or voice (whose, what language), and the job's ruling on music. |
| **Kind of work** | A client's commissioned film, the holding's own, or an independent spec piece. |
| **Limits** | What may not be shown or claimed; the house boundaries of the one who orders. |

## What a strong direction note looks like

Weak: *"Make it more dynamic and premium."*

Strong: *"Open on the logo alone for half a second, then let its three bars swing closed into a ring — the ring
is the dial of the Now Playing screen, so the next shot is already inside it. Keep the camera drifting right the
whole time so nothing ever sits still; when the playlist wall arrives, pull back through the card rather than
cutting. End on the app icon exactly where the logo began."*

The strong note names shapes, says what each becomes, gives the camera something to do, and ties the end to the
beginning. It can be storyboarded line by line. Ask for notes of this kind, and write the board in this language.

## Questions worth asking the one who ordered

- If the viewer remembers one frame, which one?
- What does the product look like at the moment it is most itself?
- What would make this film unmistakably this brand's and nobody else's?
- Where will it be seen first, and will the sound be on?
