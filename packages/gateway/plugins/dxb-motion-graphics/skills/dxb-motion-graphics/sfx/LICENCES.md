- **Pixabay sound effects** — https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/ — Content License Summary (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): allowed "Use Content for free", "Use Content without having to attribute the author"; prohibited: "You cannot sell or distribute Content (either in digital or physical form) on a Standalone basis. Standalone means where no creative effort has been applied to the Content and it remains in substantially the same form as it exists on our website." A file may go into a film; a folder of their files in this plugin would be standalone distribution. The summary says only the full Content License is binding; the full text was not read (the page refused the request).
- **Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles** — https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc/ — licence (https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/): "Licensee may use and modify the licensed sound effects for personal and commercial projects without attribution to the original creator." and "Licensee may not distribute, publish, sub-license or otherwise supply the sound effects as sound effects to any other person, without the Licensor's prior written permission. This applies whether the sound effects are unmodified, modified or re-designed, and whether they are supplied on their own or as part of a sound effects library, sample pack, asset pack, project template, software development kit or anything similar." The same licence carries a clause headed "NO AI TRAINING OR USAGE": "the Licensee is expressly prohibited from using any sound effects licensed under this Agreement for the purpose of training artificial intelligence technologies." Royalty-free in a film; not redistributable, so not bundled.
- **freesound.org, CC0 sounds** — the licence would pass, the download does not: the site's help page (https://freesound.org/help/faq/) says "To download a sound, first make sure you are logged into your registered account." No account, so nothing was taken. Each sound there carries its own licence; only the CC0 ones are free of attribution.
