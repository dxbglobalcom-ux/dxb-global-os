## The law of this board

1. **A row is opened only on the CEO's word.** The board holds the holding's special parts — the
   Ferrari's parts still to be added — not every job. CEO, 2026-09-28: *"tahta holding için ferrarinin
   eklenecek parçaları. özeller"* · *"bir daha ben söylemeden oraya yapılacak şeyler yazılmasın"*.
   <!-- CEO-OK: opening-three-headings-and-board-archive-2026-09-28 -->
2. **No row closes without evidence.** A row closes with a command and its decisive output, or
   with the CEO's own eye/ear where the machine cannot check it. A closed row keeps its evidence.
3. **New design goes into the spec that already owns the contract** as a registered adaptation —
   never into a new spec.
4. **Historical order.** Rows are ordered by the date the item was opened, oldest first, because
   the CEO's ruling of 2026-07-27 is that half-finished older work outranks new work.
5. **Ledger parity.** When a row closes here, the ledger that owns it is corrected in the SAME
   session. A stale ✓ or a stale ◐ is a governance violation of the same tier as an invented number.
6. **ONLY REAL WORK ENTERS. A DROPPED IDEA LEAVES NO TRACE — HERE OR ANYWHERE.**
   A row belongs here when the work is **real, ordered or approved, and unfinished**. An idea that
   was discussed and dropped, a plan the CEO waved away, a possibility nobody committed to — none
   of it is written here, and none of it is written anywhere else either. **Silence is the record.**
   CEO, 2026-07-31, after finding a cancelled conversation written onto this board: *"orası sadece
   gerçek onaylanmış ama yarıda kalmış tamamlanmamış işler için… ben uyarmasam yine bir yığın boş
   yazı olacaktı, ben habire boş sohbetler için uyarmak zorunda mıyım?"* **He is not the filter.**
   The reflex being banned is "write it down just in case" — the same reflex that grew the
   always-on layer to 27 pages. When in doubt, write nothing: a dropped idea costs one sentence to
   raise again, and costs the register its meaning to keep.
   **Accepted by the CEO, 2026-08-01** — *"1. yi onaylıyorum. dünde onaylamıştım. kaydedilsin demiştim"*.  <!-- CEO-OK: governance-repairs-accepted-2026-08-01 -->

7. **A PLAN IS ACCEPTED ONLY IF IT OPENS WITH TWO SENTENCES.** Proposed to the CEO on 2026-08-20 and accepted by him in his own words — *"kuralı kabul ediorm"* <!-- CEO-OK: two-sentence-plan-rule-2026-08-20 -->
   1. **What will be on the CEO's screen when this row closes** — one sentence, no technical word in it.
   2. **Which single command proves it.**

   A plan that cannot answer both is not ready to be planned; it is ready to be discussed. **Why this exists:** he had a large list planned by an earlier model — decomposed, planned, coded — and the result disappointed him. The defect was not too much planning. **The plan described the WORK and never described the RESULT he would look at**, so the work was correct and the target was wrong.

   **And the plan lives in a FILE, not in the conversation.** Plan mode's output dies with the session; the approved plan is written to the row's own `PLAN.md` before that session ends. **The execution session reads the PLAN. The SUMMARY is written afterwards and is EVIDENCE, never instruction** — that is what keeps the detail from being lost behind a short summary, which he asked about the same day.

8. **AN OPEN ROW STAYS THIN** <!-- CEO-OK: board-thin-row-ceiling-2026-09-28 -->. A row keeps six parts: what it is · why it is open · who it waits on · what closes it · his words with their markers · its plan link. Everything else — a new leg's detail, measurements, phases (done / accepted / left) — goes word for word to `.planning/board-rows/<ID>.md`, and the row points there in one sentence. The records ruler (R5) stops the commit when an open row passes 1,600 bytes.

### Law 5 is enforced by machine (U41, 2026-07-28)

Until tonight these two laws were honour rules, and the honour failed in three measurable ways on
one evening: two commitments written in a document were built nowhere and tracked on no ledger;
five records asserted a state the database contradicted (one said the holding had ONE employee
while it had 199); and a roadmap row sat ◐ for ten days after its own audit trail proved the work
done. The CEO named the pattern himself — *"her gelen model bir şeyleri atlıyor mutlaka"*.

`scripts/governance/ledger-truth.mjs` runs in the battery and enforces both laws over the whole
corpus. Every durable statement is now one of two kinds, and the distinction is the fix:

| Kind | Example | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **STATE** — what is true NOW | "199 active employees" | carries `<!-- STATE: id = value @ date -->`, **re-measured against the live company database on every run** |
| **EVENT** — what happened ONCE | "restore drill 2026-07-17, RTO 5s" | frozen forever, and **must carry its date** |

An `<!-- OPEN: <row> -->` marker, where one is written, must name a row that exists and is open.
