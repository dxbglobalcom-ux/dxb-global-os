## 3. Mandatory video acquisition and full-video analysis standard

### 3.1 Source quality

For every existing and newly supplied source video:

1. Download the highest genuine source quality available.
2. Prefer **1080p**. The minimum acceptable resolution is **720p when the source provides it**.
3. Do not present upscaling as genuine HD.
4. Record URL, file name, resolution, codec, frame rate, duration, audio availability and download method in a source manifest.
5. If the source itself is below 720p, record that limitation explicitly and do not overstate visual conclusions.

### 3.2 “Watch the video”, not only screenshots

A sparse-frame review is not accepted. Full temporal behaviour must be analysed:

- movement of Jarvis, Nimbus and other assistants;
- how the assistant repositions itself when screens change;
- speech-responsive vibration or waveform behaviour;
- animated relationships between nodes, agents and departments;
- moving light/data points through connections;
- page transitions and continuity;
- live numbers, graphs, statuses and state changes;
- audio quality, rhythm and human-likeness;
- whether the UI feels like a living operating system rather than static cards.

Use the full video, audio, transcript and dense sequential evidence together. If direct playback is not available to the model, use appropriate local tooling and dense time-sequenced extraction. State exactly how the temporal analysis was performed. **Do not claim to have watched a video if only a few frames were inspected.**

### 3.3 Existing transcripts and frames

- Existing transcripts may be reused if they are accurate.
- Validate transcript timing against the new video file.
- Correct transcription errors that affect meaning.
- Regenerate important frames from the new high-quality downloads.
- Do not waste time recreating valid work without a reason.

## 5. Required honesty about the current DXB Global OS

The current system must be evaluated critically. Do not conceal defects with architecture language, project size or claims that the foundation is powerful.

The CEO’s current assessment is that the visible product is substantially below the quality demonstrated by the reference systems. The only existing visual element that is pre-approved is the **left navigation bar and its icons**. Everything else remains open to redesign.

The audit must specifically investigate and resolve:

- repeated or meaningless `routing_change` and internal machine events displayed in executive/business views;
- blank detail pages after clicking visible items;
- unclear labels such as orchestrator/resident worker without human-readable identity and purpose;
- empty `reason`, `confidence` or detail fields;
- tiny, misaligned or unreadable table columns;
- static, disconnected and visually dead pages;
- charts, counts or cards that do not support a real business decision;
- duplicated, stale, dummy or unexplained data;
- inconsistent identity between the Hamza in chat, voice and system-control surfaces;
- inconsistent or missing morning briefings;
- construction/project-build data mixed into the operating interface of the actual holding company;
- engineering/development telemetry shown where real company operations, revenue, portfolio, marketing, sales and decisions should appear.

### Absolute data-domain separation

The holding-company operating dataset and the system-construction/engineering dataset are separate domains. The business OS must contain **no construction/build-process file, label, card, metric or navigation item** unless it is intentionally exposed inside a clearly separated machine/admin environment.

The business-facing hierarchy must be designed around:

1. Command
2. Finance
3. Operations
4. Organization
5. Intelligence
6. Governance
7. System

The exact navigation may be improved, but the distinction between **running the company** and **building the software** is non-negotiable.

## 6. Product target: a living, serious, revenue-centred Holding OS

The target is not a decorative dashboard. It is a living AI-native holding operating system whose primary business objective is to produce revenue and profit through real company operations.

### Core principles

- Every page must read live, traceable data or show an honest empty state.
- No panel may display a number that cannot be traced to a query, source or calculation.
- Every click must lead to meaningful detail, context and action.
- Each page must be connected to the rest of the system through clear business relationships.
- Animation must communicate state, flow, priority and life—not distract or turn the product into a game.
- 2D, 2.5D or 3D may be used where it improves hierarchy, presence and understanding.
- Graphs and charts are required only when they explain real business information.
- The interface must feel premium, precise, intelligent and active.
- Do not copy “Burj Al Arab” literally across every screen. Luxury is a quality standard, not a decorative theme.

### Hamza

Hamza is the persistent general manager and central operating intelligence of the holding:

- one coherent identity across chat, voice, dashboards, meetings and system control;
- knows the Kernel Holding system, company structure, directors, departments, policies, data and tools;
- can navigate and operate the system, not merely answer questions;
- changes position and presentation intelligently according to context;
- visibly responds while speaking;
- may use a premium holographic/volumetric face without literal human eyes, nose or mouth;
- uses an animated mouth-line/waveform when speaking;
- communicates fluently in Turkish, English and Arabic;
- never gives a lazy “cannot be done” response: when blocked, it must state the real constraint and propose the safest workable path.

Voice direction: achieve **JARVIS-class quality**—calm, precise, premium and human-like—using an original or properly licensed voice. Do not clone or imitate a real performer’s protected voice without rights.

### Directors and meeting room

- Approximately 20 department directors must have distinct identities and domain expertise.
- Each director needs a coherent visual symbol, role, status and knowledge boundary.
- A meeting-room view must place Hamza centrally with the relevant directors around it.
- Conversation, decisions, tasks, dissent, confidence and outcomes must be visible and traceable.
- Director visuals may follow the same holographic language as Hamza, but must remain individually recognisable.

### Chat, voice and action execution

The current Chat with Hamza / voice-line surface must be redesigned. It must support context, history, actions, status, approvals and evidence—not a shallow text box.

For sensitive actions such as account creation or temporary credentials:

- use encrypted secret storage, never plaintext;
- apply least privilege, expiry/TTL, approval gates and audit logs;
- delete or rotate temporary secrets according to policy;
- make every privileged action observable and reversible where possible.

## 9. Acceptance criteria and failure conditions

The work is rejected if any of the following occurs:

- analysis is based mainly on low-resolution downloads or a few screenshots;
- the agent says it watched a video when it inspected only sparse frames;
- unsupported criticism remains in the revised files;
- CEO-confirmed evidence is ignored or incorrectly presented as independent verification;
- all previous work is discarded without auditing what can be reused;
- old files are overwritten without backup or change history;
- DXB is praised defensively instead of audited honestly;
- construction/engineering data remains mixed with the business OS;
- visual design is described only in prose without actual mockups;
- implementation starts before written CEO approval;
- numbers, charts or statuses are shown without traceable data provenance;
- Hamza remains fragmented across chat, voice and control surfaces;
- “living UI” becomes meaningless decoration rather than functional motion.

The work is accepted only when the evidence chain is clear, the old analyses are corrected, the gaps are admitted honestly, the product architecture is coherent and every major screen has been visually approved.
