### Hükmü ne değiştirir
- <what would turn the verdict, and whether the rows looked at it> [L0081]

Build it this way — every Turkish word above stands exactly as written; each <…> is yours to fill:
- Three lists under exactly these three `### ` headings, in this order, and nothing else in the section.
- Every item cites the ids it stands on. A list no row speaks to holds the single item `—`: zero is an answer.
- The verdict is the rows', not yours: nothing is estimated, and a number is one a row states.
