## Why this is a decision and not an install

Three things sit on it, and none of them is an author's to settle:

1. **Money out** — the approval gate by name, even at the $19.97/mo floor.
2. **An outside account connected to a live store** — W-C42-4's own CEO cell: *which accounts exist and may be connected*.
3. **A business model, not a tool.** Dropshipping supplier-arbitrage is a commercial direction for the holding, not a purchase.

**The cheaper path is already ours and is written as P38-2:** a **read-only WooCommerce connector** for Outleteuro under the existing gate — orders, products and revenue read on a schedule into `v_objective_progress` — which needs **no vendor, no subscription and no new account**, because the store's REST API is part of the store we already run. That closes the measured gap (revenue reaches the CEO's screen because a person types it into `revenue-entry-form.tsx`). Sell The Trend answers a different question — *what should we sell* — and that one is his.
