# ChatGPT's side (chatgpt.com, 2026-11-25 ~09:35, web search) — claims to check at the source, not facts

1. Robertson 2023 (Upworthy, 22,743 experiments, ~105k variants, 370M impressions): each negative word CTR +2.3 %; positive −1.0 % (abstract; a registered analysis did not confirm the positive penalty).
2. Sahni, Wheeler & Chintagunta 2018: name in subject line opens 9.05→10.80 %, leads 0.39→0.51 % (+31 %). Failed replication 2023 (students −3.2 %, n.s.); title+surname +11.8 % opens (politician sample).
3. Linguistic Effects registered report 2023 (24,333 comparisons): first-person singular +0.241 log-odds, third singular +0.216, "we" −0.149, "you" not reliable (p=.044), longer slightly better +0.07; held-out pairwise accuracy of the simple language model 54.42 % (chance 50).
4. When Curiosity Gaps Backfire 2025 (8,977 confirmatory experiments): nonlinear; more concreteness predicted to help 8.7 % of headlines and harm 50.9 % (snippet).
5. TikTok 2021 (thousands of auction ads, N undisclosed): on-screen text within 7 s +43 % conversion; multiple scenes +38 %; spoken explanation + visible offer +87 %; explicit CTA text +80 %. Regional non-validations.
6. TikTok/Lumen 2021: suspense +16 % watch time (snippet).
7. MediaLabs working paper, October 2026, 3,340 TikTok Shop videos (of 13,309 top sellers, 29 categories), revenue per view by opening: price/offer 1.53× (n 193, 1.37–1.71), bold claim 1.28× (478), question 1.27× (184), relatable POV 1.05×, problem call-out 1.00×, demo 0.89× (242), unboxing 0.88× (122), borrowed viral/film footage 0.76× (41, 0.60–0.97), product close-up 1.15× (147), AI-generated opening 1.37× (20). Observational; top sellers only; revenue estimated. AUC of their sales model 0.641 with possible leakage.
8. Motion Creative Benchmarks 2026: 578,750 creatives, 6,015 accounts, $1.29B; ~5 % winners (≥10× account median spend and ≥ $500).
9. Teixeira, Wedel & Pieters 2012: surprise → attention, joy → retention.
10. Direct answers: no proof that surprise/danger/event in the first second beats other openings, for watching (suspense has support) or selling; no universal attention→sales coefficient.
Datasets: Upworthy Research Archive (32,487 experiments; ~22 % flagged randomisation problem, June 2024), KuaiRand, MicroLens, KuaiRec.
