## 2. The answers to his questions
1. **Event/danger in the first second.** A valid family, kept as experts' experience. One experiment with sales exists (Zhang, Peng & Tan 2026, small numbers): an impossible clip before the product film raised full watching and buying per view; bare oddity lost.
2. **"80 % of the sale is the hook".** Ogilvy's sentence on print headlines (1963). Not measured by anyone. Measured: of 1,558 TikTok sales ads, the median keeps 46 / 28 / 21 % of viewers at 1 / 2 / 3 s, and 11 % of conversions fall in the first 3 s, 77 % after second 6.  So the hook is the gate: four in five leave there; the sale is made after it.
3. **Highest view-through had 51 % lower conversion lift** (887 TikTok ads, System1) ✔. Two scores are kept, never one: *stops* and *sells*.
4. **Topic and offer weigh more than the sentence.** Same-creator pairs of hit and typical video show nearly the same hooks (102 pairs; 1,027 vs 1,445 videos); a product × promotion cell explained 9 % of ROI where the opening line's type explained none (2,735 Douyin ads). The factory settles topic and offer first.
5. **The winning type differs by sector** (Meta's own study): healthcare demo/product; packaged goods visual appeal and humour; car realism; shopping interactive. The library is read by sector.
6. **How many.** 4.0–8.8 % of new Meta ads become winners (578,750) ✔. For a 90 % chance of one: about 57 ads for a small account, 25 for a large one (computed, if independent). "Make 3–5" is too few over a month; one shoot with many openings is the way. Four variants tested are worth +24.6 % clicks over the average one (ours) ✔.

## 4. The hook scorer — `hookscore.py` rewritten (Fable 5.2 · high, at B28's rank)
- **What it does.** Scores a hook twice — will they stop, will they buy — names the weak part, and writes better variants; it never replaces the live test.
- **Trained and tested on** (all opened without a login today unless said): Upworthy (32,487 headline tests, clicks, CC BY 4.0) · TTCC v0.2.0 (61,789 TikTok ads with per-second retention, click and conversion curves) · AdsTrace (2,833 Douyin ads with ROI) · SnapUGC (120,651 videos, share watching past 5 s) · Postdom CSV · our own results, which come to outweigh them.
- **The bar, on data it has not seen:** Upworthy pairs above 65.1 % and the picked headline above +8.0 % clicks (a 38-rule model of ours already does this ✔ `code/upw2-out.txt`); Instagram hits vs misses above the original's AUC 0.56; on AdsTrace, better than the product × promotion cell alone. Below the bar it stays a lint.
- **Known limits.** A panel of AI personas was worse than one plain reader; LLM prompts alone are near chance (38–40 % where chance is 33 %); writers trained on tested winners drift to clickbait words, so the outcome *buy* stands beside *click*. The Yale result (its headlines preferred 44 % against about 30 %) is read second-hand.
