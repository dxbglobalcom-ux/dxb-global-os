## Key points
- Frames JEV's value proposition against the real, well-known "parse-validate-retry" cost every production LLM integration pays when extracting structured output from free-text generation — JEV's typed-output guarantee removes that specific tax.
- Explicit disclosure at time of writing: **"all performance metrics, pricing, and speed figures originate exclusively from TypeSafe's own launch announcements — no third-party verification, named customers, or disclosed funding exists"** [note: this was true only briefly — funding ($40M/DCVC) and multiple third-party benchmarks were published within days; treat this specific line as a snapshot of the information available at 2026-09-19, now superseded by the benchmarks and company-profile evidence elsewhere in this corpus].
- Core distinction, quoted: **"A type system can guarantee output validity; it cannot guarantee correctness."** — This is the same distinction independently corroborated by KDnuggets and by the `baibizhe` tool-hallucination finding.
