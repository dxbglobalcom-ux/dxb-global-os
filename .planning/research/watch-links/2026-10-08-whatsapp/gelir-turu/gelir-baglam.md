# The yardstick — what "useful to the holding" means (2026-10-08)

## The holding's purpose — the first thing to know before judging anything

**His words, 2026-10-08: "TEKNOLOJİK GELİŞMİŞLİK VE GELİR ÜRETİMİ ELBETTE HOLDİNGİN KURULUŞ GAYELERİNDEN."**
The holding has two founding purposes, bound together:
- **Technological advancement** — the machine itself: the world's first digital OS-kernel holding, built to
  the Ferrari standard (first place in a world competition, never amateur).
- **Revenue generation** — real economic activity earning sustainable, HALAL net profit (P0, binding since
  2026-07-16): find revenue opportunities continuously, turn them into projects, run them with digital
  employees, sell, measure, scale winners, stop losers, 24/7. It is a company and a factory: it produces,
  sells and earns.

So **"useful to the holding" MEANS: how much this advances the holding's technology and its earning** —
usually both at once, because the machine is what earns. A link that truly advances the machine is useful
in its own right, even before its money road is visible; a link that brings money is useful even if it
adds no technology. Nothing the holding does is "useful but leads nowhere": usefulness is advancement and
earning power — directly, by making a revenue line possible, faster, cheaper or better, or by cutting what
we spend.

## Earning is wider than any list (his correction, 2026-10-08: "gelir üretme holding için sadece bu dar çerçeve değil ki bunlar bir kısmı küçük bir tarafı işin sadece")

The holding is a factory of companies, not a list of channels. With its machine — the operating system,
the digital workforce (214 personas), the studio, the research and opportunity-discovery engines — it can
open and run a business in ANY halal sector and earn in every halal way: its own companies and brands
(e-commerce, trade, physical companies — the Outleteuro pilot was one), services sold to clients, products
and software, content and audiences, partnerships and sponsors (MiniMax sponsors DXB; YouTube sponsors DXB
as the world's first digital OS-kernel holding), the OS itself (its standing, licensing it, selling the
machine), and whatever its discovery engine finds next. Every real improvement of the machine multiplies
all of these at once — that is why technology is earning power.

So judge each link by how much it raises the holding's TOTAL earning power — now, and as the machine
grows. The road names below are common examples for the gelir_katmani field, never a boundary: when a
link's money comes another way, name that road in your own words. Never force a link into a box, and never
score it down because its road is not on the list.

## holding_puan (0–3) — the holding's usefulness: technological advancement and earning power

- **3** — brings money to the holding directly, or a real technological advance of the machine (or a
  capability a revenue line needs now); the mechanism is real (seen in the content or measured); inside our
  boundaries; startable with what we already hold plus at most an account/approval step. Act on it.
- **2** — a real money mechanism, a clear advance of the machine, a capability that clearly raises what we
  can sell, or a large measurable cost saving — but it needs building, accounts or a first test.
- **1** — a small or indirect contribution: a minor saving, enabler or improvement, or a money idea whose
  mechanism is weak or unclear.
- **0** — advances neither the machine nor the holding's earning.

Judge SEVERELY in both directions. Never drop a money idea because the creator's numbers are unverified or
a platform rule is a risk — find the real number (one or two searches, cite the URL) and write how the risk
is passed. Drop it only when (a) it is haram or deceptive, (b) the mechanism is fake (pure bait / a course
funnel with nothing behind it), or (c) we demonstrably cannot do it. Severe ALSO means no hype: a creator's
income claim is an "iddia" until a primary source shows it; separate what the creator earns from the thing
itself from what he earns selling courses about it.
