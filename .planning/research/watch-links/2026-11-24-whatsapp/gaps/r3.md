## dm-010 — Adi Elena (@adielena.finance), CAC and LTV (39 s)
https://www.instagram.com/reel/Db786C6i1yK/

**(a)** Finance educator account; nothing sold in this reel. **(b) Withheld:** nothing.

**(c) Given, verbatim**
- "CAC, you take everything you spend on marketing and sales last month. You divide it by how many new customers you got." Card: CAC = marketing + sales / number of new customers.
- "LTV, take your average monthly revenue per customer, you divide it by the percentage of customers you lose each month." Card: LTV = avg monthly revenue per customer / % customers lost.
- "Now you divide the LTV by CAC, that's your ratio. One to one, you're losing money on every customer. Three to one, you're breaking even at the best. Five to one and above, now you have a model that can scale." Card: "1:1 losing money · 3:1 breaking even · 5:1 & above scalable model — OPTIMAL".
