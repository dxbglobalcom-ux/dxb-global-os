## 2. Proposed leg texts — for the lead to place on the CEO's word

### 2.1 B28 — "The hook factory" (from the 24 Nov WhatsApp links)
> On his word of 2026-11-24 the hook links of three WhatsApp groups were studied whole; the system is
> `.planning/research/watch-links/2026-11-24-whatsapp/HOOK-SYSTEM.md`. What the row gains: (1) one hook library with a fixed
> schema (family, four channels, placement, awareness stage, persona, language, market, source, numbers, verdict); (2)
> `hook-writer` (#31) with a lint — the open pack's dealbreakers and Kallaway's three parts — and the rule that every
> claim has a proof row; (3) the Wednesday hook session: fifty hooks, forty from proven shapes, ten new; (4) Parker's
> operating rules in "measure and pick": `scoreboard.csv`, one variable a day, no verdict under 24 hours, `learning.md`;
> (5) the sample-size table: a 1,000-view judgement sees about six points, not less. **The trial:** four weeks, two
> videos a week, one holding line, one platform, nothing paid. **Stops at him:** the accounts; installing the open pack.

### 2.2 B43 — "The first-second drawer"
> A kisayol drawer for visual hooks: the ~20 types (anticipation, danger, impossible, fake-out, plot twist, near-miss,
> escalation, cliffhanger, stitch, effect hooks), each with the 77-card prompt pattern (9:16 phone look, a timed
> single take, sound, "cut before the impact", no one hurt, no text inside the clip) and the brand at the payoff;
> a bridge line to the product within two seconds. **The trial:** three hooks for one holding product
> on the local engine, to his eye, minutes and cost recorded.

### 2.3 B28 — addition to the Meta Ads MCP leg (2026-11-18)
> From İsa Nurdoğdu's ad agent (dm-038): the agent asks before it reads — product, price, margin per sale, lead-to-sale
> rate, monthly budget — because no ROAS can be read without the margin; drafts are created paused and go live on
> approval; Google Ads is driven through the browser where no connector exists, and only while it is open. The Monday
> report follows the Weekly Creative Strategy Report prompt (dm-022, kept verbatim in `gaps/r4.md`): a significance
> threshold per creative, variants judged as one concept, hook rate read against ROAS.

### 2.4 B28 (H6) — "The creator army" (new module — opens only on his word)
> For e-commerce clients: recruit creators already posting in the niche; qualify them before (audience match,
> engagement, content quality, niche fit) and after (sales, CTR, ROAS, GMV); a free product, a commission and a
> one-page creator brief (customer, problem, mechanism, benefits, objections, hooks, proof — "not a script"); a weekly
> creator call showing the winners; the marketplace (TikTok Shop where it operates) as the test bench; winners' usage
> rights bought and run as Meta ads. Sold as a monthly management fee, with a share of the program's sales where agreed.
> **Stops at him:** opening the module; creator and usage-rights contracts; a seller account; the halal screen of each
> product. Source: `ADS-REVENUE.md` §2.

### 2.5 B84 — "The decision-engine page and the quiz"
> The product page rebuilt so each block answers one question (what is the problem, is it normal, does it work, is it
> safe, is it for me); reviews next to the buy button; shipping and returns in one line above the fold; a three-step
> diagnostic quiz that recommends and saves the result to an email. Read by the funnel-leak audit; sold as a fixed-price
> repair beside the ad retainer. **Asked first:** the scratch-card pop-up and the BNPL widget (two halal questions).
