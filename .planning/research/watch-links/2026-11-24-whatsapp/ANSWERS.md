## dm-025 — on dm-024 (Kallaway, "How to Create Irresistible Hooks"): "best hook strategy for ad or social media videos"

**Answer.** The best single method in the job, and the spine of `HOOK-SYSTEM.md` §2.1: (1) **context lean** — say
what the video is about and make the viewer lean in (common ground, a benefit or pain, a metaphor, a surprising
fact); (2) **scroll-stop interjection** — one contrast line, usually "but"; (3) **contrarian snapback** — on topic,
in the opposite direction. Then: a visual hook (3–5 bold words + the right amount of motion), lead with benefit or
pain, wrap the unknown in something known ("cult hopping"), first value within ~4 seconds on short form, short
sentences up front. For an **ad**, two things are added from the other sources: the callout that says who it is
for (Hormozi) and a specific problem tied to a solution and a reason to act now (Chase Chappell's 10/10). His
"400+ hook database" and his software are paid; not needed.
