## 2. Is it double?

**No — they are two layers that touch in the middle.**

- **Overlap (both do):** silence detection/cutting, burned subtitles, colour grade, loudness, overlay animations through the *same* two renderers (HyperFrames, Remotion), a self-review step, session memory, Claude-Code-native. On every overlapping row video-use's implementation is the tighter *cut craft* (word-boundary snapping, 30 ms fades, lossless concat, output-timeline SRT, automatic -14 LUFS two-pass) and OpenMontage's is the wider *production system* (checkpoints, approvals, budget, board, 13 pipelines).
- **video-use alone:** executed **filler-word / false-start cutting on word boundaries**, multi-take best-take selection, pop-free segment boundaries, publish-grade loudness as part of the render. It is one skill + six Python helpers (2,595 lines); it owns no provider and generates nothing.
- **OpenMontage alone:** the **long→short clip factory**, podcast repurposing, auto-reframe (centre-crop today, face-tracked once mediapipe is installed), face/eye enhance, dubbing/localisation pipeline, 52 generation providers, Remotion scene stack, Backlot living board, fail-closed approval gates, cost tracker.

**Direct answers.**
- *Does OpenMontage already cut filler words/silence from a filmed talking head?* **Silence yes** (`silence_cutter`, measured: 6 gaps, 5.8 s removed from the 19.9 s test clip). **Filler words no** — there is no tool for it; the agent would have to read `transcriber` word times and issue `video_trimmer` cuts by hand, and `transcriber` is unavailable here anyway. video-use does exactly this as its first-class job and did it today on local Whisper output.
- *Does OpenMontage already make Shorts from a long video?* **Yes, by design** — `clip-factory` (transcribe → rank candidates → per-clip aspect → subtitles → compose → export package). Reframe is the weak leg (its own skill says so, `scene-director.md:28`; `face_tracker` unavailable here). **video-use does not do this at all** — the earlier claim is corrected.
