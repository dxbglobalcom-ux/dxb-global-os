## 1/10 — SEO · AEO · GEO (Get found on Google & AI)
- **Keyword Research** — Find the words & questions your audience searches
- **On-Page SEO** — Optimise titles, headings, content & images
- **Technical SEO** — Speed, crawling, indexing & site structure
- **Off-Page & Local** — Backlinks, reviews & Google Business Profile
- **AEO** — Be the direct answer in snippets & AI Overviews
- **GEO** — Get cited by ChatGPT, Gemini & Perplexity

## 2/10 — How Search Works (Crawl → Index → Rank)
- **Crawling** — Googlebot discovers pages by following links
- **Indexing** — Google stores & understands each page
- **Ranking** — The most helpful, relevant page shows first
- **Search Intent** — Learn, compare, buy or find a specific site
- **SERP Features** — Ads, map pack, snippets, videos & AI answers
- **AI Answers** — AI tools summarise many sources into one answer

## 3/10 — Keyword Research (Start here)
- **Seed Keywords** — List your services, products & customer problems
- **Search Intent** — Match each keyword to the right page type
- **Long-Tail** — 3+ word phrases: easier to rank, higher intent
- **Question Keywords** — "How", "what", "cost": great for blogs & AEO
- **Free Tools** — Keyword Planner, autocomplete, People Also Ask
- **Keyword Mapping** — One main keyword per page, no overlap

## 4/10 — On-Page SEO (Optimise each page)
- **Title Tag** — ~50–60 characters, keyword near the start
- **Meta Description** — ~150–160 characters, written to earn the click
- **Headings** — One H1, then clear H2s & H3s
- **URL** — Short & readable: /seo-services-indore
- **Images** — Compressed WebP + descriptive alt text
- **Internal Links** — Link related pages with clear anchor text

## 5/10 — Technical SEO (Help Google crawl)
- **Core Web Vitals** — LCP ≤ 2.5s · INP ≤ 200ms · CLS ≤ 0.1
- **Mobile-First** — Google mainly uses your mobile site
- **XML Sitemap** — Submit it in Google Search Console
- **Robots.txt** — Never block important pages or files
- **Redirects & 404s** — 301 moved pages, fix broken links
- **Schema Markup** — JSON-LD for business, articles, FAQs & products

## 6/10 — Content & E-E-A-T (Helpful content wins)
- **Experience** — Show first-hand use, photos & real examples
- **Expertise** — Written by someone who knows the topic
- **Authoritativeness** — Mentions & links from trusted sites
- **Trust** — Accurate info, contact details & reviews
- **Topic Clusters** — One pillar page + related supporting posts
- **Fresh Content** — Update important pages regularly

## 7/10 — Off-Page & Local (Build authority)
- **Quality Backlinks** — Links from relevant, trusted websites
- **Digital PR** — Share data, stories & expert quotes with media
- **Google Business Profile** — Right category, photos, posts & services
- **Reviews** — Ask every happy customer & reply to all reviews
- **NAP Citations** — Same name, address & phone everywhere
- **Brand Mentions** — Get talked about on blogs, news & forums

## 8/10 — AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
- **Answer First** — Reply to the question in the first 40–60 words
- **Question Headings** — Use H2s exactly like people ask
- **Lists & Tables** — Steps, comparisons & clear structure
- **FAQ Sections** — Cover follow-up questions on key pages
- **Featured Snippets** — Short, direct answers can win position zero
- **Voice Search** — Natural, conversational answers

## 9/10 — GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
- **Be Quotable** — Clear definitions, facts & specific numbers
- **Be Credible** — Cite sources & show real experience
- **Be Mentioned** — Reddit, YouTube, Quora, LinkedIn & reviews
- **Be Consistent** — Same brand details across the web
- **Be Crawlable** — Plain HTML; don't block AI search crawlers
- **Track AI Traffic** — GA4 referrals from chatgpt.com & perplexity.ai

## 10/10 — Track & Improve (Measure results)
- **Search Console** — Clicks, impressions, CTR & average position
- **Google Analytics 4** — Traffic sources, leads & conversions
- **Rank Tracking** — Weekly positions for your target keywords
- **Page 2 Wins** — Push keywords ranking 11–20 first
- **AI Visibility** — Check monthly if AI tools mention your brand
- **Monthly Report** — Work done, results & next steps
