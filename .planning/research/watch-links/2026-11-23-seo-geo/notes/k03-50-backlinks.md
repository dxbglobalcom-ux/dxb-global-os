# k03 — "50 More Free Backlinks" (Klint AI, David Roberts)

Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/13AyIxLAMZFFcdT7e-2a_grKk_9nWwgkJ594Sofv6S6E/ (read whole via /export?format=txt, 2026-11-23; raw copy: ../dl/k03/doc.txt)
DR = Ahrefs domain rating as stated by the author, "checked on November 16, 2026" — not re-measured by us.
Author's claims: average DR 75; 34 of 50 are DR >= 70; "many of these links are nofollow" (his own words); "Google's AI cites review sites in over a third of buying searches (SE Ranking data)" — unverified.
"None of them from the first list" — there is an earlier list (the first 50) not in this doc.

## Step 1 — listing kit (one document, same name and description everywhere)
- Company name, website, support email
- Tagline under 60 characters, written as the search you want to win (e.g. "AI note taker for sales calls")
- Short description (160 chars) and long description (~150 words)
- Category, pricing, 3-5 key features
- Square logo 512x512, wide banner, 3-5 screenshots
- Founder names, year started, location, social links

## Step 2 — the agent prompt (verbatim)
> Get us on all 50 of these sites. Use only the facts in my listing kit, and keep the name and description the same everywhere. Go in order, highest domain rating first. On each site, find the free sign-up or submit page and fill in every field. Stop and ask me when a site needs an email code, a captcha, a phone number, or a payment. Never pay for anything, and skip any site with no free option. Keep a log as a table: site, status (live, waiting for review, needs me, skipped), and the URL of our profile.

Suggested by the author: a browser agent (Claude in Chrome, ChatGPT agent mode); about 10 a day, done in a week; email confirmations and captchas are human steps.

## After you submit (author)
- Finish every profile (logo, screenshots, full description).
- Get 5 reviews on Trustpilot and Clutch, happiest customers first.
- Search brand on Google and ask ChatGPT about the category before and a month after.
- Keep the log; update every profile when pricing/description changes.
