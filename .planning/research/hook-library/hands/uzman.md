## 3. The ten strongest

1. "Listen up — the soap you shower with…" (Dr. Squatch): accusation timed before the skip button; nearly 44,000 conversions at $33.70, about $1.5M spent on that film.
2. "What's a super easy way to tell that your bed is awful? The raw egg test" (Purple): about 400M views; the agency credits it with launching a $300M business.
3. "Hi I'm Mike… for a dollar a month we send razors to your door" (Dollar Shave Club): 12,000 orders in 48 hours, $4,500 film.
4. "I'm Aches and I'm Pains… we've been out of a job" (Purple 2024): 34% lower cost per visitor than the Black Friday spot.
5. "10-minute exercise routine" still: beat three other messages, over $2M spent on one ad. "5-minute makeup routine" also passed $2M.
6. "Why would you want to contour your nose?" (Jones Road): 3,000 to 130,000 followers in a week, revenue nearly 4x (shared with a second video).
7. "I like hedgehogs but I also have borderline personality disorder": the odd order slip, then a pan to the shirt (Nice Shirt Thanks); 21,000 orders over the whole format, no traditional marketing.
8. "Does discomfort slow down your family adventures?…" beat "Tired of missing out…": ROAS 0.95 to 1.23, order value +68%, only the text changed.
9. "20% Off Every Order -- 12 Days of Christmas Day 9!" beat the same words with the offer last: +14.4% revenue per e-mail.
10. "I have a hair extension salon in LA… 1.2 million… they leave" (Hormozi Short): 144x his median. Four more of the same shape ran 41–129x (one, 69x, is not a row).

## 4. Surprises and contradictions

- **Motion, 550,000 ads, $1.3B:** about 5% of ads win. First lines by win rate: newness 11.4%, price anchor 10.9%, confession 8.7%, contrarian 7.0%, plain "you" 6.7%, authority 6.4% (last). Text-only ads win most (11.6%), carousels least (4.5%). It was sale season, and the numbers were read from the PDF's text layer.
- **Contrarian:** Hyperbeam (50 hooks, vendor blog) ranks contrarian first at -34% cost per customer; Motion's far larger count puts it mid-table.
- **Opens mislead:** the e-mail with fewer opens and 16% fewer clicks earned 63% more revenue.
- **Product first or not:** MomentIQ says 11% of $1M videos show the product in 3 s. Ninja Blast's best sellers show it in 18 of 18 first frames, and the $65k and $90k TikTok videos were not story-led.
- **Experts misjudge:** on the Ridge test, two of three well-known ad people picked the loser; in Shackelford's hall "everybody got it wrong" on one pair.
- **Jenny Hoyos's "10 million per Short"** is history: her last 1,500 Shorts have a median of 198,500 (my count). A blog's "30 million" for her McDonald's Short is 8.1M on YouTube.
- **The longest runner of Selzee's 54** (1,172 days) has no hook trick at all: "Travel outfit essentials".
- **Galloway admits** the Noah Kagan door thumbnail used an actress who is not in the film. The row says not to copy that.
- **Live sales:** no source prints how the $1M–$2.7M streams opened. Only a rule row was possible.
