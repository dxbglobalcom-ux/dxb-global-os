# Watching films that went viral by themselves — addition to WATCH.md (2026-11-25)

Read first, whole: `/home/dxb/.local/share/dxb/hook-lab/WATCH.md` (it points to BRIEF.md and SHELVES.md beside it: read both). Everything there holds, with
these differences — the films here are ORGANIC: nobody paid for the views (a machine check took out films with many views and almost no likes).

The CEO's question today: "Viral olmuş videolar neden viral oluyor? Yani oradaki şeyler önemli, hooklar önemli." You answer it film by film.
And: "Sektörden sektöre, konudan konuya, videodan videoya her şey değişebilir … ürünün kendi özelliğine göre." So every row says what it depends on.

- The card's PROOF line = views against the same account's own median; LIKES line = likes as a share of views, comments, date.
- Fields: `door` "TENFOLD"; `outcome` "watched"; `channel` "short_organic"; `measured_by`: "<platform of the card>, views against the account's own median,
  read 25 Nov 2026"; `result`: the PROOF facts and the like share in one line; `url`: the ADDRESS line; `proves` "whole".
- `why`: two or three sentences [mine]: what stops a stranger in the first second; what question, feeling or promise then holds him; what makes him pass it on
  or write a comment. Say what you SEE, no praise.
- one more field `depends_on`: one line: what must be true for this shape to work — the kind of product or job (result visible in seconds? extreme dirt?
  big scale? dangerous? tasty sound? a face people know?), the seller (known or unknown), the audience (trade people, parents, everybody).
- `for_client`: the same shape for a concrete small business of ANOTHER sector — ready to film, and it must end in that business (what it sells), not in fun.
- LEAVE OUT besides WATCH.md's list: pure entertainment with nothing a business can learn; children's shows; weapons; medical gore; a film whose force is only
  a famous guest or a trend song; what is not in English (silent films are fine).
- `strength`: "usable", "weak" when you doubt the film; the lead sets it from the numbers.
- KEEP is choosier than it looks: a shape you could sell to a client. But do not miss the quiet ones: a job done well, a plain question, a price told.

Final message, at most 16 lines: looked at / kept / left out by reason; the checker's line; the eight best (id, account, shelf, one line why); what the viral
films of this list share in their first second, in three lines, with counts; what could not be judged.
