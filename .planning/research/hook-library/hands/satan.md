**4. What surprised me or contradicts what is taught**
- **Views are not sales.** Evian Roller Babies drew 50m views while sales fell 25%. Loctite's Super Bowl film raised its own sales 8.6% and rival Gorilla Glue's nearly 45%. Neither is in the openings; both are rules.
- **Fame is not money.** LifeCall ("I've fallen and I can't get up") went out of business in 1993. Other sources say Energizer lost share to Duracell while the bunny became famous. Both are out.
- **The famous Alka-Seltzer "two tablets doubled sales" is not confirmed** (Snopes), so it is out.
- **Dropbox's "5,000 to 75,000 overnight" belongs to the Digg screencast**, not to the well-known paper-figure film, so it is out.
- **Slow and silent openings won at scale.** Gorilla shows four seconds of plain purple; Specsavers, Cog and Epic Split also start slowly. Those audiences were mostly sitting in front of a television, so I would not carry this over to a skippable feed untested.
- **Direct Line replaced a double IPA Gold campaign** and the new one was 20% more effective, against "never change a winner".
- **In direct response the pattern is plain:** maker's name with "I invented", a wound close-up with its price, a survival test ("will it blend?"), or the old way failing on screen.
