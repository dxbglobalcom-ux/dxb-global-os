**THE THREE MOST SERIOUS PROBLEMS**
1. **Two "They sold" proofs look manufactured, and a third cannot be repeated.**
   - Hyperbeam and Advize show template signs. Galvor gives no N.
   - README §1 rows "Contrast, comparison, against-the-habit", "Start in the middle of an event" and "The problem acted out" rest on Hyperbeam or Galvor alone.
   - Motion's 30,015 contrast ads and 6,006 contrarian ads win at 6.78 % and 7.01 %, below its 7.98 % average. Their spend-use ratios are 1.20 and 1.04.
2. **"Price or offer first" is weaker than written.**
   - Motion's 10.89 % is **71 winners of about 650 ads** (95 % range 8.7–13.5), not "578,750 ads". The table was read by eye in `mo-21.png`, and the ad count comes from winners ÷ hit rate. It is not distinguishable from offer (9.29 %) or urgency (9.73 %).
   - In ContentIQ a price video sells about as much per video as the others, sixth of the ten hooks in the table. Its 1.47× per view goes with about a third fewer views, on a winners-only sample with estimated sales.
3. **Small cells were quoted where the same evidence contradicts them.**
   - "As a dentist…" 3.16× is 30 videos: 16 of 480 top videos against 7 of 480 bottom videos. It does not survive a 5 % correction for 59 features. Trender gives credentials **0.90 on 2,091 Reels**.
   - "If you…" 1.29× is a 16-account test. The same page's family row is **1.03× over 2,149 openings**, and it says no positive family is proven.
   - "Words on screen 1.79×" is 89.9 % of top videos against 84.3 % of bottom videos, in accounts picked because they use on-screen text.
