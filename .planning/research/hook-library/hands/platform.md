**4. Surprises and corrections to round one**

- **Early brand is not free for small brands.** In the full System1 report, strong two-second recognition goes with −19 % conversion lift for challengers (+25 % for leaders). Positive feeling gives challengers +77 % (leaders −25 %).
- **Hard sell depends on size.** Small brands: entertaining openings 2.4x conversion lift, hard-sell features +41 %. Leaders: 3.5x hard sell, +29 % entertaining. Round one's "+88 % salesmanship" left out that entertaining ads also convert +50 %.
- **Spoken brand name is not the top cue.** Sonic asset +191 %, logo inside the scene +182 %, spoken name +14 %, bare logo −30 %. The 2026 playbook also puts sound first.
- Snap's +71 % belongs to entertainment. The 70 % confidence on Meta's 15 % figure is in the source itself.
- **TikTok's +175 %** has no source in its own footnotes and no N, so it is marked weak.
- **The cleanest creator-vs-brand test is nearly a tie:** Nurofen, Circana three regions, +9.5 % vs +8.0 % sales.
- **Watching, remembering and clicking had different winners of the same film.** Mountain Dew's mystery cut: +26 % watched, −54 % recall. L'Oréal's glossy ad: 82 % more watched, less than half the clicks.
- Split screens and direct-to-lens score low in System1's memory work but won sales in Google's test.
- I found one page that isolates the opening and gives sales, the Pigeon agency's doubled ROAS. It says its numbers are invented, so I left it out.
