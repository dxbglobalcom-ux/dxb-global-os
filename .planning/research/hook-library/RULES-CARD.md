# THE OPENING CARD

Made from the 495 rule rows of `in.jsonl` (all read). Numbers are copied from the rows. No rule comes from general knowledge.

**Grades.** A = randomised tests of at least two different owners agree. B = one good test (more than 10,000 people), or a count across many tests, or several keyed/split tests whose size is not printed. C = agreeing experience of trusted experts with results, no test of the rule itself.
**"Randomised" here** includes A/B tests and split runs where people were shown one version by chance. **"Keyed"** = replies counted per ad, not always side by side.
**Counted once.** These rows describe the same test or study, so they are one source: r-abarsiv-022 = r-chatgpt-002 · r-abarsiv-012 = r-chatgpt-003 · r-abarsiv-006 = r-davranis-006 · r-abarsiv-016 = r-davranis-044 · r-bilimsoz-029 = r-davranis-031 · r-klasik-008 = r-schwartz-013 · r-abarsiv-005 and r-davranis-007 (both Wikipedia) · r-platform-001…005 = r-uzman-010…014 (all one Motion report) · every "Upworthy" row (r-bilimsoz-005, 007, 018, 020–026, 034–039, 045 and others) = one publisher's archive, 2013–15.
**Who paid.** [seller] = the number comes from someone who sells the space, the tool or the service. Not tagged each time: the 6,700 shop experiments (r-davranis-057) are the testing firm Qubit's, the NextAfter tests (most r-abarsiv rows) an agency's own archive, Caples, Hopkins, Schwartz, Sugarman, Halbert and Ogilvy their own accounts.
**Never.** Every deadline, stock figure, crowd number, "already yours" and flaw must be true. That is also what the rows show: r-abarsiv-014, r-hopkins-007, r-caples-016 (its advice to hide a call is not used), r-davranis-053.

# 1. THE RULES

## What to say

### 1. Choose the promise before the words: two promises for the same thing can be worlds apart. [B]
- Numbers: bought, 19-1/2 times as much (one case, wordings not shown). Replied: the winning appeal in the headline 502, 511, 550, 666 and 867; without it 191 to 263 (N=4,412 replies). Calls, two and a half times for "cool, quiet bedroom" over "Get rid of that humidity". Replies, 65 percent more (two appeals on bus cards), nearly 50 percent more (six reasons on postcards), almost three times (best against weakest of eight promises). Same body, new headline only: 9.8 vs 13.9 orders per thousand.
- Rows: r-caples-001, r-caples-008, r-caples-022, r-caples-032, r-caples-034, r-caples-036, r-caples-037, r-hopkins-017, r-hopkins-024, r-hopkins-033, r-hopkins-009, r-schwartz-016, r-schwartz-017, r-sugarman-040, r-sugarman-052, r-sugarman-061, r-uzman-003, r-uzman-038, r-klasik-012, r-sugarman-001, r-sugarman-070, r-caples-002
- Failed: r-davranis-060 (kind of appeal 1.06, not significant, postal surveys); r-davranis-049 (of nine letter wordings to 964,870 people only two moved anything); r-sugarman-020 (one ad: a new headline +20%, a bolder guarantee +100% — the offer can matter more).
- Writer: list eight one-line promises, show them to real buyers or run them as cheap stills, then write many openings round the one that wins.

### 2. Say plainly what they get, in the first line, and show at once how to get it. [A]

Replies to one letter, 1,000 per opening: straight ask 4.3%, news 3.3%, question 2.3%, sensation and sympathy under 2%. Point at once: 17 percent against 3 percent. Revenue per e-mail, offer moved to the front: $0.036 to $0.058 (+63%). Same book, plain title: e.g. "How to" 0 to 30,000 copies a year (r-klasik-002). Way to answer: coupon moved to the top of four ads, returns tripled; ad with no button nearly 2.5 times the cost; one school sold one out of two who telephoned, one out of five who wrote.
- Rows: r-abarsiv-001, r-abarsiv-013, r-abarsiv-019 (clarity won 73 of 93 valid tests), r-klasik-001, r-klasik-002, r-klasik-009, r-klasik-010, r-caples-004, r-caples-006, r-caples-019, r-caples-029, r-caples-038, r-caples-009, r-davranis-008, r-uzman-035, r-platform-002, r-platform-003, r-uzman-011, r-uzman-012, r-sugarman-046, r-sugarman-057, r-davranis-043, r-abarsiv-021, r-caples-020, r-caples-024, r-hopkins-019, r-hopkins-035, r-hopkins-036, r-sugarman-011, r-sugarman-054
- Failed: r-hopkins-012 ("free" on top cut beauty/hygiene replies to a quarter); r-schwartz-002 and r-caples-007 (a promise too big to believe did not pay); r-bilimsoz-002 (price alone won fewer sign-ups than a benefit beside it); r-hopkins-037 (half price does not bring new users — his claim, no numbers); r-sugarman-005 (held the sales message back — no numbers); r-abarsiv-001 itself: benefit AND offer together won, +55.6%.
- Writer: "[what it does for you] – get [the thing]".

### 3. If something true is new, scarce or ending, that goes first. [B]
- Numbers: share of ads that became winners [seller, observational, sale season]: newness 11.37% (83 winners), urgency 9.73% (4,855), offer only 9.29% (4,798) against authority 6.40% (590). - Rows: r-platform-002, r-platform-003, r-uzman-011, r-uzman-012, r-davranis-057, r-davranis-049, r-davranis-040 (a set hour to call: +61% on top), r-davranis-041, r-davranis-061, r-sugarman-059, r-klasik-006, r-klasik-013, r-caples-004, r-sugarman-002, r-bilimvideo-015

- Writer: write the real news, the real stock or the real last day in the first words, one such sign per screen; if there is none, do not make one.

### 4. Offer one thing and use one device. [A]
- Numbers: bought, one watch model vs nine 3 to 1 (split, N not given). Loan letters, 53,194 randomised: one worked example instead of several raised take-up about as much as a 25% price cut (product outside our line; method only). Insurance letters, n = 744,510: sender's picture + plan line +13.17% enrolments, "kitchen sink" letter +1.84%. Of 100 best offers of one house 91 centred on a single idea (second-hand, survivors).
- Rows: r-sugarman-019, r-davranis-035, r-davranis-036, r-davranis-053, r-davranis-011, r-uzman-009, r-sugarman-015, r-davranis-008, r-davranis-022 (a match works, a bigger match adds nothing; 50,083)
- Failed: where the first question is "which one is for me?" a chooser won: r-chatgpt-001 (+12.18% purchases, 136,000 visitors; but "Shop by Category" moved under the hero lost 6.2%); r-hopkins-030 wants every tested claim in the body (no numbers).
- Writer: strike every opening that promises two things; show the range only to a visitor who came to choose.

### 5. Put the proof inside the promise, and keep the promise believable. [B]

### 6. Say the flaw yourself, then answer it. [C]
- Numbers: Volkswagen 120,000 (1959) to 430,000 (1967); Domino's same-store sales +14.3% in the first quarter (new recipe too). Confession openings on Meta [seller, observational]: 8.74% winners (675), spend against use 1.41.
- Rows: r-klasik-014, r-satan-014, r-sugarman-007, r-platform-002, r-platform-003, r-uzman-011, r-uzman-012
- Failed: nothing tested it alone; every case changed other things too. r-caples-010: scolding the BUYER instead was "an unqualified failure".
- Writer: take the truest, harmless complaint from your reviews as line one; line two is the repair.

### 7. Hand it over as already theirs — when it really is. [A]
- Numbers: "Activate" over "Learn more"/"Enroll": +25.7% clicks (N 1,635), +31.5% (N 1,235), +206% sign-ups (N 26,326). Reminder texts "reserved for you": beta 0.41, P = 0.05 (47,306 patients); repeated with 689,693. Text "I have booked you a place": turn-up 26.8% vs 10.5% (n = 1,224). Shop subject lines: "You're getting…" is what the +32% model added most (descriptive) [seller].
- Rows: r-abarsiv-008, r-abarsiv-007, r-davranis-013, r-davranis-014, r-davranis-002, r-davranis-009 (take the frame only; the draw is outside our boundaries), r-bilimsoz-033, r-hopkins-028
- Failed: r-davranis-062 (262,085 patients: 0.99, 1.00, 1.01 — nothing, except for those who had a booking, 1.04); r-abarsiv-007 ("my" did not work in Danish; "your" on a payment page -24.95%).
- Writer: "Your place at 10.30 is kept" only if it is kept.

## To whom

### 8. Call the people who already want it or already have the trouble. [C]
- Numbers: Retirement ad naming the wish: nearly 3 times the inquiries of its 25 predecessors. "The Deaf Now Hear Whispers": inquiry cost to 34 cents from over a dollar. Worst-situation line (adjustable beds): cost per customer cut to a third (landing page changed too). Toothbrush convert "at least $25" (his estimate).

## The first words

### 10. Not about us: the reader's result, or one person's story — never "we". [B]
- Numbers: pairs of headlines (24,333, randomised): I/my +0.24, she/he/it +0.22, we/our −0.15, you +0.05 (not supported). One-person language about +6.4% clicks per step (draft). ## Length and pace

### 19. Ask short; explain in full. [A]
- Numbers: short request e-mail 4.8% vs 2.7% replies (7,002 people); shorter forms odds 1.58, brief e-mail letter 3.26. Where the gain must be understood, longer won: finished applications 1.04% vs 0.86%; mobile banner twice as long +40%; one-line donate banner a 95% drop; cutting the winning facts 483 vs 699 gifts; long ad value text +316% (N 710,592). Full ad texts had the lowest cost per lead; worst 2.4 times the best (one offer). Headlines: longer, full sentences win slightly (β +0.040); ten words or more sold more goods in a department store.
- Rows: r-davranis-026, r-davranis-027, r-davranis-028, r-davranis-038, r-davranis-048, r-davranis-050, r-davranis-060, r-davranis-061, r-abarsiv-021, r-abarsiv-025, r-bilimsoz-034, r-bilimsoz-051, r-bilimsoz-054, r-bilimsoz-065, r-sugarman-029, r-sugarman-035, r-sugarman-036, r-sugarman-046, r-sugarman-056, r-sugarman-065, r-hopkins-023, r-hopkins-030, r-caples-030, r-caples-013

# 2. WHERE THE EVIDENCE CONTRADICTS ITSELF

1. **Short or long?** Short: r-davranis-026 (4.8% vs 2.7%), r-davranis-028, r-davranis-061 (3.26). Long: r-davranis-038 (1.04% vs 0.86%), r-abarsiv-025 (95% drop when cut; +316%), r-davranis-050 (483 vs 699), r-sugarman-035. *Decides:* a plain request to a busy stranger → short; a gain, a price or a doubt to be understood → long. After the click, shorter again (1,478 vs 1,374, r-davranis-050).
2. **Person or facts/thing?** Person: r-abarsiv-003 (3.68% vs 0.25% clicks), r-davranis-059 (0.24% vs 0.09%), r-bilimsoz-022 (+6.4%). Thing: r-davranis-051 (375 vs 108), r-davranis-046 (1.6-2.3 times), r-klasik-003 (15,000 to 42,000), r-bilimvideo-028 (-1.0679, practical goods). *Decides, partly:* personal letter or story → person; list of facts or practical goods → thing. At Wikipedia time decided too: same organisation, two years apart.
3. **Brand early or late?** Early: r-platform-006 (+196% memory), r-platform-032 (nearly +300% recall), r-bilimvideo-012 (intent 0.698 vs 0.312), r-satan-004 (rival gained nearly +45%). Late: r-bilimvideo-014 (early costs -29.88% / -35.60% shares), r-bilimvideo-041 (later name linked to more sales, known brands, no size), r-platform-016 (small brands -19% conversion, leaders +25%). *Decides:* the goal (memory and intent vs shares), and how known the brand is — but for small brands the sales cell is one seller's unsized quartile. Not settled for small sellers.
4. **Product in the first seconds or not?** Yes: r-bilimvideo-041, r-bilimvideo-040 (99% vs 27%), r-uzman-038 (18 of 18 best sellers), r-platform-005 (demo 8.11%). No: r-uzman-030 (11% of 100 best sellers), r-platform-009 (below average for memory), r-platform-010. *Decides:* not settled; r-bilimvideo-001 suggests it is not WHEN but whether the product is the thing people look at.
5. **Digits.** For: r-bilimsoz-023 (+3.6%, +5.2%), r-davranis-018, r-abarsiv-010. Against: r-bilimsoz-033 (winner removes numbers, shop mail), r-bilimsoz-063 (0.029), r-abarsiv-010's own exception (+14.3% for the general line, N 144,374). *Decides:* headline/price/fact → digit; offer mail or appeal to feeling → the gift in words first. The mail side is descriptive only.
6. **Question.** Against: r-bilimsoz-020 (0.8645), r-klasik-009, r-caples-038. For: r-bilimsoz-061 (+257%), r-abarsiv-011 (+125.9%), r-bilimvideo-006 (interactive, shops), r-chatgpt-001, r-bilimsoz-053 (comments ×1.69). *Decides:* asked for a reply, a choice or about the reader's own thing → can win; asked instead of telling → loses. Uneven even inside one archive (I² 75.8%).
7. **Loss/fear or gain/hope?** Loss: r-davranis-040 (41% more calls), r-davranis-020's organ donors (3.1 vs 2.9), r-sugarman-069 (3 times), r-satan-010 (+75%). Gain: r-hopkins-042 (four to one), r-abarsiv-001 (+45.6%), r-sugarman-068 (almost 20%). Nothing: r-davranis-020 (both +1.6 points, about 17,000 per letter). *Decides:* people already in the trouble → loss; everyone else → gain.
8. **"Many do it" or "few do it"?** Many: r-davranis-023 (+13-18%), r-davranis-057 (+2.3%), r-hopkins-006. Few: r-abarsiv-005 (+77.1%, +84.2%; removing it lowered gifts in dozens of tests). Neither: r-davranis-015. *Decides:* things people buy → many; a shared free good asking for support → few; where nearly everyone already does it → neither. Never "many do wrong" (r-davranis-016).
9. **Free or discount first?** For: r-abarsiv-001 (+133.7%), r-caples-019, r-hopkins-034 (foods), r-platform-003 (giveaway 2.29). Against: r-hopkins-012 (quarter of the replies, hygiene/beauty), r-hopkins-037 (no new users), r-bilimsoz-002. *Decides in the rows:* food, guides, gadgets → free first; health/beauty → reason first; repeat purchase → benefit beside the discount. The health/beauty half is one man's keyed test of the 1920s.
10. **How-to.** For: r-klasik-002 (0 to 30,000), r-platform-040 (tutorials +45% installs rate), r-bilimvideo-047 (showing use +0.568 s), r-platform-034 (how-to more than twice the clicks of the glossy ad). Against: r-bilimsoz-035 (−0.00054), r-bilimsoz-063 (0.029), r-bilimvideo-031 (-2.11 points, cold), r-uzman-014 (about 5%). *Decides:* the reader who came for the skill (catalogue, app, search, followers) → how-to; the cold feed → show the result.
11. **Entertain or sell hard?** Entertain: r-platform-017 (+50% conversion), r-platform-015 small brands (2.4x). Hard sell: r-platform-017 (+88% conversion but memory -8%), r-platform-015 leaders (3.5x). *Decides:* brand size (one seller's cells).
12. **Keep the winner or change it?** Keep: r-caples-012 (seven years), r-sugarman-064 (eleventh run), r-satan-006 (15.2:1), r-schwartz-003, r-schwartz-015, r-sugarman-023, r-sugarman-048. Change: r-davranis-051, r-abarsiv-017 (+0.5% daily users by rotating), r-bilimsoz-057 (−0.309), r-sugarman-058, r-satan-008 (20% more effective). *Decides, partly:* same people every day → rotate the line; keep the promise or the character, refresh the face.

- Which kind of opening wins in which trade, with a size. Only ranks (r-bilimvideo-006) and one seller's thin cells (r-platform-005, r-uzman-014).
- Any randomised sales test of a short-video opening for a small local business. Nearest: r-uzman-037 ($85, clicks, line not printed).
- Whether a small seller should hide or show its name in second one, by sales (pair 3).
- Whether a winner in one channel wins in another. Only old claims (r-hopkins-009, r-klasik-005, r-caples-036); ads-to-organic is one listener's remark (r-uzman-008).
- Languages and markets. Nearly all is English and US/UK. Danish reversed one rule (r-abarsiv-007); one German page (r-bilimsoz-010); Turkey appears inside one intent panel (r-platform-039); no Turkish-language test; market against market appears only as a failed or repeated line (r-davranis-015, r-davranis-051, r-klasik-005).
- Sound: music, silence, a trending sound, an AI voice, by sales. r-bilimvideo-007 prints no range; r-sugarman-045 and r-platform-033 no figures. No row compares an AI voice with a human one.
- On-screen text by sales: how many words, where, how long, what size.
- Emojis (two descriptive mentions: r-bilimsoz-033, r-platform-039), hashtags (no row), WhatsApp first lines, radio, podcast openings (no row with a test).
- Whether Upworthy's 2013–15 results hold for ads and for today's readers; today's readers did not prefer that style (r-bilimsoz-043, stated choice).
- How long an opening lives in a feed. One seller's 7-14 days against 4-6 weeks (r-uzman-036).
- Where the funny opening sells, by trade; humour's sales effect is only marginal (p<.10, r-bilimvideo-034).
- Hook against offer against product: one man's successive tests (r-sugarman-020).
- What a true deadline or stock line does to trust over a year.
- Openings made by AI under an AI label, beyond one field study (r-bilimvideo-035: gain 1.2 to 0.2 points).
- The wording itself: several of the biggest wins do not print the line (r-caples-034, r-caples-036, r-hopkins-024, r-schwartz-016, r-uzman-037, r-platform-031).
- Contracts and big-ticket services: rows stop at leads (r-bilimvideo-030, r-abarsiv-024); I found none that follows an opening to a signed contract.
