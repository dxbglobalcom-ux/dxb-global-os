# Fashion: clothes, shoes, bags, hats, jewellery, watches — what won here (25 November 2026)
63 winners of this trade in the library: 28 sold · 17 seen by strangers, unpaid · 11 kept paying for · 7 brought clicks or names · 3 made the account grow (a winner may hold two roles); 26 films on this machine, 22 may be shown to anybody.
## What won in this trade, and why
1. **SOLD** — One odd thing on the product or its wearer that nobody explains. One looks for the reason, reads or listens on, and meets the goods on the way.
   First second: an elegant man in a white shirt with a black patch over one eye; an old man in a cap shaped like a fish. The product is worn, not held up.
   Hathaway shirts: the first insertion cost $3,176 and within a week every shirt in stock was sold out (o-sugarman-042). Bob Kapas, fish hats, filmed by a young man in a mall: first run of 2,500 sold out, 800 more pre-ordered (o-ad3-012). Fits cheap and dear, known or not; the oddity must be true or openly a costume.
2. **SOLD** — The thing itself, large, on a plain ground or laid out in a real place, without a mood scene and often without a person. The buyer judges the thing and puts himself into it; it also keeps us modest.
   First second: the watch alone; shirt and trousers laid out; wallet after wallet with short words.
   Hamilton, the watch alone against a period scene: three times as many sales (o-tec5-006). Taylor Stitch, clothes without a model: cost per buyer 18% lower (o-platform-013). Ridge, product shots without a voice: 20% more sales per advertising dollar at $10K each, then months in use (o-uzman-081).
3. **SOLD** — Under the picture: one true reason to believe or to save, whole, with nothing to calculate. People already looking at the goods want to decide, not to be amused.
   First second: a customer's sentence, the bundle, or the old price struck through, on the product picture itself.
   Twillory shirts, a review on the card: $4.41 back per dollar against 1.96; a bundle on the card: 4.49 (o-platform-015, o-platform-016). Latico bags, old price struck through: 32% higher return (o-platform-012). Evo, "$50-off" against a percentage: 170% more revenue (o-abarsiv-171). Worth's Clothing, coupon "$23, Reg. $60": over $3,300 (o-caples-059).
4. **SOLD** — A trial that could destroy the product, or an expert who cuts it open. What is inside cannot be seen in a shop, so one waits for the result.
   First second: the test is already standing there (a boat engine in a glass tank), or the knife is at the leather. Fits watches, shoes, leather, dear or distrusted goods.
   Timex: five million sold by 1958 (o-satan-114). A leather craftsman cutting open a $500 bag, "Is it worth it?": sold out all inventory, his consultant's account (o-uzman-015).
   **SEEN** — the same on film here: Rose Anvil, a blade into black leather until red shows: 602,000 views, 54.7 times its middle film (o-y5-005).
5. **SOLD** — The story of one garment or one maker, small and true, told before the sale. "Only 2 sales" or "people tried to buy it off my back" is believed because nobody boasts with it.
   First second: the daughter's face and the garden shed; a drawing of one long coat.
   FunkyPeepers, hand-decorated glasses: from 2 sales to nearly 150 new orders (o-ad5-011, not for showing). J. Peterman, one coat: 160 sales, about $29,000, from $3,000 of space (o-klasik-153). BluBlocker, "what I saw I could not believe": one of his most successful ads (o-sugarman-016).
6. **SEEN** — A strange instrument or a hidden state of the thing, in a craftsman's hands, without a word. One cannot tell what it is for, and the answer is a finished hat, a clean shoe, a ring.
   First second: an iron measuring machine comes down on a living head; a shoe in a rock of mud; a bead of gold beside a filthy carpet. It works in every language; sales are not published.
   Blade-Hats: 156,800,000 views (o-to7-002) and 96,300,000 views (o-to7-003). The Shoelada: 820,600 views, 34.1 times (o-to7-012). TraxNYC, gold out of an old carpet: 5,500,000 views (o-tec6-026). NAJE, drawers of small things: 3,700,000 views, 399.6 times (o-to8-015).
7. **GREW THE ACCOUNT** — The founder, unknown, shows the one movement that makes the thing different; her next films drew at least twice the views. A maker explains a secret of her craft.
   First second: face at the propped phone and the reason in one sentence; then one step back and the hand does it.
   The Clear Republic: 11,900,000 views on an account whose middle film has 1,260; the next 15 films x14.6 (o-to6-023). Its second film x2.22 (o-to6-025). nina, "There's a ring that every goldsmith has to make": x3.1 (o-to8-017, not for showing).
   **SEEN** — the same shape by a man: EliseDaniel, two bag halves become one: 1,200,000 views, 516.1 times (o-to7-004).
8. **KEPT PAYING** — The trouble of one kind of buyer or the cloth itself, seen at once. Their owners kept these running or spent most; no sales figure is published.
   First second: a fitting-room curtain flung open on a shirt that does not fit; a slit that opens on a pair of jeans; a wide gown turning once.
   Under510, clothes for short men: 616 days (o-meta4-013). EDWIN jeans: highest spend tier, 55% still watching at 2 s (o-tta1-001). Royals Fabric, a modest pleated gown: highest spend tier (o-tta1-007).
   **CLICKS** — NNormal, a hole in a shoe and "at first it made me really mad": 594 days, its owner pays for clicks (o-meta4-015).

## What it depends on
- A thing that changes in one movement (bag, reversible coat, repair) → 7, the founder and a phone. A hidden quality (leather, stitching, watch) → 4.
- Made to measure, dear → 6: the instrument at the customer, silence, the finished piece last. A name already known → 1, with a photographer.
- A web shop with many articles and ready buyers → 2 and 3 first: counted tests, stills, no model needed. One kind of buyer with one trouble → 8.
- Modest clothing: the cloth turning (o-tta1-007), clothes laid out (o-platform-013), the owner in her aisle: MERRACHI, 942,000 views, 32.3 times, seen, not sold (o-to7-014).
- The methods behind: the thing alone for a wordless still (m-087), the product that looks like no other (m-009), one real customer's sentence (m-022).
