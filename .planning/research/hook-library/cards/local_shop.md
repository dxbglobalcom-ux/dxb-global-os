## What won in this trade, and why
Roles: SOLD = a published sales result. CLICKS = answered, clicked, left a name. SEEN = strangers watched, unpaid. GREW THE ACCOUNT = later films drew twice the views. Most SOLD figures here are the owner's own, told to a newspaper.

1. **SOLD** — The child or the owner shows the empty shop, one sentence, no request. People supply the help themselves. It is said once, and only when it is true.
   First second: the empty room or the back of the one who waits. Taco-Bout-Joy's: "It breaks my heart to see my mom watching the door", from 10 to 20 customers a day to a line outside the next morning, 58.1 million views (o-ad5-006). Lee's Noodle House: the room full for weeks, revenue up 30-40% (o-ad6-022). Billy's Donuts, a son's post: sold out within hours (o-ad6-025).
   Owner himself, three facts: "I'm 19 years old … I saved up my money", Detroit Loves Tacos 2: selling out nearly every night, no till figures (o-ad5-009). A bookshop's "Not a single book sold today… £0.00": £1,000 of orders overnight (o-ad6-024).
2. **SOLD** — The surprising price over the full plate, or the price on a coupon with a last day. It is the whole offer in three words.
   First second: food and price, nothing else. "Cendol Harga RM1", a stall in Kuala Lumpur filmed by a man with a large following: about 5,000 customers a day, daily profit from RM800 to almost RM10,000 (o-ad3-022).
   Print, a bicycle in a coupon, "$68.88—Reg. $89.95 … Limit 1 per customer": all sold the first afternoon, over $14,000 from a $480 advert (o-caples-051).
3. **SOLD** — The maker's own hands and one true reason. He is plainly no salesman, and the object is good to look at.
   First second: the maker and the thing in his hands. Sparta Candle Co., an old man with a soap with peach slices made in honour of his wife: 9 million views; in eight months of the series sales tripled (o-ad3-021). Hello Sweets, packing one customer's order without a word: an order every minute, the shop closed to restock (o-uzman-011).
4. **SOLD** — A judge who pays himself tastes it. Praise is not certain, so people wait for the first bite.
   First second: a calm face and bags still closed. Keith Lee at Southern Taste Seafood, "Today I spent $450 on the burger": 900 per cent more sales, the counter is not named (o-ad5-013). At Frankensons Pizzeria: sold out overnight, 2-3 hour waits for days (o-ad6-023). He has millions of followers; that did part of it.
5. **SOLD** — To customers and visitors you already have: an exact fact, a clock or the crowd. Only this shop could know it.
   First second: the line. "Your watch has not been here for cleaning for twenty-three months", a watch repairer's letter: business from over 50 percent of those written to (o-klasik-102).
