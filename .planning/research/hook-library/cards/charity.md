# Charities and good causes — what won here (25 November 2026)
96 winners of this trade in the library: 53 sold · 29 brought clicks or names · 7 seen by strangers, unpaid · 5 kept paying for · 2 remembered (a winner may hold two roles); 5 films on this machine, 4 may be shown to anybody.
## What won in this trade, and why
Read this first: most measured winners are the words of a page, a banner, an e-mail or a letter; films with money counted are few (finding 7).
1. **SOLD** (page, banner). One person asks politely, like a neighbour. It is read as a request from a human, so people read on. First second: a soft half sentence or a face with "please read"; nothing sold.
   "If it's not too much to ask…": 0.10% to 0.15% of 282,562 [o-abarsiv-018] and 0.74% to 1.4% at a second charity [o-abarsiv-019]. "Please read: A personal appeal": more gifts in 6 of 6 pairs [o-abarsiv-072].
   Never close the door after a sign-up: "could we take a moment to ask you something?" 1.6% to 2.8% givers [o-abarsiv-047].
2. **SOLD** (letter, e-mail, page). One being or a small number that can be pictured: a name, a child, a few couples. He sees whom his money reaches. First second: the name and the face, or the small count.
   "Sushila worked as a school teacher…" with her photograph: 0.36% gave against 0.11% [o-davranis-044]; "Allow 36 More Couples" 1.2% to 2.4% [o-abarsiv-123]; "send a kid to camp" 0.01% to 0.03% [o-abarsiv-124].
   **CLICKS**, same shape: "Sponsor this child right now" 0.39% to 1.3% of 682,978 [o-abarsiv-032]; "Give a child a childhood!" 5.9% to 8.0% [o-abarsiv-044].
3. **SOLD** (banner, e-mail). A tiny sum each, and an end in sight. Anybody can do it and it soon stops. First second: the sum and the end in one sentence.
   "If everyone reading this donated $5, we would only have to fundraise for one day a year": 1,041 gifts against 579, average gift lower [o-davranis-097]. "With a gift of $10 or more" 0.04% to 0.07% of people who never gave [o-abarsiv-015].
   "just a few gifts short" 0.07% to 0.11% [o-abarsiv-029]; "$5 worth of truth" asked of article readers 0.01% to 0.06% [o-abarsiv-012].
4. **SOLD** (letter, envelope). Remove what he fears before he says it: overheads, endless asking, is it any good. First second: that one sentence, alone.
   "to cover all the overhead costs": 8.55% gave against 3.36% [o-davranis-049]. "Make one gift now and we'll never ask for another donation again": 0.66% against 0.34%, $152,928 against $71,566 [o-davranis-050].
   Naming the known foundation that doubles his gift: +51% money per letter [o-davranis-051].
5. **SOLD** (letter, e-mail, banner). It is about him: his name, his town, his last gift, his run of giving. The eye finds what is its own. First second: that word above everything.
   "Previous Gift: <date>" 7.59% against 6.33% [o-davranis-065]; "[City] Community Drive" 5.51% against 4.12% [o-davranis-066]; "Dear David" 12% against 5% [o-davranis-003]; "Don't Break the Streak" 0.49% to 0.80% [o-abarsiv-028].
6. A real thing falls out of the envelope, the first piece of the cause or of the act. The hand has started before the head decides. First second: the thing itself, no words needed.
   **SOLD**: a cardboard teepee in a clear box, 600 sent, 400 bought tickets or gave, $88,000 net [o-ad1-042].
   **CLICKS**: a real dollar bill, better than 90 percent response from 175,000 letters, about $270,000 [o-schwartz-010]; a ripped exercise book 10.04 per cent [o-klasik-136]; a tea bag £56,000 [o-klasik-138]; cake sprinkles 4.2% [o-ad1-037].
7. **SOLD** (film). A face in the first second, and a form that breaks the pity he expects or makes him wait. First second: a child behind birthday candles; a bald child's back in a locker-room light; two words alone, "Every hour".
   Save the Children, one second a day of one girl: online donations +93% [o-satan-107]. SickKids VS: $57.9m in three months [o-satan-081]. ASPCA: about $30m [o-satan-080]. Medair, face and request inside three seconds: 48,000 USD in four weeks [o-platform-041].
8. **KEPT PAYING** (feed ads; no money figure). One child, one animal or one finished well, close, unexplained. Proof stands before the request. First second: the living being or the running water, one unchanged small caption.
   Three wells with three donors' names, 231 days [o-meta5-023]; a smiling dog with one bandaged leg, 202 days [o-meta5-025]; "a child who shares a birthday with you", 219 days [o-meta5-022].
   One case: **CLICKS**, street signs with the letters A, B and O gone, over 30,000 new blood donors registered in 10 days [o-ad4-029].

## What it depends on
- Page or banner people already see: findings 1 and 3, words only, testable in a week.
- Own past givers: finding 5 (their date, their name); they are warm, so the object of finding 6 pays too.
- Cold strangers in a feed: finding 8, one being and no explanation; a local cause: name the town.
- Doubted charity: finding 4 first. Unknown name: show the finished well, the one animal, the true small number.
- A famous house with a crew: finding 7, break the pity; a small house with a phone: one locked shot of one being.
## What is thin
- Only six films of the trade lie on this machine, and only one of them is tied to givers counted; two of the five shown come from near trades.
- The big sold films (Save the Children, SickKids, ASPCA) are described, not on this machine; they are dramatic and some frighten children.
- Very many rows are Wikipedia and one American testing house (NextAfter); small charities outside the English-speaking world are few.
