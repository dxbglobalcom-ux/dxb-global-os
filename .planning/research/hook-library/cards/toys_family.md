## What it depends on
- Result seen at once (moves, grows, pours, climbs): show it in the first second, findings 4 to 6. Result later (sleep, behaviour, learning): the parent's exact wish with a free thing, findings 2 and 8.
- Cheap and impulsive: finding 4 or 6. Dear or a safety matter (car seat, buggy, building panels): the proof in the hands, finding 5.
- Unknown maker: finding 7, his own hands and voice; a known toy with a hidden history: the old maker's face, finding 7.
- A shop without products of its own: its staff and the toy of the season, finding 1 (o-ad5-022, x-tec6-060).
- Phone camera is enough for 1, 2, 6, 7 and 8. Children appear with their parents' leave; hands alone also won (o-meta5-009, o-to10-017).
