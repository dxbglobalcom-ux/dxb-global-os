## What it depends on
- The trick is seen at once (folds, lights, snaps, prints): 1 and 5. Not seen at once (sound, strength, software): a test (3), or the doubt and one fact.
- Cheap present bought on the spot: 4, 5, 6. Dear and thought over: 1 as Bose did it, 2 and a plain price.
- A new kind of thing nobody knows: 1 and 2, won mostly by new products of small makers; the phone's and the mask's makers brought followers with them. A known name: customers' own films (8).
- Phone camera: 2, 6 and 8 need no crew; 5 needs clean light and one cut per move; 1 as Bose did it needs a crew.
- Pre-order or crowdfunding: for the pen the press worked beside the film (o-ad3-015); plan page, press and film together.
- Big views come to reviewers (7); a maker who wants orders starts from 1 to 4 and lends his product to a tester.
