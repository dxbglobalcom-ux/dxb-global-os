# Health and dental (dentist, optician, clinic, physio, remedies) — what won here (25 November 2026)
91 winners of this trade in the library: 28 sold · 25 brought clicks or names · 21 seen by strangers, unpaid · 16 kept paying for · 3 made the account grow · 1 remembered (a winner may hold two roles); 33 films on this machine, 25 may be shown to anybody.
## What won in this trade, and why
Roles: SOLD = a published sales or response result. KEPT PAYING = the owner paid for it for months or years, no sales figure. CLICKS = opened, clicked, left a name. SEEN = strangers watched, unpaid. GREW THE ACCOUNT = later films drew twice the views.

1. **SOLD** — Speak of the treatment as already the patient's. People fetch what is theirs sooner than they buy what is offered.
   First second: a phone message, nothing else. "A flu shot is waiting for you at Walmart" was the best of 22 messages: +2.9 points over 29.4% with no message, 689,693 people (o-davranis-031). "[First name], a vaccine has just been made available to you … Claim your dose" brought +94.84% appointments against no text at UCLA Health, 93,354 people (o-davranis-048).
   The same shape also drew answers (**CLICKS**): "You have been selected for a FREE doctor's appt." 1.4% said yes against 1.0% for the plain offer, City of New Orleans (o-davranis-024).
2. **SOLD** — One exact figure in the first line, where the trade usually uses a fine adjective. The reader can check it, so he believes it.
   First second: the figure itself. "Dental Plans for $8.33 a month. Acceptance Guaranteed." +72.76% conversion, best of six headlines (o-chatgpt-001); "Over 55,000 Dental Care Providers" +26.41%, second of the six, same site (o-abarsiv-108).
   Another seller: "Not attending costs NHS £160 approx." cut missed appointments to 8.4% from 11.1%; the same line without the sum did less (o-davranis-040). Cheap or fixed price: lead with the price. Dear: lead with another exact figure.
3. **SOLD** — Let him test himself. Nobody can smell his own breath or hear what he mishears; the test makes a hidden trouble his own.
   First second: the test, already going on. Orabrush, "How to Tell When Your Breath Stinks", a young man in a white lab coat with a spoon: about 900,000 views, roughly 10,000 sold in six weeks, one film of about $500 (o-uzman-088). Specsavers lets a famous song be sung wrong: hearing-test bookings +66% against a target of 5% (o-ad4-003).
   In print: "Do you recognize the seven early warning signs of high blood pressure?" sold more than 20,000 copies of a $4.95 book in about 10 runs (o-tec5-004). Works for small unknown sellers; the test must be honest.
4. **SOLD** — The one who knows, with the proof in his hand. A white coat is a claim; a white coat doing something is evidence.
   First second: a named professional at his place; by second two the proof. Dr. Best presses a brush into a tomato and the skin holds: share in Germany from 6% to over 40%, 1988 to 2000, the whole brand (o-satan-109). Sensodyne opens on one quoted sentence, then the real dentist who said it: UK use 5% to 23%, whole brand, ten years (o-satan-112).
   Words only (**CLICKS**): "When Doctors Have Headaches, What Do They Do?" won by 71% (o-schwartz-029).
5. **KEPT PAYING** — For dear dental work: show the old nuisance as an object. Two different sellers paid for the same picture, a denture let go into a glass of water; no unkind word is needed.
   First second: a still jar of water, then a hand and the denture sinking. Nuvia: 387 days, four copies, 16th by impressions (o-meta1-012). Palmdale: 996 days, the jar at 1.8 s after the dentist's first words "You don't have to settle for traditional dentures" (o-meta3-021).
   Beside it, no word at all: a gap in a row of teeth and a screw that slowly closes it, Leytonstone Dental Centre, 1825 days, 2nd by impressions, "from £800" in the text (o-meta1-011). A result that comes later or is feared can still be seen at once this way.
6. **CLICKS** — The patient's own words for the trouble, not the doctor's. He recognises himself before he has decided to listen.
   First second: the sentence as a patient says it. "If you think you need rehab you do", taken from a patient's book review: over 400% more clicks and 26% more leads on the next page, Beachway (o-abarsiv-091). "Get Relief for Your Weekly Migraines" +331% (o-abarsiv-142). "Avoid open back surgery" 47% more conversions (o-abarsiv-151). All three are for urgent trouble.
7. **SEEN** — Make the hidden damage visible, step by step, with nothing ugly in it. He wants it to stop and measures himself against it.
   First second: the harmless thing whole, then the first spot. Oasis Dental Studio's drawn tooth: 5.2 million views on a channel whose usual film has 546 (o-y3-027). Doctor Eye Health puts us behind the windscreen at night, every lamp a red streak: 3.2 million views, 27,900 shares (o-to5-022).
8. **SEEN**, in two cases also **GREW THE ACCOUNT** — The professional gives a verdict on what everybody owns or does.
   First second: his face, his coat, and the known thing. "Podiatrist Rates Shoes": a logo pops up, he grimaces before he speaks: 8.2 million views, usual film 1,376 (o-to5-005). The optician's wheel of lines you test your eyes with: 2.3 million views, and the median of the next 15 films was 34,800 views against 11,700 before (o-to5-014). A dentist holding two smiles of a 30-year-old, flossed and not: 4.2 million views, later films x3.1; take the shape with real photos of the client (o-to5-007).

## What it depends on
- Result seen at once (gap closed, denture replaced, eye test): show the old thing and the new one, findings 5 and 8. Result unseen (decay, pressure, hearing): make it visible or testable, findings 3 and 7.
- Dear and thought over (implants, braces): the object of the nuisance and a calm way out, finding 5. Cheap or fixed price (plan, check-up): the exact figure, finding 2.
- Urgent or painful: the patient's own words, no puzzle, finding 6. Existing patients: a message that says it is already theirs, finding 1.
- Unknown practice: self-test and drawn damage worked from accounts of a few thousand followers (o-to5-014, o-y3-027). A song or a famous voice needs a known name and the right to use it (o-ad4-003).
- Phone camera is enough for findings 3, 7 and 8 and for the dentists' own films; finding 4 and the talking objects need a crew or a good studio.
- Every medical sentence must be true of this client and checked by him; the pictures of teeth must be his own patients', with their written leave.
## What is thin
- Only one film on this machine stands behind a published sale of a clinic's service (o-ad4-003); the dental films are paid-for or watched, without patient numbers.
- Most of the SOLD and CLICKS rows are words: text messages, landing pages, letters, print; several are public health causes, not shops.
- Hospitals: one campaign with figures, in print (o-caples-002); cosmetic clinics, pharmacies and laboratories have no film with a sales figure.
