# Books, courses, schools and teachers — what won here (25 November 2026)
175 winners of this trade in the library: 87 sold · 42 brought clicks or names · 38 seen by strangers, unpaid · 9 kept paying for · 1 remembered (a winner may hold two roles); 39 films on this machine, 30 may be shown to anybody.
## What won in this trade, and why
Read this first: the **SOLD** winners of this trade are mostly print, letters and envelopes; the film winners are mostly **SEEN**. Films that really sold a counted number of books exist, and they are finding 2.
1. **SOLD** (print, mail). The title says in everyday words what the reader will get or be able to do. He stops because it is about him and needs no guessing. First second: only the words, with the gain in them.
   One publisher (Haldeman-Julius) only changed titles: "How to Argue Logically" went to 30,000 copies a year; "Einstein's Theory of Relativity Explained" 15,000 to 42,000 [o-schwartz-017, o-klasik-019].
   Other sellers: "How to fix cars—quickly, easily, right", orders up 20 percent with the easy word [o-caples-071]; "How to Make Anybody Like You!", repeated nineteen times with no drop [o-schwartz-002].
2. **SOLD** (phone film). Somebody else films the unnoticed author in a true moment. The viewer wants to be the one who stops and buys. First second: a wide room, one small person alone, one line of text, quiet music.
   "my dad spent 14 years writing a book": over 100,000 copies in about five weeks, 58.3m views [o-ad5-002]. The man nobody stops for in a bookshop: book sold out online, 81.8m views [o-ad3-010].
   The "defeated" author in a supermarket: about 150 copies sold before the film, No. 1 on Amazon after it, 26m views [o-ad5-004]. It must be true, happen unasked, and the person filmed must be glad of it.
3. **SOLD** (print). A small human story with a turn: first embarrassed, then admired. He stops because the line ends before the end. First second: one scene everybody fears (party, restaurant, dinner).
   "They laughed when I sat down at the piano": profitable in every size from full page to one inch [o-caples-101]; the French-course twin "They grinned when the waiter spoke to me in French" also sold [o-caples-102].
   "Again She Orders – A Chicken Salad, Please" sold an etiquette book, two million copies in all [o-klasik-127]. Film of this shape is not in the library for this trade.
4. The reader tests himself. He cannot leave before he knows whether he is right. First second: the riddle itself, large, solvable by looking, and silence for his answer.
   **SOLD** (print): "What Do You Know?" 45,500 against 14,500 copies a year [o-klasik-036]. **SOLD**: Cain's Jawbone, "figure out the six killers": reprints of 10,000 and 70,000 copies ordered after a 6.7m-view film [o-ad5-014].
   **CLICKS** (print): "Can you spot these 7 common decorating sins?" best by 250 percent [o-sugarman-047]; "Do You Make These Mistakes in English?" near three times better, ran more than 40 years [o-klasik-124].
   **SEEN** (film): "which letter of the alphabet is a person?" 1,800,000 views, 27,600 shares [o-to9-009]; "Could you teach 1/3 x 1/2?" 3,100,000 views, 3,701 comments [o-to9-015]; British or American? 13000000 views [o-y1-030].
5. **SOLD** (envelope, catalogue). Half-told useful facts: he is told that the answer exists and on which page. First second: three or four such lines on the outside, each touching another everyday worry.
   Boardroom's Book of Secrets: over 20 million pieces mailed, over 500,000 copies [o-schwartz-044]; Haldeman-Julius "The Truth About Patent Medicine": sales more than tripled [o-schwartz-019].
6. **CLICKS** (page, e-mail, ad). For a free book or course: say "get" or "activate", and name what is inside. It reads as receiving, not as work. First second: headline and button saying the same gain.
   "Get your FREE copy … and learn how to" 38.2% to 46.7% [o-abarsiv-009]; "Activate your free course now" 19.9% to 25.1% [o-abarsiv-005]; "Generate breakthrough ideas" 47.2% to 51.1% [o-abarsiv-021].
   **SOLD** by the same shape: button "Get Your Resource For a Gift of Any Amount Here" 0.76% to 0.90% givers, 163,961 visitors [o-abarsiv-016].
7. **SEEN** (film). The skill is done live by the learner or the hand, and it could go wrong. He waits to see if it works. First second: the act is already running, no greeting, no name.
   A four-year-old reads card by card: 5600000 views, 134.9 times the median [o-y2-001]. Panda falls on slow piano fingers: 12,300,000 views [o-to9-019]. Word code turns into a picture: 7,100,000 views, 55,400 shares [o-to9-011].
   The office version, the slow way first and then one quick move: "POV: Your boss thinks you enter names like this" 8,100,000 views [o-to9-024]; "DON'T DO THIS" 5700000 views [o-y1-032].
8. **KEPT PAYING** (feed ads; no sales figure). The seller stands in the result, or uses the skill on the viewer. He sees the proof before the offer. First second: something impossible, or the wished place, already there.
   Fake-cliff effect for an effects course, 212 days, 5 ads [o-mb2-002]; exam coach answering a question sticker in front of Lake Louise, 878 days [o-meta3-022]; a boy at his workbook among alpacas for a home school, 339 days [o-mb3-010].

## What it depends on
- Cheap book, bought at once: findings 1 and 5 on the cover, shop page and mail; finding 2 only if a true, unasked moment exists.
- Dear course, thought over: findings 3 and 4 in print and letters; on film finding 8, result first, offer later.
- Skill seen at once (music, reading, office tricks): finding 7, phone camera, one shot, the pupil does it; parents' consent for a child.
- Skill seen much later (exam, language, business): finding 4, let him test himself and answer inside the film.
- Unknown name: findings 2, 4 and 7 all won for unknown sellers. People already on your page or list: finding 6.
## What is thin
- No film in the library has a counted number of COURSE places or pupils sold; the course films are views or running time. The counted book films were unplanned and cannot be ordered.
- The print and mail cases are old; many are one publisher's yearly counts before and after, not same-day splits.
- Schools that sell places (nursery, private school, university) have almost no paid winners here.
