# The shelves of the hook library (2026-11-25)

A shelf says **why the person stops**, never how the sentence is built. One main shelf per row (`shelf`); a second one
(`shelf2`, else "") only when the opening would lose half its force without it. Two fit? Take the one a rival would have to
steal to get the same effect. Read the opening AND the picture. Use the keys exactly as written.

## SHOW — he sees it with his own eyes
- `test` — the claim is tried in front of you and could fail: torture test, drop test, blind test, instrument, "try it at home", live comparison on camera. Not a finished result (→ before_after).
- `before_after` — the change or the result stands first: before/after, transformation, "ordered vs got", one person two lives, result then how.
- `versus` — two things side by side, the eye chooses: us/them, right/wrong, old/new, two equal people who part, "which of these".
- `in_use` — the product itself, alone, in its place or at work: its sound, its motion, its making, its packing, being eaten; a satisfying process. No trial, no change, no story.
- `spectacle` — something rarely seen: impossible act, record, giant or tiny scale, one-take craft, chain reaction, stunt.

## CURIOSITY — a gap stays open
- `odd_detail` — one thing in the picture or the sentence is left unexplained: strange object, odd word, hidden product, reaction shown and its cause hidden, closed box.
- `unfinished` — an event or a sentence broken off before it ends: begins in the middle, cliffhanger, to be continued, half a story.
- `insider` — knowledge he is not meant to have: the truth about, what they do not tell you, what experts do for themselves, secrets, inside the minds of, what never to buy.
- `odd_fact` — one exact surprising fact or number carries it: "at 60 miles an hour", a count you can picture, a measured truth. Not a buyer's result (→ customer), not how many buy (→ crowd).
- `puzzle` — he has something to solve or to test on himself: quiz, spot the mistake, tick-list, "does yours pass", picture riddle.
- `list` — a counted list is the promise: 7 ways, 161 patterns, numbered myths, top ten.

## TROUBLE — he recognises his own trouble
- `trouble` — the pain itself, shown or said in the sufferer's words: symptom, chore, embarrassing moment, damage close-up, private annoyance, "has this happened to you?"
- `mistake` — something he does is wrong or harms him: are you making these mistakes, you are doing it wrong, stop doing X, the harmless thing that harms.
- `warning` — a danger or a loss he does not see: hidden cost, what you lose each day, before you buy X, signs to watch for, what happens if you do nothing.
- `enemy` — a villain or an old way is attacked and left behind: never the old way again, throw away X, rename the rival thing, the germ.
- `objection` — his own doubt or excuse is said first, then answered: no time, too expensive, "does it really work?", "I thought it was a scam".

## GAIN — he sees what he gets
- `offer` — price, free, discount, gift, bundle, trial or guarantee IS the opening.
- `plain_gain` — the benefit or the thing itself in plain words: how to …, get …, the exact name, the plain title that beat the clever one, what the click gives.
- `fast_easy` — the result with its small cost: in 10 minutes, in one evening, without X, one small change, no skill needed.
- `news` — new, first, at last, announcing, a discovery, an event.
- `wish` — the longed-for moment or self is put before him: feast, home, holiday, pride, reward, the happy scene.
- `yours` — already his, reserved, chosen, invited, nearly finished: claim, activate, one step left.
- `deadline` — a true limit: date, last ones, a price rise with its reason, a fresh-start day.

## TRUST — he believes a person
- `story` — a scene with a person begins: they laughed, a small true story, a serial, overheard talk, two brothers.
- `customer` — a buyer's words or a buyer's result: review line, real customer to camera, a case with exact figures.
- `maker` — the owner, inventor or one named person speaks or writes to him: founder says name and price, signed letter, handwritten note, sender's name.
- `expert` — a doctor, tradesman, scientist, professional or laboratory speaks or chooses.
- `borrowed` — strength taken from something already known: famous face, film, sport, song, fairy tale, award, big client, the news or trend of the day.
- `crowd` — how many people: most people here, join 172,783, best seller, people like you, everyone is asking.
- `honest` — the seller says the awkward truth about himself: flaw, second place, true reason of the sale, open books, worst reviews, reasons not to buy.
- `ask` — a modest human request or thanks: please, a favour, a small sum, if everyone gave a little, thank you.

## ADDRESS — it is about him
- `call_out` — says whom it is for: trade, place, age, owner of X, "to people who …", "if you …"; or the picture of his own trade, pet or street.
- `question` — a question whose honest answer pulls him in; not a riddle (→ puzzle), not a pain (→ trouble).
- `personal` — his own name, record, purchase, town, date, what he did last time.
- `dare` — challenge, wager, forbidden thing, "if you have the nerve", a command that provokes, "do not skip".

## PLAY — he feels something
- `joke` — humour, twist, absurdity, parody, pun, serious film with a silly mistake, expectation turned over.
- `character` — a child, an animal, a mascot, a talking thing or a recurring figure is what stops him.
- `jolt` — fear, grief, tenderness, shock or silence comes before the message.
- `native` — it looks like what stands around it: phone note, message, reply to a comment, news page, street interview, skit, article.

## PLACES with their own rules
- `button` — the words on a button, a form or the last step.
- `object` — a real thing in the hand or the envelope: coin, sample, torn toy, name label.

## Two more labels on every row
- `proves` — **what the evidence really proves**: `words` = the rivals differed only in the opening (headline, first line, subject line, button, first seconds), the rest was the same · `whole` = the whole ad, film, letter or page won, or was paid for or kept running; the opening is its first part · `brand` = only a brand's sales or growth over a time is known; the ad was one part of it.
- `fits` — who can use it: `any` · `known` = it works because the brand, face or song is already known, or it needs a big production · `new` = best for a seller nobody knows yet.
