**1. "Sorry. No temporary crowns."**

- **Picture (0–3 s):** At 0 s the small tooth-white ceramic block is held between Dr Wong's gloved thumb and finger, right at the lens and sharp. Behind it, out of focus, is the milling machine's window with water spraying. At 1 s the focus pulls to his face, which is calm and a little apologetic. From 2 to 3 s he tips his head toward the machine behind him, and through its window a crown is being carved under water. It is one frame with one focus pull.
- **On screen:** Sorry. No temporary crowns.
- **Heard:** Dr Wong, low and dry, no music: "I'm afraid we don't do temporary crowns."
- **Caption, first line:** Broken tooth, Bracknell? Your real crown is made here the same day. From £750.
- **The seven rooms:**
  - SHOW: the picture carries it. The ceramic block is at the lens and the mill is carving behind him.
  - CURIOSITY: the on-screen line and his voice carry it. A dentist apologising for refusing something leaves the question "why?" open.
  - TROUBLE: the words carry it. Anyone who has had a crown remembers the temporary and the second visit, so it is named without showing pain.
  - GAIN: the caption carries it: the real crown, the same day, from £750.
  - TRUST: the picture and his voice carry it. Dr Wong's own face and voice, in his own surgery.
  - ADDRESS: the caption carries it: "Broken tooth, Bracknell?"
  - PLAY: the words carry it. Bad news delivered deadpan turns out to be the good news.
  - Missing: none. The one limit is that the TROUBLE only lands with viewers who already know what a temporary crown is.

**2. The oven-door crouch**

- **Picture (0–3 s):** Seen side-on, Dr Wong in scrubs crouches at eye level with the mill's small window, chin on his fists, completely still. This is the pose everyone knows from someone watching a cake rise. At 1–2 s, through the window, the water jets hit the white block as the burr cuts. At 3 s he still hasn't moved. His face and the window are in one frame.
- **On screen:** Your crown. About five minutes.
- **Heard:** First the real whine and hiss of the mill. Then Dr Wong whispers, without looking away: "Nobody touch the door."
- **Caption, first line:** Broken tooth? Dr Wong mills your crown here in Bracknell while you wait. No temporary, no metal.
- **The seven rooms:**
  - SHOW: the picture carries it. The block is being carved under water, with its real sound.
  - CURIOSITY: the picture carries it. A dentist kneeling, spellbound, at a machine is left unexplained.
  - GAIN: the on-screen words carry it: a crown in about five minutes.
  - TRUST: the picture and the caption carry it. Dr Wong is the person, named.
  - ADDRESS: the caption carries it: "Broken tooth?" and Bracknell.
  - PLAY: the picture and the whisper carry it. It parodies the well-known oven-watching pose and is played completely straight.
  - Missing: TROUBLE is only weakly present, through "no temporary" in the caption.
- **Methods used:** m-072, m-047, m-031, m-013, m-101, m-023

**3. Six hundred, by weight**

- **Picture (0–3 s):** At 0 s Dr Wong stands by the dental chair holding a thick ream of paper in both hands. It is the practice's real five-star Google reviews, printed with names hidden, and stars are visible on the top sheet. At 1–2 s he drops it onto the dental chair with a thud. At 3 s he looks at the camera with a faint smile.
- **On screen:** 600 five stars. Some were scared.
- **Heard:** The thud of the stack, then Dr Wong, dry: "Six hundred. A few of them were scared of me."
- **Caption, first line:** Afraid of the dentist, and now a broken tooth? One visit, crown made here, Saturdays, free parking, Bracknell.
- **The seven rooms:**
  - SHOW: the picture carries it, but only weakly. The count becomes a weight you can see, landing on the chair people fear. The product itself is not seen.
  - CURIOSITY: the words carry it. What did he do to win five stars from scared people?
  - TROUBLE: the words, the picture and the caption carry it. The fear of the dentist is named, and the chair is in the frame.
  - GAIN: the caption carries it: one visit, Saturdays, free parking.
  - TRUST: the picture and the words carry it. Dr Wong himself, plus 600 real reviews.
  - ADDRESS: the caption carries it: "Afraid of the dentist…", plus Bracknell.
  - PLAY: the voice carries it. "Scared of me" is self-mockery about himself as the dentist, said deadpan.
  - Missing: none. SHOW is the weakest room, because the crown and the mill are absent.
- **Methods used:** m-081, m-033, m-124, m-080, m-035, m-004

**Strongest:** No. 1. A single deadpan sentence from Dr Wong holds the twist, the trouble everyone remembers and the gain all at once. The block and the mill show the answer before he says it, and the whole thing reads in half a second with the sound off.
