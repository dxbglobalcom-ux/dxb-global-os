# Brief — short films for one real client

This holding produces high technology, aims at large revenue and will found large companies; its business lines' clients are small, medium and large, up to the biggest clients in the world. Its ad office makes short films for clients in the client's own language, as a monthly package that must bring the client real customers.

## The client (real; facts taken from its own pages — use nothing beyond them)
CrownWood Dental, Bracknell (England), a local dental practice hardly anybody outside the town knows; its own small TikTok and Instagram account. It makes a crown (a new top for a broken tooth) in ONE visit: a small camera films the tooth instead of a mouthful of paste; in seconds a 3D model of the tooth stands on the screen; a milling machine in the practice carves the crown from a small ceramic block in about five minutes under water; it is bonded on the same day, no temporary crown, no metal, the colour of the patient's own teeth. From GBP 750. Led by Dr Wong, more than 25 years a dentist; over 600 five-star Google reviews, several from people who were afraid of the dentist; open on Saturdays; free parking.
Goal: local people with a broken or crowned tooth book a visit. Language: English. Vertical film, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

## Your only tool
`methods-only.jsonl` in this folder: 138 methods — recipes for how an opening is built (`do_film`, `do_words`, why, where it fits). Each has a `shelf` = why a person stops. There are NO examples, on purpose: build every idea from the client's own facts.

## What to do
1. Write 25 rough ideas, quick and varied, each naming its method.
2. Keep your best 5. For each, write the WHOLE film, 20–30 seconds: the opening (0–3 s), what follows second by second, and the ending that tells the viewer what to do. The opening must be understood by a stranger in half a second with the sound off.
3. Rules: commercial; holds a person or raises curiosity; no pity, no agitation, no fake urgency, no fake reviews, no invented patients or numbers; no greeting, no logo first; filmable in a real practice in one day.

## What to return (plain text, only this)
For each of your 5:
- **Method:** id, name, and its shelf
- **Film (20–30 s):** second by second — what is seen, what is heard, any on-screen text (English)
- **Ending:** the last line and what the viewer is asked to do
- **Why it works:** one line
Then one line: your strongest of the 5, and why. Do not write files.
