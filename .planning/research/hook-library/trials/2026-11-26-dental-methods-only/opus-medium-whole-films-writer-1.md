**1**
- **Method:** m-013, "A big job in an exact short time", shelf `fast_easy`
- **Film (24 s):**
  - 0–3 s: Close-up of the milling machine carving a tooth-coloured block under water spray. A large timer at 00:00 starts running. On-screen text: "A new crown. Carved in about 5 minutes." Real machine sound.
  - 3–7 s: Cut to the small camera gliding over a tooth on a model. The 3D tooth appears on the monitor. Text: "No paste. A camera."
  - 7–12 s: Back to the mill, with the timer jumping forward in true elapsed cuts. Text: "Carved here, in the practice."
  - 12–16 s: The chamber opens, and gloved fingers lift out the crown, which matches the shade guide. Text: "No metal. Your tooth's colour."
  - 16–20 s: Dr Wong, half-face to camera, says calmly: "Scanned, made and fitted the same day. No temporary crown." The same words appear as text.
  - 20–24 s: Front door of the practice. Text: "CrownWood Dental, Bracknell · From £750 · Open Saturdays · Free parking."
- **Ending:** "Broken tooth? Book one visit. Tap the link." The viewer is asked to book through the profile link.
- **Why it works:** Two exact numbers, "one visit" and "about 5 minutes", over moving material can be read silently in half a second, and both come from the client's own pages.
