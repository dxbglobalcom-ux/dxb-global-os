# Brief — one hook that carries all seven rooms at once

This holding produces high technology, aims at large revenue and will found large companies; its business lines' clients are small, medium and large, up to the biggest clients in the world. Its ad office makes short films for clients in the client's own language, as a monthly package that must bring the client real customers.

## The client (real; facts from its own pages — use nothing beyond them)
CrownWood Dental, Bracknell (England), a local dental practice hardly anybody outside the town knows. It makes a crown (a new top for a broken tooth) in ONE visit: a small camera films the tooth instead of a mouthful of impression paste; in seconds a 3D model of the tooth stands on the screen; a milling machine in the practice carves the crown from a small tooth-white ceramic block in about five minutes under water; it is bonded on the same day, no temporary crown, no metal, the colour of the patient's own teeth. From GBP 750. Led by Dr Wong, more than 25 years a dentist; over 600 five-star Google reviews, several from people who were afraid of the dentist; open on Saturdays; free parking.
Goal: local people with a broken tooth book a visit. Language: English. Vertical film, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

## The owner's order (verbatim, Turkish): "tek bir şeyde olacak. Hepsi aynı anda."
His correction: AT LEAST FIVE of the seven rooms. The hook — the first 3 seconds — must carry at least five rooms of `shelves.md` AT THE SAME TIME, not one after another (more is better, but never forced at the cost of clarity or strength):
SHOW (he sees it) · CURIOSITY (a gap stays open) · TROUBLE (he recognises his own trouble) · GAIN (he sees what he gets) · TRUST (he believes a person) · ADDRESS (it is about him) · PLAY (he feels something).
A hook has four layers that run at once: the picture, the words on screen (at most 6), the first spoken words or sound, the caption's first line. Use all four so that, together, at least five rooms are present in the same 3 seconds — and the whole still reads in half a second with the sound off. One single, simple picture; not a collage.

## Your tools
`shelves.md` (the 41 shelves in 7 rooms — why a person stops) and `methods-only.jsonl` (138 recipes — how). No examples, on purpose.

## The bar
- Would a stranger who sees it show it to a friend? If not, drop it.
- Lines are sharp, adult and specific — never childish or explanatory ("this block will become a tooth" is the kind of line that fails). No line at all is better than a weak one.
- Commercial; no pity, no agitation, no fake urgency, no invented reviews, patients or numbers; filmable in the real practice in one day.
