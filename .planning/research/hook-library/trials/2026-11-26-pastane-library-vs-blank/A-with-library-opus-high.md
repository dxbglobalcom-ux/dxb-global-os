**Best 5: Gülsüm Pastanesi (Kadıköy)**

---

**1. "Hepsi bu sabah"**
Rooms: CURIOSITY (puzzle, list), SHOW (in_use), PLAY (joke), TRUST (maker), ADDRESS (call_out, question), GAIN (plain_gain). That is 6 rooms.
Methods: m-054 riddle solved by looking, m-005 counted list, m-023 name the place, m-031 maker's hands close, m-035 real workroom, m-038 a joke that carries the product, m-134 the first picture works silent.

- **Hook (0–3 s):** Picture: five trays stand in a row on the counter with no labels: pasta, profiterol, poğaça, çörek, kurabiye. Gülsüm's son's finger passes over them. On screen: "Hangisi bu sabah yapıldı?" First words, from the son: "Kadıköy, bir tahmin: hangisi bu sabah yapıldı?" Then 2 seconds of silence for the viewer's guess.
- **Scenario:**
  - 3–6 s: Gülsüm comes in behind him with one more tray and says with a straight face: "Hepsi." On screen: "Hepsi. Her sabah."
  - 6–20 s: five cuts in the kitchen behind the shop, about 3 s each, with the real sound and the counter "1/5 … 5/5". The knife goes into the cake, chocolate runs over the profiterole, the poğaça tray comes out of the oven, the çörek goes onto the tray, the cookies are laid out by hand.
  - 20–24 s: the son says: "1978'den beri böyle." Gülsüm says: "Yarın sabah yine."
  - 24–30 s: the shop front with the door open. On screen: "Gülsüm Pastanesi · Kadıköy · 07:00–22:00". Spoken: "Sabah yediden akşam ona kadar buradayız. Gelin."
- **Why it works:** The viewer stops to guess, and the answer "hepsi" is itself the promise (all handmade this morning), proven straight away by the five cuts.

**2. "Yarına pasta? Yapamayız."**
Rooms: TRUST (honest, maker), TROUBLE (warning), GAIN (deadline), SHOW (in_use), PLAY (joke: an ad that says no), ADDRESS (call_out). That is 6 rooms.
Methods: m-033 say your weak point first, m-003 tiny skit, m-035 real workroom, m-031 maker's hands, m-050 sentence dealt out in pieces, m-023 name the group.

- **Hook (0–3 s):** Picture: in the kitchen behind the shop, Gülsüm's hands are finishing a celebration cake, filmed close. The son's voice comes from off camera: "Anne, yarına doğum günü pastası istiyorlar." Without looking up, Gülsüm says: "Yapamayız." On screen: "Yarına pasta? Yapamayız."
- **Scenario:**
  - 3–7 s: Gülsüm looks into the lens and says: "Çünkü her pastayı burada, dükkânın arkasındaki mutfakta elimizle yapıyoruz."
  - 7–15 s: her hands at work, close, with the real sound: cream is spread and the decoration goes on. On screen: "Elle. Burada. Her sabah."
  - 15–20 s: the on-screen sentence is dealt out in two pieces: first "Cumartesi kutlama mı?", then on a hard cut "Perşembe söyleyin."
  - 20–25 s: the finished cake turns once on its stand, then the box lid closes.
  - 25–30 s: Gülsüm says: "İki gün önceden söyleyin, gerisini biz düşünürüz. Dükkâna uğrayın, siparişinizi yazalım." On screen: "Kutlama pastası: 2 gün önceden · Gülsüm Pastanesi, Kadıköy".
- **Why it works:** The one limit (order two days ahead) becomes the proof of handwork, and it tells the viewer exactly when to act.

**3. "Kadıköy, 06:59"**
This is the opener of a series with the same line every time.
Rooms: CURIOSITY (unfinished), SHOW (in_use), ADDRESS (call_out), TRUST (maker), GAIN (wish), PLAY (character: a repeated line). That is 6 rooms.
Methods: m-002 begin with a hand already at work, m-104 the middle of a real day, m-012 say what you will do and what is still open, m-049 stop a blink before it happens, m-029 one clear movement, m-023 name the place, m-106 a line people can say back.

- **Hook (0–3 s):** Picture: the first frame is a hot poğaça tray being pulled from the oven, with the tray's real sound. On screen: "Kadıköy, 06:59. Kepenk bir dakika sonra." First words, from the son, with no greeting: "Bir dakikamız var, poğaçalar daha fırında."
- **Scenario:**
  - 3–9 s: the trays come out and Gülsüm fills the vitrine with poğaça and çörek.
  - 9–12 s: outside, a hand rests on the closed shutter and the frame holds for 1 second.
  - 12–15 s: the shutter goes up and light falls on the street. The clock shows 07:00.
  - 15–22 s: the vitrine seen from the street, full: cakes, profiterol, poğaça, çörek, kurabiye.
  - 22–27 s: mother and son behind the counter say the same line that will open every film: "Günaydın Kadıköy. 1978'den beri saat yedide."
  - 27–30 s: on screen: "Gülsüm Pastanesi · 07:00–22:00". Spoken: "İşe giderken uğrayın."
- **Why it works:** A real countdown holds the viewer to the end, and the line repeated every morning becomes the shop's signature in local feeds.
- **Care:** It must really be filmed that morning, at that minute. The poğaça must really be just out of the oven at opening time.

**4. "Anne mi, oğul mu?"**
Rooms: SHOW (versus), PLAY (joke, character), TRUST (maker), CURIOSITY (unfinished), ADDRESS (question), GAIN (plain_gain). That is 6 rooms.
Methods: m-099 two at one start line, m-065 put the oldest of the house before the camera, m-047 the boss is the joke (her unmoved face), m-031 maker's hands, m-113 a question he answers in his head.

- **Hook (0–3 s):** Picture: mother and son side by side at one bench, each with a row of profiterole and a jug of chocolate. On screen labels: "GÜLSÜM · kurucunun kızı" and "OĞLU". A countdown "3-2-1" is already running. The son's first words: "Bu sefer yeneceğim." On screen: "Sizce kim?"
- **Scenario:**
  - 3–15 s: one take from above with the real sound and the timer on screen. Both race to cover their row with chocolate.
  - 15–19 s: the first to finish puts the jug down. Gülsüm says nothing and only gives a straight-faced look.
  - 19–23 s: the son says to the camera: "Tamam… siz onunkini alın."
  - 23–30 s: the winning tray goes into the vitrine. On screen: "Elde yapıldı · Gülsüm Pastanesi · Kadıköy · 07:00–22:00". Gülsüm says: "Gelin, tadına siz karar verin."
- **Why it works:** A real family rivalry is funny, the viewer stays to see who wins, and the handwork is on screen the whole time.
- **Care:** The race must be real and either of them must be able to win. If the son wins, that result is published, and the closing lines are swapped to match.

**5. "İki gün önce sipariş verdi"**
Rooms: CURIOSITY (closed box, unfinished), TRUST (customer, maker), SHOW (before_after: what was ordered against what arrived), GAIN (wish), ADDRESS (call_out), PLAY (jolt: tenderness). That is 6 rooms.
Methods: m-112 open a closed thing slowly, m-049 stop a blink before it happens, m-093 show the true reaction first, m-026 biggest moment first then go back, m-063 do it for one named person, m-001 a real customer's message on screen.

- **Hook (0–3 s):** Picture: at the counter, a real customer's hands lift the lid of a white cake box a crack; the inside is not yet seen. On screen: "İki gün önce sipariş verdi." First words, from Gülsüm off camera: "Açmadan önce… bakalım, istediğiniz gibi mi—" The sentence is cut there.
- **Scenario:**
  - 3–6 s: the lid opens. First comes her face, close, at the first look, and only then the cake.
  - 6–9 s: her own order note or message appears on screen beside the cake: what she asked for next to what she got.
  - 9–19 s: on screen "İki gün önce…" and a jump back: quick cuts in the kitchen behind the shop of Gülsüm's hands making this very cake.
  - 19–24 s: back at the counter, the box closes and is handed over.
  - 24–30 s: Gülsüm looks into the lens: "Kutlamanız mı var? İki gün önceden gelin, birlikte karar verelim." On screen: "Doğum günü ve kutlama pastası · 2 gün önceden sipariş · Gülsüm Pastanesi, Kadıköy".
- **Why it works:** The reaction plus "what was ordered against what arrived" is the proof a cake buyer needs, and the film walks the viewer through the two-day order.
- **Care:** The customer must be real and agree in writing. The first look must truly be her first. The kitchen footage must be of her own cake.

---

**Strongest: number 2.** It turns the client's only limit (two days ahead) into proof of handwork and tells the viewer exactly what to do and when, which leads straight to cake orders. It also needs no customer consent and is the cheapest to film.
