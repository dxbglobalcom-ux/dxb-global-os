# Write ads for a patisserie
Write the best short-film ad openings (hooks) and scenarios you can for this client. Write 25 rough ideas first, keep the best 5.
## The client (a trial client, invented for this test — use nothing beyond these facts)
Gülsüm Pastanesi, Kadıköy, Istanbul. A family patisserie since 1978, now run by the founder's daughter Gülsüm and her son. Everything is made by hand in the kitchen behind the shop each morning: cakes, profiterole, poğaça, çörek, cookies; birthday and celebration cakes to order (ordered two days ahead). One shop, open 7:00–22:00, a few tables inside. Wants short vertical films (Instagram Reels, TikTok) that bring people from Kadıköy and nearby into the shop and bring cake orders. Language: Turkish (the client's language).

## Return (plain text, only this)
Your best 5. For each:
- **Hook (0–3 s):** what is seen, the words on screen, the first words or sound
- **Scenario (20–30 s):** what happens second by second after the hook, and the ending that tells the viewer what to do
- **Why it works:** one line
Then one line: your strongest, and why. Write the on-screen and spoken words in Turkish. Do not write files.
