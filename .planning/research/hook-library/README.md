## What it already says (the lead's reading of the hands' reports)
1. **A hook is a family, not a mould.** Plain and loud, long and short, question and statement all have winners; what decides is the job (stop, click, buy, remember), the channel and how known the seller is.
2. **Plain beats clever where it was counted.** Every before/after title of Haldeman-Julius gained by being plainer; Schwab's and Caples' pairs agree.
3. **The thing shown beats the thing said** in film: Dr. Best's tomato, Purple's egg, Blendtec, Master Lock, M&S's pudding.
4. **The product must be the striking moment** (Yang 2025; Zhang, Peng & Tan 2026: an impossible clip before the product film raised full watching and buying per view, an odd-looking one lost). Only one experiment, small numbers, its film AI-made.
5. **Early brand costs small brands sales** (System1 2025: challengers −19 % conversion lift, leaders +25 %); entertaining openings suit small brands (2.4×), hard sell suits leaders (3.5×).
6. **Opens and clicks mislead**: fewer opens earned 63 % more revenue; Evian's 50 million views came with falling sales.
7. **The largest proven gain in wording is a writer trained on tested pairs**: +32 % clicks over 36 campaigns and 283 million e-mails; plain ChatGPT added nothing (Angelopoulos, Lee & Misra; one author works for the vendor). This is the base for the scorer and the writer.
8. **Real lifts are modest**: 126 trials of two public units average +8 %; famous psychology tricks mostly did not repeat (list in `hands/davranis.md`).
9. **Winners wear out**: personalisation fell from about +300 % to +30 %; Wikipedia's best appeal lost 3–4× two years later. A library must be refreshed.

- Dropped, sources not credible or not checkable: Hyperbeam (contrast, comparison), Advize, Galvor (start in the middle, acted problem), Haven, Scrptly, Kaufman, OpusClip, Ad Radar, GenSumo, 1of10, Realeyes, Mishra.

- "As a dentist 3.16×", "If you… 1.29×", "words on screen 1.79×": small cells the same evidence contradicts; not proven.
- Meta's paper does not show that the first three seconds drive conversions; Teixeira, Tucker, Molina, Mishra and Packard were misread (`hands/bilimvideo.md` §4, `hands/bilimsoz.md` §4).
- Brand in two seconds: memory for leaders, a cost in sales for small brands.
