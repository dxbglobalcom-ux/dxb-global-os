## 5. Grant package v1 — what and why

**What:** each of the 21 active departments holds the 8 `dxb-mcp/<group>` mcp items (approval, audit, cost, crm, dashboard, memory, queue, registry) = 168 grants.
**Why identity-mirror:** measured 2026-07-17 — all 21 dept profiles carried the identical 21 pinned dxb-mcp tools; the 8 group prefixes cover exactly those 21 (3+2+2+1+1+2+6+4). Mirroring closes "0/N granted" and makes the record governing WITHOUT touching the live surface mid-operation.
**Deliberately NOT done (CEO-visible boundaries, not omissions):**
- **Per-department narrowing** (finance loses registry_create_department, etc.) = a PERMISSION_MODEL/policy-file decision with live-worker blast radius — belongs to a CEO-reviewed policy pass, never a silent 23:00 write. Recommended next: derive per-dept minimal sets from `library_usage_log`/`tool_calls` actuals after 2 weeks of runtime data.
- **Persona grants:** custody (owner_dept) ≠ capability grant; a department "using" its own personas is already modeled by ownership + the personas table. Granting 214 persona items would add 4½ thousand rows of noise with zero enforcement meaning (compiler reads only tool/mcp/skill/plugin).
- **Skill/plugin grants:** the 23 skills + 18 plugins are BUILD-layer assets (Claude Code sessions on the control machine), not company-runtime capabilities — worker SDK sessions never load them (measured: `_skills` consumed nowhere at runtime). Granting them to departments would be a fiction. When runtime skills ship, grants follow reality.
