## 5. The build project

**The honest framing first.** The CEO asked for this "done perfectly". Perfect here does not
mean a large build — it means **a small, legal, measured revenue engine whose first euro is
provable**, because an unprovable euro is worse than none under RULE #0-A. And two boundaries
must be stated before any code, because they are his own law:

- **Islamic boundaries are constitutional and CEO-only.** Whop's live campaign grid visibly
  includes a betting brand (`DONBET` in the frame read above). Any campaign selection must run
  through a halal allowlist decided by the CEO — never a denylist, which is the lesson already
  recorded from the outbox work (2026-07-26).
- **Copyright and platform terms.** Clipping other people's footage is licensed *by the
  campaign*: the source content is supplied for that purpose. Anything outside a campaign's
  supplied material is not ours to cut.

| # | Project | What is built | Owning spec (adaptation — no new spec) | Closes when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P05-1** | **Decide whether we enter, on evidence** | A one-page measurement for the CEO: current live campaigns on Whop, their rates, their categories, and how many pass a halal allowlist. **CEO decision:** enter or not. No account is created before he says so — account creation is an outward action. | `REVENUE` spec + `INTEGRATION-TRACKER` row | The CEO has real numbers and a yes/no, not an argument. |
| **P05-2** | **The objective, before the engine** | If yes: one `objectives` row with a real target, a period, `capital_limit_eur`, `max_loss_eur` and `boundaries_ack` — so the engine is measured from its first hour against the same ledger as everything else. | `REVENUE` spec | `v_objective_progress` shows a live clipping objective with a real gap and run-rate. |
| **P05-3** | **The production line** (CAP-05-C) | A pipeline the Social Media department owns: campaign intake → source download (yt-dlp, already installed) → cut selection → captioning → export per platform → post. Every posted clip writes a row with campaign, platform, post URL and view count, so revenue is reconciled against **measured views**, never estimated. | `SOCIAL_MEDIA` / department spec + `ORCHESTRATOR` | One clip goes end to end and its row carries a real view count from the platform. |
| **P05-4** | **The halal gate** | An allowlist of permitted campaign categories and brands, CEO-owned, enforced at intake — a campaign that is not on the list cannot enter the pipeline, and the refusal is logged. | Governance / boundaries spec | A betting campaign present in the live grid is refused by the gate, with the audit row to show. |
| **P05-5** | **Revenue reconciliation** | Payouts land in `revenue_ledger` against the objective, with `net_unverified` true until the payout is confirmed by the platform. | `REVENUE` spec | `realized_revenue_eur` moves for the first time in the project's history, and the number is verified. |

**Sequencing, stated plainly.** P05-1 is a measurement and can start immediately. P05-2 to P05-5
are a *revenue engine*, which is the CEO's call on priority against the Jarvis work — this
report does not assume it outranks the voice line and does not assume it loses to it either.

## 6. Verdict

**`daha iyisi` — but only if the CEO wants a revenue engine now; the capability gap is small
and the governance gap is the real work.**

- **The market is real and priced.** $1–1.50 per 1,000 views, a two-sided marketplace already
  built (Whop), demand from brands to tourism boards, and a named operator at >$30M top line.
  Nothing here needs inventing.
- **DXB is unusually well-placed on the supply side.** The download/transcribe/archive tooling
  is already installed and was used to produce this very report; there is a Social Media
  department with staff; and the objectives ledger is built to hold exactly this kind of
  per-unit economics with capital and loss limits.
- **What is genuinely missing is not technology.** It is (a) the CEO's yes, (b) a halal
  allowlist — the live campaign grid in this very frame contains a betting brand, and that is a
  constitutional limit, not a preference — and (c) a reconciliation path so the first euro is
  *provable*, because `net_unverified` exists precisely so that we never celebrate a number we
  cannot show.
- **Where we would be better than the people in this video:** they are individuals with
  throughput limits. A holding with a department, a queue, and a governed ledger can run the
  same loop with an audit trail behind every clip and every euro — which is also the only way
  this stays inside the CEO's boundaries.
