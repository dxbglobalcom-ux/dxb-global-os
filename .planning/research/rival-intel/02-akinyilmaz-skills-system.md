## 3. Capabilities — what this system demonstrably does

### 3.0 The shape of it, in one line

He is not running one assistant. He is running **two coding agents side by side on two machines**
(OpenAI Codex desktop and the Claude desktop app), **driving them by voice**, with a **named skill
library** loaded into them, **recurring jobs** that finish on their own clock and announce
themselves, and a **persistent graph memory** he calls the brain of everything he does. The skills
library is also the product he sells access to.

**Every one of the capabilities below is visible in the recording. He has confirmed to the CEO that
his systems are real and live (C), and nothing here is called decorative.**

#### 3.0.1 Voice as the primary interface, not a feature (V, 00:27-01:14)

The Codex desktop app is in a live voice session for 75 of the 143 seconds. What is visible:

- **live incremental transcription** — his words appear in the chat bubble as he speaks;
- **streaming spoken and written reply** — the answer grows word by word;
- **barge-in behaviour** — he remarks in his own instruction *"bu arada konuştuğu zaman, sizle
  konuştuğu zaman duruyor, bu da aklınızda olsun"* (V, 01:04): the assistant stops when spoken to;
- **the agent works while the conversation continues**, and each unit of work carries an elapsed
  badge: **`Worked for 1m 3s`**, **`Worked for 37s`**, **`Worked for 1m 23s`**, **`Worked for 9s`**,
  **`Worked for 10s`**;
- **a bug reported by voice, fixed, and reported back** (V, 00:55-00:57);
- **the browser scrolled by voice** (V, 01:12-01:14).

Honest note in his favour and against it: **their Turkish speech-to-text also mis-hears** — the chat
records "Ticketler bakım" for "Bakın" (V, 00:29). The voice layer is real and it is imperfect, the
same way ours is.

#### 3.0.2 A named skill, announced at the moment it loads (V, 00:46) — **the answer to the CEO's question**

The single most important second of this reel is `00:46`:

> **`🔧 Control In App Browser becerisi okunuyor`**

Three things are true at once and all three are visible:

1. the capability has **a name** (`Control In App Browser`), so it is a discrete, addressable unit;
2. it is **read at the moment it is needed** — not loaded permanently into the prompt;
3. the user is **told which one**, in his own language, inside the conversation.

That is the mechanism. "Yüzlerce yetenek ekledim" means: hundreds of these named units are available
to the agent, and the agent picks one when the job matches.

#### 3.0.3 Recurring jobs that report themselves (V, 00:14-00:21)

The Claude sidebar carries a **`Routines`** section with named standing jobs — `Streamyard reel
auto…`, `Fırsat avcısı günlük`, `Okyanusi daily advisor`, `GOD MODE — 2 yıl a…` — and a notification
card **`GOD MODE Akşam Review · Ready ✓`** proves one of them completed and announced itself without
being asked.

#### 3.0.4 Persistent graph memory (V, 02:12-02:23)

A window titled `Grafik Görünümü` showing a several-thousand-node knowledge graph, which he calls
*"yaptığım bütün işlemlerin beyni"* and claims *"hiçbir bilgiyi unutmuyor"*.

- **What is verified:** the window exists, the graph is dense and clustered, and he ships it as one
  of the downloadable "yetenekler" (V·T).
- **What is not:** which application this is. The Turkish label `Grafik Görünümü` and the window
  chrome are consistent with an Obsidian vault graph view, but the title bar is not legible enough
  to name the product. **U — to verify, the package behind his link would have to be opened.**
- **What is not proven either way:** that the agent *reads* from this graph at run time rather than
  it being a picture of files he has accumulated. He asserts it; the recording does not show a
  retrieval. **U.**

#### 3.0.5 The agent building and serving a saleable artefact from a spoken sentence (V, 01:04-01:16)

From one spoken instruction, in **1 m 23 s**, the agent produced a complete commercial landing page —
hero, live-looking opportunity radar, social proof strip, three-tier pricing in pounds, a roadmap
section — and served it at `127.0.0.1:4173`. The CEO should note the pricing tier design especially:
`£49/ay`, `£99/ay` marked *most chosen*, and a third *by negotiation*.

#### 3.0.6 Two access levels, both without a gate (V, 00:14 · 00:23)

authority over that computer. Machine two runs `5.6 Sol Sınırlı` (limited). **He has an access dial
