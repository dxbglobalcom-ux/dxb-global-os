### 2.2.4 The work surface: Slack, and what its design does for an agent

- **Palette, sampled** (`ads-51.5` crop): sidebar aubergine **`#38263C`–`#493549`**, selected-room
  pill in violet (**`#C48AE0`** mean, peak **`#FFEDFF`**), message ground **`#414041`–`#484343`**,

  reading, one colour for the act**.
- **The agent's message is a document, not a chat line.** Tom's post carries labelled sections —

  match exactly (paths, commands, identifiers), italic sub-headings inside the ads briefing

- **Identity design:** each agent is a **human name with a human portrait and an `APP` badge** —

  card carries one word of role: **`Developer`**.

### 2.2.5 The motion — **UNVERIFIED, and here is why**

Ledger law 8 requires the screen's movement to be timed, and law 10 requires a motion claim to be
measured against a static control. Both were done, and the measurement **fails honestly**:

**The control moves almost as much as the screen, so nothing here can be attributed to the artwork.**
The camera is handheld throughout. **What would be needed:** a screen recording of the same page, or
a tripod shot of the wall — neither exists in this source. **The timing for this exact system is

### Aliveness — how this system is built to live, and what DXB takes

**Premise, not question (law 8): this workspace is live.** What follows is mechanism.

**1 — What runs on its own clock.** Three things move without being asked. **Sarah pulls customer
mail and store reviews** and has drafts waiting before the owner opens the room — at 12:47 and 12:56
two letters are already drafted. **The ads agent runs a daily read** and posts state, recommendations
and an approval list with no prompt in the thread. **Tom works after the green light**: between
12:50 PM and 1:06 PM the thread fills with its own log — `todo`, `patch`, `terminal`, `search_files`
— and finishes with a PR link. The owner's only inputs in 19 minutes were **one sentence** and
**one approval**.

**2 — What makes the surface breathe, and the measurement that failed its own control.** The
workspace does not animate; **it accumulates**. A room whose last message is an hour old is a room
where nothing happened. **The freshness signal is a timestamp, not a pulse** — and on the wall page
the same idea is a field: `SYNC · 14:02:47`, the machine saying when it last knew.

The screen's own movement was measured under law 8 with a **30-frame dense pass at 10 fps over
5.0–8.0 s** on the wall page's orb, against **30 control frames of blank wall over the same
seconds**: the orb changed **43.47 %** of its pixels per 100 ms and **the static control changed
25.83 %**. Because the control moves nearly as much as the screen, **the movement in this source is
`UNVERIFIED`** — the camera is handheld and nothing here can be attributed to the artwork. **What
would be needed:** a screen recording of the same page, or a tripod shot of the wall. **The timing
for this exact system already exists in our own file:** report 01 measured its clock advancing
**0.20 s of wall time in 0.20 s of film** and its radar sweeping **≈ 90–110° per second, one turn in
≈ 3.3–4 s**.

**3 — How it answers the human.** Three exchanges, all in the same shape: the agent reports, then
recommends, then **asks one question that can be answered in one word**. *"Luke, do you want me to
implement this iOS copy-confirmation fix?"* → *"Yes @Tom, go ahead…"*. *"Approve reactivating
iOS_SCALE_LAL…"* → a yes or a no. *"publish, or type `ignore`"* → a typed verb. **Latency, measured
from the timestamps on screen:** escalation → diagnosis **2 minutes**; approval → delivered PR
**16 minutes**; customer letter → reviewable code **19 minutes**.

**4 — What DXB takes.** Five mechanisms, each measured against our own state in §4:
