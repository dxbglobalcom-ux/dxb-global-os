## 2. What could not be read or tested

- **His book "Be the SOURCE".** Two packs quote it by chapter (`source-grounding/references/source-book.md`,
  `ai-visibility-dashboard.md`); the stance file is his summary, not the book. The book would settle the "Eligible"
  definition, the cost list and the four problems in their own words. It is the next thing to read for this study.
- **The companion skills his packs name but do not contain**: "SOURCE Scorecard", "Structure Audit", "Engine Coverage
  Tracker", "Brand Hallucination Monitor", "Brand Mention Builder", "Brand Consistency Audit", "AI Crawler Access
  Checker", "Is My Page Better?". Whether they are public, and what they add, is unknown.
- **The Hawk Academy web tools** the skills route to were not opened; whether they are ungated, and how they convert,
  is unmeasured.
- **The sample workbook** of the topical map was read for its tab names and row counts only (15 core, 70 supporting, 78
  links, 85 staged pages, 90 ordered rows), not cell by cell.
- **No skill was run.** His instructions say "install and run on the sample"; the brief forbids it and the fixtures were
  read, not executed. Whether our engine finds the planted defects of his crawl sample is the first test of
  `FOR-THE-HOLDING.md` addition 3, not a result.
- **The surface question** for AI-answer rounds: how far the assistants' API answers (with search on) differ from the
  consumer apps a buyer uses, per assistant and market. Measured nowhere yet; it decides how #3 is run at scale.
- **DataForSEO as the page-level qualifier.** His backlink ladder's best rung is the linking page's own traffic and
  keyword count, which his skill reads from Ahrefs. Whether DataForSEO's ranked-keywords data for a single URL gives
  the same rung, at what cost per prospect, is to be measured at B84's tool trial.
- **Review policy tables beyond Google** (Trustpilot, Yelp, marketplaces, regional platforms) and **testimonial law per
  market** (his file names only Australia's health-practitioner rule) — to be researched before `review-proof-sort` and
  #20 serve a client outside one market.
