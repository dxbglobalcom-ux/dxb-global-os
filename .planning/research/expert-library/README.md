## What the five of them say together — the lead's reading

They teach different trades and arrive at one shape. The office that wins:

1. **shows the prospect a measured truth about his own business before it asks for anything** (Matt's gap
   tools, Harry's four tools, Mei's funnel-leak read, Hormozi's "reveal their problem");
2. **does part of the work before it is paid** (Hormozi's proof-first offer; Matt's roadmap sent before the
   first call);
3. **publishes only what a model could not have written** — the owner's own knowledge and the client's own
   numbers (Matt's weekly interview, Harry's evidence gate, Mei's quote bank);
4. **keeps a cadence a tired team cannot** (weekly page-two work, weekly profile post, nightly scoring of
   every sales conversation, a reply within a minute);
5. **measures what it did and shows it** (the live cohort report, the guarantee read in the client's own
   Search Console, the proof ledger).

A human agency does each of these sparingly, because each costs hours — Mei turns away brands under $30k a
month of ad spend, Matt's tools answer by e-mail in English for two countries, Hormozi needs fifty people to
keep a sales floor consistent. **For the holding each of them costs tokens.** That is the whole strategy: spend
what is nearly free for us — making, measuring, speed, languages — exactly where the buyer's risk, delay and
effort sit. Not "a cheaper agency": the things an agency cannot afford to do.

### One chain, not eleven ideas

Every strong idea in the eight studies is a station on one chain, and each station's output is the next
station's input — nothing is made only for marketing:

| Station | What it is | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Measure** | one set of engines: can an AI read this page, are AI bots blocked, is speed really a problem, the link-and-listing gap (the 162-source table), the profile gap, who the assistants recommend in this trade and city (asked three times), where the funnel leaks, how fast the firm answers an enquiry | Harry 4 · Matt A 2, 4 · Matt B 5, 7 · Mei 5 · sales-skill 3 |
| **Show** | the same engines public and ungated on our own domain; and the prospect's own home page rebuilt, or his ads made, before he is asked | Harry 4 · offers 1, 8 · marketing 1, 3 |
| **Fix** | repairs at a fixed price by tomorrow — the job no agency turns up for; the hand-over says honestly what he does not need | offers 2 |
| **Found** | the fixed-price base: a readable site, one identity everywhere, presence in every measured source that fits his country and trade, Google's preferred-source button, the baseline | Harry 2, 3 · the links (s23, k03, s05) |
| **Build** | pages to blueprint; no page ships without an evidence item; the owner gives ten minutes of talk a week | Harry 1, 9 · Matt A 1 · Matt B 1 |
| **Care** | the monthly loop: page-two work, the local profile, one data story a quarter, the ad loop (listen → roadmap → belief brief → studio → read) | Matt A 5, 6 · Matt B 2, 4 · Harry 5, 8 · Mei 1–6 |
| **Sell** | a reply in a minute; one conversation written once; every conversation scored the same night, one change a week; the same desk sold to firms with human sales teams | sales-team 1–4, 8 · sales-skill 1–6 |
| **Prove** | every delivery writes a measured before-and-after; ninety days later it is read again in public; the proof becomes the films, the studies and the next prospect's opening line | offers 10 · marketing 2, 5, 11 · Harry 7 |

The measuring engines are built once and used three times — as the free tool that brings the client, as the
gate before a delivery leaves, and as the first page of the proposal. That is why they come first.

### The order, and why

- **The asset before the audience.** Measured on Harry's own account: 136 posts in March 2026 drew 46 comments;
  32 posts in June drew 10,435, once one concrete free list stood behind one keyword. Hormozi's most-viewed
  videos of a quarter sold nothing. So the measuring engines and the measured library exist before any reel
  machine runs.
- **Proof before scale.** By Hormozi's constraint method the holding's bottleneck today is not capability; it
  is proof and an offer. The whole chain is therefore run first on the holding's own site — it is the test
  bench, case study number one, and every guarantee is run there before it is offered to anyone.
- **Then one trade in one country**, with built previews, until five clients and the cost per reply are known.

### What is honestly ours, and what is not

Hormozi puts "an agency for small firms" in his worst class of business: labour-heavy and a commodity. Workers
remove the labour and make the commodity worse — every rival gets the same models. What stays ours is what
accumulates: the measured library per country and trade, the proof ledger, the reasons kept with every ad and
every page, the Media Studio, speed, and the brand. What we do not have: one client, one tried guarantee, one
measured conversation by an AI seller. The candidate prices in `offers-business` are the analyst's, from
general market knowledge, not a price study. And our own research of 2026-11-04 found the evidence for AEO and
GEO as a separate discipline weak — so it is sold as measurement ("this is the page the assistant quoted"),
never as a promise.

### What is refused, from all five

Link swaps and paid placements presented as earned links; fake reviews, review rewards and scripted reviews;
fake newsletters, staged scarcity, posing as several people, invented statistics about a rival; hidden
promotion in forums; paraphrased competitor reviews as testimonials; interest-based financing; prize draws
until the CEO rules. Each has its clean form written in the playbooks, and the clean form is what would be
built. An AI seller says it is one.
