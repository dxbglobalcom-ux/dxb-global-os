## What deserves a deeper study, and why

1. **The 80 untranscribed posts, in this order:** the Dec 2023-Feb 2024 run on creative-testing structure and
   cost caps (gap 5); the May 2025 "course" reels not in the set — listicles, the statics trick, iterations
   ("psa: you're doing iterations all wrong", 2025-05-05); the 2025-04-30 carousel "lessons from scaling in
   2025". These are the parts of her method that the 30 newest reels assume and do not repeat.
2. **Frames of four reels only** — the email-ad prompts, the notes-ad examples, the Lake House Mom brief as
   shown on screen, the roadmap screen in the Muse reel. Four screenshots would close gaps 3 and 6. This is
   the one place where looking at the picture, not second by second but at a single frame, pays.
3. **Meta's Muse beside our own research door.** She says no other tool comes close for mining Instagram,
   Facebook and Threads, because it searches Meta's own content from inside. A side-by-side on one brand:
   what does it find that Reddit, reviews and comments read from outside do not?
4. **Eugene Schwartz's five stages of awareness and belief-shift copy** — the primary sources under her
   roadmap and her brief. A short study would turn the stage table in the playbook into rules with examples
   per stage.
5. **Meta's Advertising Standards, read at source, for the sectors we expect first** — personal health and
   appearance, unrealistic outcomes, personal attributes, cosmetic procedures. She names the skill ("how you
   say it decides whether Meta throttles it") and does not teach it; it is a checklist we would have to
   write.
6. **Other strategists who publish the parts she holds back** — full briefs, naming conventions, test
   budgets. Worth one research pass to find two or three whose templates are public, to set beside hers.
7. **The under-$30k market she refuses.** How many brands sit there, what they pay today, and to whom — the
   sizing behind the claim that our cost structure reaches them.
