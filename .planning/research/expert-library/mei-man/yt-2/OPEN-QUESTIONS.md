# Mei Man, her other 19 YouTube videos — open questions

Studied 2026-11-23.

## The question the lead asked: are her cost-cap and testing-structure series in these videos?

**No.** The 19 videos run from April 2019 to October 2021. Her channel's public list holds 36 videos
(`yt-dlp --flat-playlist`, 2026-11-23): 15 read by the first study, these 19, and two with no subtitles. No
title of the 36 is about cost caps, bid caps or a testing account structure. The series named in the first
study's gap 5 (December 2023 – February 2024) exists only as Instagram posts whose captions were read; their
spoken content has not been transcribed. What these videos do hold is her **earlier** testing and scaling
method (interest ad sets at ~$10 a day read over seven days, a "super ad set" of the winners, +10-20% budget
steps, duplicating ×3, a CBO campaign of winners — PLAYBOOK §3, §5) — the method her 2023-24 captions abandon.
**Gap 5 stays open**, and it is to be closed from her Instagram reels of that period, not from YouTube.

   testing structure). They are the bridge between the media buyer of these videos and the strategist of
   2025-26, and the only source of her current bidding method. The first study names them already; this
   addition confirms they are not elsewhere.
2. **The two uncaptioned videos** (gap 1), by local speech-to-text — about 13 minutes of audio.
3. **Meta's own pages for the conversation-ad lane**: click-to-message ad set-up, Messenger and Instagram
   messaging policy, and WhatsApp's AI-provider terms — before idea 3 is offered to anyone.
4. **Marginal-return reading in practice** — how incrementality tests (Meta's conversion lift, first study §12)
   and a simple budget-step table agree or differ; one published method set beside ours before the report uses
   it for a client.
