## 2. New rows

### Row 49 — in "2. Found and build" (after 46)

| # | Skill | What it does | Specified in | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 49 | `ai-call-readiness` | Makes a local business answerable when Google's AI phones for price and availability: what the machine cannot read is published; what it must ask is answered, with a price, by staff or an answering worker that says it is AI; measured by Google's own "Check for me" result | MC C1 · with 8 `gbp-optimise`, 39 `first-touch` | B84, B83 |

- **Trigger.** Onboarding of any local client in a country where Google's AI calling is live (today: the US
  except five states — re-read Google's help page at each run); then monthly; and on any new service or price.
- **Inputs.** The client's signed price sheet (ranges allowed) and service list; opening hours and calendar;
  the phone line and who answers it; Business Profile access; Merchant Center access if goods are sold; the
  trade and town.
- **Steps.** (1) Read Google's current help pages for the feature and the opt-out (the two in PLAYBOOK C1).
  (2) Check that every service on the site and profile states a price or range and how availability is given;
  write what is missing from the price sheet, never from a guess. (3) For goods, check stock is in Merchant
  Center. (4) Confirm Advanced settings → "Google automated calls and text messages" is on. (5) Place a test
  call in opening hours and one outside; record rings to answer and whether a price was given. (6) If the
  client takes the answering worker: load the price sheet and calendar; the worker opens "This is the AI
  assistant of [business]", answers only from the sheet, books or takes a message, and sends the owner a
  summary within five minutes. (7) Run "Check for me" for the trade and town with "don't call" three times on
  different days and record what the machine finds online; log Google's calls on the client's own line
  (answered with price / without / missed). Never place a request that makes Google phone businesses for a job
  nobody will buy.
- **Output.** A one-page readiness sheet (published prices: yes/no per service; setting; test-call results);
  the three reads and the call log side by side; a line in the monthly report (calls received, answered, price given).
  It refuses to publish or quote a price the client did not sign.

- **Trigger.** A prospect chosen for outreach; or a client's onboarding, as the first brand check.

  it is used. (6) Publish as a private page, link sent only to that business.

  nothing but layout). Refuses to publish a preview anywhere public, and refuses to stage a product it has no
  photo of.

- **Trigger.** A finding from an engine, a gate or checklist ready to publish, a platform feature the worker

  template; the account's language.
- **Steps.** (1) Truth check: primary source opened and linked; scope and date stated; any code or string

  MEASURED; refused words: killed, destroyed, illegal, BREAKING on our own offer. (3) Slide 2: what it is,
  the source. (4) One step or fact per slide, ≤ 30 words, one accent phrase, our own screenshot with one
  arrow. (5) A re-hook only if the next slide pays it. (6) Proof or limit slide: our measurement or the
  source's quote, and what it does not do. (7) Last slide: the page address; optionally "or comment WORD".
  (8) Caption with the full steps, the source link, the keyword, required AI labels. (9) Render 1080 × 1350 in
  the Media Studio. (10) Ledger row: ask class, subject, source, date.
- **Output.** The slide files, the caption, the ledger row; the 24-hour and 7-day reads go to skill 33.
  Refuses a post whose last slide unlocks something the page does not show.
