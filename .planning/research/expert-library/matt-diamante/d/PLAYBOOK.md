### D17 — A press release a month (NEW)
`DbigxdoRjem (2026-08-02)` 00:30. **[his]** "Do one press release per month, talk about new reviews,
achievements, et cetera." **[test]** A release is not a link play: Google's link-spam policy names "links with optimized anchor text

hire, a new location), never a monthly release with nothing to say. Paid wire distribution is money out.
