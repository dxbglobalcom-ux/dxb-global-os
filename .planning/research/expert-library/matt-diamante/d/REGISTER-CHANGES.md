## 2. New rows

### Row 62 — in "3. Care" (after 18)

| # | Skill | What it does | Specified in | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 62 | `page-change-guard` | No edit or deletion of a page that earns clicks without a risk read: what it earns now, what the change touches, the clicks at risk, a stored copy, the rollback and the date it may be judged; a rewritten money page is then left alone until that date | MD D4, D5, D45 · in front of 15, 16, 18 · beside HSK 53 `migration-guard` (site moves) and 60 `decay-triage` (after a loss) | B84 |

- **Trigger.** Any proposed change from skills 15, 16, 18 or a person, to a page with clicks in the last 90 days;
  any page on a prune list.
- **Inputs.** The URL; Search Console for the page (16 months: queries, clicks, positions); referring domains;
  the proposed change as a diff; the client's threshold for "clicks at risk".
- **Steps.** (1) Read what the page ranks for and earns; list the queries with clicks. (2) Map the diff to them:
  which query's words, heading or section the change removes or moves. (3) Score the risk: clicks on touched
  queries ÷ page clicks; a URL change or deletion is always high. (4) Decide: go / smaller change / not now,
  with the reason in one line. (5) For go: store the page as it is, write the rollback, set the re-read date
  (four weeks for a line or title; 8–12 weeks for a rewrite) and lock the page against further edits until
  then. (6) For a deletion: links or clicks present → merge or 301 to the nearest equivalent, never a bare
  removal. (7) At the re-read date: compare with the baseline; keep, roll back or try the next change.
- **Output.** A guard row per change (risk, decision, rollback, re-read date, result) that feeds `proof-capture`;
  refuses a second change to a locked page and a deletion without the link and traffic read.

- **Trigger.** Weekly per local client or holding brand that has the accounts.

  removals and warnings; a removal stops posting in that group.
