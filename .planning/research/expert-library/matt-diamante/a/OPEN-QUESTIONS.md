## What deserves a deeper study

1. **His book *Get Found*** (January 2026) — the method in one place, and probably the agency's own
   story. Read as text.
2. **One full Rank Week** — the five recorded sessions and the pre-training. It is his best-tested
   teaching sequence (9,500 people) and the exact model for the sprint in idea 3: what he has them do
   each day, where they get stuck, and how and when Insiders is offered. It costs $10 and an email,
   so it is the CEO's choice (below).
3. **The carousel slides of his 30 most-commented posts** — read as images; they hold the tactics the
   captions only tease, and they are his proven slide structures for our own carousels.
4. **His blog on heytony.ca** — the post sitemap lists it; I read only the pricing post. The posts on
   "SEO software used at HeyTony", the local SEO checklist and the case studies (pet daycare,
   wholesale, waxing studio) would give his tool stack and real client timelines.
5. **An independent measurement of local ranking factors** (the yearly Whitespark survey he himself
   points to, and Sterling Sky's published tests) — to settle point 8 before the profile skill is
   authored.
6. **Google's spam policies on link schemes and Wikipedia's conflict-of-interest rule, at the source**
   — to fix the exact line for the link service (I relied on the holding's library note and my own
   knowledge for these two).
7. **Two rivals of his in the same model** — a second and third teacher-agency with free tools — to
   see which parts of the machine are common practice and which are his own.

## For the CEO to decide

1. **He is called our consultant.** If that relationship is real and paid, the gated assets, the Rank
   Week recordings and Insiders are available through it without anyone signing up anonymously — may
   the lead ask him for them directly?
2. **Prices and the name** under which a sprint, a monthly plan and a done-for-you offer would be sold,
   and in which markets and languages first. I wrote none; his are on the page for comparison.
3. **Email in front of free tools, or the result shown at once?** He gates every tool. Showing the
   result first and asking for the email to send the full report is more generous and may convert
   better — it should be measured, and the choice of which to try first is his.
4. **Identity steps for the holding's own entities**: ChatGPT's business registration (government ID)
   and Google's message agent (EIN or personal ID) stop at him.
5. **Paid link placements.** His agency prices a backlink at about $250 and delivers "guest posts or
   placements". I recommend the holding never buys a placement presented as editorial; if he wants
   sponsored, labelled placements considered as a separate paid road, that is his word.
6. **Who is on camera** in the holding's own content, if anyone — his engine is built on his own face.
