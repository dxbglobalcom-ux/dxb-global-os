## 3. For the CEO to decide

1. **First vertical and country for the outreach test.** The method needs one avatar. Our recommendation:
   pick by measurement, not by taste — run 30 built previews in each of three candidate verticals in one
   English-speaking market and keep the one with the best reply rate. If you prefer to name the vertical, the
   test starts there.
2. **Working free for the first five clients** of the web office and of the agency seat, for the stated trade
   (use, feedback, a review, referrals). It costs GPU time, tokens and data fees. Recommendation: yes — it is
   the only route to proof that does not rest on claims.
3. **The sending identity** for cold outreach: a domain and mailboxes, and which social accounts may send
   direct messages. Recommendation: a separate sending domain, never the holding's main one.
4. **A human face or voice.** His whole method says B2B buyers trust demonstrated human experience, and that
   an AI presenter is weakest exactly here. Options: you front the long pieces; a hired presenter with
   consent; or no face at all — the screen, the data and the work speak. Recommendation: start faceless with
   measured data and built previews, and decide after the first test whether a face is needed.
5. **How "run by AI workers" is framed** to each audience. It is true and is always stated. For technical and
   cost-minded buyers it is the selling point; for a mass consumer audience his Coca-Cola case warns the
   pairing can repel. Recommendation: lead with the result and the speed, state the method plainly second.
6. **Prize draws.** He recommends raffles for a year of service at events and in newsletters, and
   "sweepstakes tickets" inside a product. A draw that people pay to enter is a game of chance; a free draw
   among people who would receive a gift anyway may be different. This is your boundary to rule on. Until you
   do, we use no draw of any kind — the same goal is met by "the first N get it free" or a gift to everyone.
7. **Budget caps**: data-provider cost per free diagnostic report, and the first ad budget once a winner
   exists. Both are money out.
8. **Publishing the 2026–27 flagship** (the measured study) under a public date — a commitment only you make.
