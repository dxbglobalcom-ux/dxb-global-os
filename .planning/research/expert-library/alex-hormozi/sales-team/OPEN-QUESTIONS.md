## 2. What deserves a deeper study

1. **His offer and guarantee doctrine** ($100M Offers; the business/marketing analysts' slices). His diagnosis
   order is "fix the offer, not the rep" `[T 186:42]`, and the best-case/worst-case close only works with a
   guarantee. Our sales script cannot be finished before the web office's first offer, price range and
   guarantee exist.
2. **His CLOSER framework and the individual closing videos** (the `sales-skill` slice) — to merge the two
   outlines into one script skeleton rather than keep two.
3. **His referral and outreach chapters** ($100M Leads) — the referral ask here is one script; the book has
   the full set (he says referrals are 20–30% of sales).
4. **The acquisition.com careers page (SDR application)** — he offers it as a model of a sales letter with a
   VSL `[T 96:39]`. Worth one read as a template for any page that must make people apply (clients for a
   pilot programme, partners).
5. **Voice.** Before any AI voice worker speaks to a prospect: per market, the rules on AI-voice calls,
   recording consent and written outreach. Verified so far only at summary level: the FCC's February 2024
   ruling (AI voice = "artificial" under the TCPA, prior consent needed) and the EU AI Act Article 50(1)
   disclosure duty from 2 August 2026 — both from law-firm summaries, not from the primary texts.
6. **Call-analysis rivals** for idea 8 (Gong, Demodesk, Fireflies scorecards, GradeMyClose) — prices and what
   a small business actually gets, before the service is priced.

## 3. What the CEO must decide

1. **The first offer the sales worker sells** for the web office and for the agency seat — what, at what price
   range, with what guarantee. Everything else in this slice is ready to be built around it.
2. **Pilot clients for proof:** three to five first clients at a reduced price in exchange for measured
   before/after and a testimonial — yes or no, and the price.
3. **Whether a person appears** in the frame (the films before the booking and before the close) and whether
   he gives a short founder word at the close of the first deals.
4. **Voice or not, at first.** My recommendation: chat and e-mail first, voice only for call-backs a prospect
   asks for — the law and the measurability both point that way.
5. **A referral reward** — none (his script uses only a compliment), a discount, or cash. Money out if cash.
6. **Idea 8 as a revenue line** — selling the scored-conversation service to businesses with human sales
   teams: whether it goes on the revenue list now or after the holding's own sales desk has run.
7. **The four repairs** (`PLAYBOOK.md` §3): I dropped the "find the money" close, the "you're above most
   people" line, any scarcity that is not real, and any client story that is not real. If he wants any of them
   kept in some form, it is his word.
