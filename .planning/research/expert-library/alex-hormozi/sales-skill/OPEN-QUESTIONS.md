## 2. Law and platform rules to read before building (not checked beyond one search)

- **Withdrawal rights.** He says a three-day refund is something "you legally have to" give (SF2 16:05). That is
  not a general rule: the US three-day rule covers door-to-door-type sales; the EU's 14-day withdrawal right
  covers consumers in distance contracts. Business buyers are generally outside both — my reading, to be
  confirmed per market. The holding can simply *choose* a clear withdrawal window as its own promise.
- **AI disclosure.** Several jurisdictions require telling a person they are dealing with an AI (the EU AI Act's
  transparency duty; US state bot-disclosure laws), and AI-generated voice calls in the US fall under
  telemarketing consent rules — from my knowledge, not re-checked today. It
  needs a per-market read before the voice channel.
- **Cold contact.** Unsolicited SMS and automated DMs are restricted or forbidden in many markets and on the
  platforms themselves; business e-mail to a published address is allowed in some countries and not others.
  The outreach skill needs a country table before any campaign.
- **Automatic renewal.** Opt-out renewals carry disclosure and easy-cancel duties in many markets.

## 4. What the CEO must decide

1. **Does a standard order go through without him?** The approval gate stops contracts at him. For fixed
   packages under terms he approves once, may the worker take the order and payment, with only exceptions coming
   to his phone? Without this, every sale waits on him and the 60-second advantage ends at the price.
2. **The prices, the three tiers and their real differences**, the payment-plan terms, and whether a rush fee and
   a withdrawal window exist — for the site, the monthly SEO package and the ad package.
3. **Whether the worker may guarantee anything**, and what (deliverables and dates; ownership; a refund window).
4. **The talk-to-the-founder option** — wanted at all; paid, or reserved for orders above a size.
5. **The AI front desk as a product** (idea 3) — a yes to selling it, and its price.
6. **The voice channel** — a phone number and an outbound-capable account are an identity and money step.
7. **The first outreach campaign** — niche, city and volume for idea 1's test.
8. **Whether a named human appears in the worker's introduction** ("[name] approves every order") and which name.
