## 2. What deserves a deeper study, and why

1. **$100M Money Models, whole** (the audiobook is on his podcast as episodes 939–946, and the book is
   free on his site) — the exact mechanics and scripts of each of the fifteen offers, above all the
   waived-fee, trial-with-penalty and rollover offers that our continuity model leans on.
2. **$100M Offers, the guarantee and pricing chapters** — his refund arithmetic and the wording patterns,
   to finish the guarantee ledger.
3. **Podcast episode 985, "The Only Two Numbers That Decide If Your Business Survives"** — the LTV:CAC
   ladder in his own words.
4. **His three AI episodes together** — "Stop Ignoring AI" (963), "The Barbell Strategy for Surviving the
   AI Shift" (982), "The Case Against AI" (993), with "Why AI won't make you rich". The titles pull in
   opposite directions; the "barbell" one may speak directly to our premium-plus-volume position.
5. **The other Scale or Fail episodes on agencies and service firms**, and the one trailed at the end of
   the sticker episode (a specialist service told to charge five times more) — more worked diagnoses to
   test the `business-diagnosis` skill against.
6. **Any episode where he diagnoses a marketing agency** — his view of our exact vehicle, beyond the one
   line in the "idiot index".
7. **A price study of site, care and ads packages in the first target markets** — outside this expert, but
   the offers cannot go live without it.

## 3. What the CEO must decide

1. **The price list and the standard terms** — repairs, the three site sizes, Care / Grow / Scale, the
   waived-fee term, credits, the priority fee, the prepay discount. (B84 already holds this at him.)
2. **The guarantees** — whether the ledger in FOR-THE-HOLDING §2.5 may be offered once each row has passed
   on the holding's own site. A guarantee is a contract term.
3. **His eye on every site job (B84 decisions 4–5) against a volume line.** Are repairs, the Care package
   and the pre-sale demos "site jobs" that wait for him? By Hormozi's method this becomes the bottleneck
   the day demand exists. Proposal: demos and repairs pass on the machine's gates; full sites keep his eye.
4. **What the studio is** — the barbell (flagship for buyers who pay for speed and certainty, plus a
   fixed-price volume line) or one of the two. The mid-priced custom site is the place his method says to
   avoid.
5. **Free cases** — how many firms get a full site free in exchange for a documented case, in which niche
   and city.
6. **Islamic boundary — his alone:**
   - Is a prize draw with free entry acceptable as a lead offer? (I have proposed a form that needs no
     draw.)
   - Our own instalments: only at the same total price, or may a deferred price differ from the cash price
     when fixed at signing? (I have assumed the same total and a prepay discount.)
   - A fee waived against a 12-month term, with the remainder due on early exit — acceptable as stated?
   - Accepting client payments made by credit card, where the client may be paying his bank interest.
7. **Ad spend on the holding's own brands** for the first test of the ads offer — money out.
8. **Consent to publish a client's numbers** in the 90-day series — a clause in the standard terms.
9. **Whether the weekly constraint page becomes a standing output of the management worker** — and on
   which surface he wants to see it.
