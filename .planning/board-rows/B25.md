## Added 2026-11-17 — the two tools' recipes for that measurement <!-- CEO-OK: tab-rows-placed-on-the-board-by-the-next-session-2026-11-17 -->

On his word <!-- CEO-OK: tab-rows-placed-on-the-board-by-the-next-session-2026-11-17 -->. Read at the source 2026-11-17; nothing is installed — his word of 2026-11-16 above stands (the three are measured together, at this row's turn). This machine: Ubuntu 26.04.1, GNOME 50.1 on Wayland.

- **Handy** — `cjpais/Handy` v0.10.1 (2026-11-14), MIT, fully local: `sudo apt install ./Handy_*.deb`. For Turkish a Whisper model is chosen (Small, Medium, Turbo, Large) — its recommended Parakeet models do not know Turkish (25 languages, no `tr`). Its README: Wayland support is limited; on Ubuntu 26.04 `wtype` does not work and `ydotool` is needed for typing; the global shortcut is set in GNOME Settings with the command `handy --toggle-transcription`.
- **OpenWhispr** — `OpenWhispr/openwhispr` v1.12.0 (2026-11-17), MIT: `sudo apt install ./OpenWhispr-*-linux-x64.deb`. Local mode downloads a model and is free; whether its first screen lets the sign-in be skipped is not measured. Paste on Wayland: the `input` group (`sudo usermod -aG input $USER`) or `ydotool`; hold-to-talk needs GNOME 48+ (met here). Whisper runs on the card (CUDA); the Turkish of its default model (Orukeet) and of Cohere Transcribe is not measured.
- **What the measurement adds for both:** the paste into an open program on this GNOME Wayland desktop, and his own sentences in Turkish. `sudo` steps are his click.
