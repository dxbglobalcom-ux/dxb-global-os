## Mission and vision <!-- CEO-OK: b43-mission-and-vision-2026-11-09 -->

**Mission:** the holding's studio and agency for everything about advertising and visual content, end to end — the idea, the script, casting, shooting and generating films and stills, 3D and animation, short-form and user-style video, long-form films and documentaries, edit, sound and finish, and whole campaigns run for the client from first brief to last delivery — for clients of every size, from local businesses to the world's largest brands, each job made at the depth it deserves. It also makes the holding's own channels' documentary and Islamic content. It is one of the holding's revenue engines and works together with the DxB Web Studio. The DxB Media Studio makes; the DxB Agency, its face to clients, sells.

**Vision:** a studio whose films read as filmed, not generated, and compete for first place in the world's advertising awards, and that clients of every size choose because what it makes sells for them.

Nothing written for the studio — a plan, a rule, a limit, a decision — may narrow this mission. When the studio's own introduction file is written, it opens with this mission and vision; its seats receive them at the start of every job; every plan brief for the studio opens with them.

## The rules that stand today

### How a film is made

- **R1 · Long form** (2026-08-31; scope: long films; no registered marker). A long film is never one generation: one script → scenes → shots, each shot generated separately → one timeline; music, effects, subtitles and transitions land last; job, scene and shot are records the studio screen shows, each shot with its state, its cost and its clock.
- **R2 · The edit is the cost lever** (the view of 2026-08-31; no registered marker). Only part of a professional long film is truly generated — 20–40 % was the view of that day — and the rest is narrated stills, motion graphics, type, product photography, screen capture and B-roll; the screen shows every job's generated percentage, because that number is the margin.
- **R3 · The order of operations for one finished shot** (2026-08-31, as his September rulings left it; no registered marker for the chain itself). Script and direction → the reference set, where the job needs continuity → the image, made here → motion on the brief's road (R4) → the finish at native size: grade and grain, audio (the voice by R22; music and mix in post), assembly → the finished native draft to his eye (R35; finished, not raw — his word of 2026-11-17 <!-- CEO-OK: draft-shown-finished-not-raw-2026-11-17 -->) → enlarging only if it is asked at that job (R36), the finish then re-run from the master, grain after the enlarging; no step is skipped or folded into the one before it. The prompt is written as a cinematographer writes it — product, logo, light and camera all in the words. Where a route takes a frame, the still is judged and corrected first: a video engine cannot repair a frame it was handed, it can only move it.
- **R4 · The road comes from the brief** (2026-09-04) <!-- CEO-OK: road-comes-from-the-brief-t2v-default-2026-09-04 -->. The brief names the road — "with a prompt" (text-to-video), "with a storyboard / pictures" (image-to-video), or "choose the best", and only then the seat chooses; the general default is text-to-video and image-to-video is not forbidden.
- **R5 · Everything is made here; only the shooting engine differs** (2026-09-14) <!-- CEO-OK: flux-local-engine-only-everything-made-here-2026-09-14 -->. Script, storyboard, panels, stills, first frames and the cut are made on this computer on either route — the station's own engine or an external engine (RunPod, an API or an MCP hand); the local engine is the first choice; text-to-video and image-to-video are both open from the start on either route. A human or a product enters as a real photograph or as a written sheet (his September amendment (a); no registered marker says this clause).
- **R6 · The handed reference — a measured fact about H3, not a studio rule in either direction** (his account, 2026-11-17) <!-- CEO-OK: h3-handed-reference-is-an-engine-fact-2026-11-17 -->. On H3 a handed face is not kept exactly: in the trial behind the sentence of 2026-09-14, H3 was handed an avatar made in Flux and did not reproduce it exactly, and the UGC came out better when H3 made the character itself (ArcFace 0.86 against the reference on 2026-11-05). So in H3 UGC the character is born in the engine unless the job accepts that likeness; it was not tried outside UGC. Whether a reference is handed is decided engine by engine, by how exactly that engine keeps one, and an engine that keeps it exactly gets it — a paid one is named with its price, and buying is his. The wording is the engineer's, told to him; his word changes it.
- **R7 · Continuity is an asset the studio holds** (his words 2026-08-31; the rule taken 2026-09-14) <!-- CEO-OK: astra-rule-accepted-flux-scoped-to-local-2026-09-14 -->. *"Adam bir sahnede siyah ceket giyiyorsa sonraki shot'ta birden mavi ceket olmamalı."* A job that needs continuity across takes holds a reference set — character, wardrobe, location, product, palette, lens and camera language. The director takes the simplest road: one take when it suffices; an engine that can shoot several shots in one run is evaluated first; when separate takes are needed they are joined by the shooting engine's own frames — the last frame of one take is the first of the next — on whichever route; no film is pre-split into a fixed number of parts. Not yet tried in production (leg 3).
- **R8 · Marks and lettering** (2026-09-03) <!-- CEO-OK: no-brand-restriction-2026-09-03 -->. The holding has no brand restriction of its own: a third-party mark appears as it is in the real picture. The engine never draws lettering; a readable mark comes from the real file or the real photograph, laid in post. A client's own brand guideline is honoured as their instruction.
- **R9 · The cast** (2026-09-03; his addition 2026-09-15) <!-- CEO-OK: avatar-cast-men-and-elderly-women-only-2026-09-03 --> <!-- CEO-OK: cast-law-client-may-order-female-content-2026-09-15 -->. The holding's own cast is men and elderly women only (Elif and Nora retired, their codes kept); when a client asks for an advertisement with female-presenter content it is produced, on the client's responsibility, recorded on the brief.
- **R10 · The cast today.** AHMET (DXB-A-010) and JAMES (DXB-A-011) were born in H3 from a text-only casting take (2026-09-04) <!-- CEO-OK: ahmet-cast-member-born-in-engine-2026-09-04 -->; the four presenters drawn with FLUX on 2026-09-03 — ARDA, İDRİS, TOMAS, ROSA — are out of use, codes and files kept (2026-09-13) <!-- CEO-OK: flux-avatars-retired-and-complaint-numbering-2026-09-13 -->.
- **R11 · The quality rule** (2026-08-31; no registered marker). The work must not look like AI video: the bar is *"Nike, Apple, Mercedes, Coca-Cola reklamı gibi gerçek çekilmiş sinematik reklam hissi"* — a shot that reads as filmed. - **R25 · The hyper-realistic UGC recipe — scope: UGC and ad videos** (2026-11-07) <!-- CEO-OK: hyper-realistic-ugc-recipe-2026-11-07 -->. UGC and ad videos are made so realistic — picture, voice, everything — that nobody can tell they are AI; our own factory is the default (the studio on our own card, free tools, Voice Studio instead of ElevenLabs). The factory pipeline, in order:
  1. Reference: only framing, room, light and phone angle from real UGC go into the LOOK prompt; the face comes from our own cast, never a real person's — the composition is taken, never the person, the face or the content. Prompt language: `tools/studio-prompts/realism/ugc-realism-prompts.md` (the worked skincare example ready to paste) and its source `realism-prompts.md` beside it.
  2. Script: the writer seat; no "not X, Y" and no triple short lines, fillers allowed; the WER gate.
  3. Image: R20.
  4. Video: MiniMax H3 T2VA / Ref2VA, 640×1152, 24 fps.
  5. Voice: R23; the clone is a Voice Studio clone of a consenting cast voice fed to H3 `--ref-audio` (Voice Studio's default engine OmniVoice; VoxCPM2 beside it).
  6. Lip sync, only if `--ref-audio` does not hold: LatentSync 1.5 (1.6 needs 18 GB and does not fit the 16 GB card).
  7. Grade and grain (FFmpeg): `selectivecolor=yellows='0 0 -0.15 0':correction_method=relative,eq=saturation=1.08,noise=alls=5:allf=t+u` — a starting point; the values are his eye's.
  8. Interpolation: ComfyUI's `FrameInterpolate` with RIFE 4.25, ×5 → 120 → `fps=30`; 60 shown beside it for his eye.
  9. Loudness: `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=9`.
  10. Final check: WER, identity first ↔ last frame, the picture by eye, the voice by ear.
  11. His eye on the finished native draft (R35) — graded, grained, with its sound and its cut, not enlarged <!-- CEO-OK: draft-shown-finished-not-raw-2026-11-17 -->.

  Enlarging is not a step of the recipe: it is asked at that job (R36), after step 11; where a job is enlarged the finish is re-run from the master — the enlarging first, then grain and interpolation.
