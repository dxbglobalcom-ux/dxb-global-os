1. **The preparation dossier** — read-only. Per foundation part: what the board's rows will load on it, what stands
   today, which defects the board already names. The design is then read from the dossier, not from the code.
2. **The target design** — the lead, at `xhigh`: the foundation's architecture, written against what will be built on it.
3. **The verdict table** — each part: kept, repaired or rewritten; who writes it; in what order. Audited blind by Sol
   and a second Fable 5.2; then it goes to him once.
4. **The build** — part by part, test first, on the construction engine (the bench rule). An old part is removed only

the money path, moving data from the old schema to the new; Fable 5.2 `high` for the weighty parts — the worker engine,
