# B86 — the holding builds on itself

**Opened 2026-11-22 on his word** <!-- CEO-OK: holding-builds-on-itself-row-opened-2026-11-22 -->. Nothing is designed or built.

## His question and the answer he took

His question, verbatim: "Sistemimizde en önemli şeylerden bir tanesi sistem kendi üzerinde bir şeyleri inşa edilecekse inşa edilecek kapasitede olmalı. Mi böyle mi olsun yoksa mesela VS Code'da biz şimdi bunu inşa ediyoruz. Yine VS Code'da girip bak şimdi işte bu holdingin operating sisteminde şöyle bunu değiştirelim deyip buradan mı yine yapacağız?"

The lead's recommendation, as told to him: two layers. What he changes day to day — approval rules, budgets, job types, a seat's brain, switching an office on or off — is changed from inside the system, from his screen (B85's plan §2 A: his policy lives in the database behind his doors); no VS Code. The system's own code — a new office, a change to the foundation — is written from the construction's sessions while the foundation is built: a base that cannot yet carry itself is not built on itself. Once the foundation stands, code work becomes a job type: the holding's own masters write a new office from inside, through the same desk, the same checks and the same tests; a change that touches the foundation itself stops at his approval.

His answer, verbatim: "Sistem kendi üstüne inşa edilmesin mi? Sorusu bu öneri neyse onu yapalım."

## Where it stands

- **Joins the foundation** after stage 2 (the job, the checks as parts, the outward path and its gate) — PLAN §6; an office's change lands in the office's own place and the ruler lists what fell outside it (PLAN §2 M).
- **Near it on the board, not the same thing:** a worker asks for a tool it lacks and is granted it (B41); the tools are watched and upgraded (B42); a defect is detected, owned and repaired (C39); a change is kept only if it scores better against a locked score (C40). None of them writes the system's own code as a job. Found by a word search of the board and the row files (2026-11-22); a row saying it in other words could have been missed.
- **Not decided:** which changes count as "the foundation itself"; who checks a code job (the construction's own rule today: Fable 5.2 writes weighty code, a blind audit follows); how a change is applied to the running company. Each comes with its plan.
