### A job, end to end (his outletcity example)

1. The client speaks in their own words, gives a reference link, logo, photos — no prompt.
2. The machine tears the reference down (decision 3), or the cupboard already holds it; the field experts read their parts.
3. The director sets the site's class and idea, writes three directions and the build brief; the client looks once.
4. Three masters build the home page apart, one direction each, with quick images; the best is chosen and grafted (his choice at ≥ 1,000 $, the director's below).
5. Images for the chosen direction: the client's own first, the library next, the studio for the gaps; film from the Media Studio.
6. The winning master finishes the site, with the copywriter and the motion hand on the same live page; the platform specialist when a shop or WP is asked. The director's rounds run inside this step.
7. The machine's gates and Sol, one closing round; fixes.
8. The home page goes to the client first, then the whole; launch; the monthly package offered.
