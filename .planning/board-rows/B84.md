### Newsletter, PR and links — the monthly service (decision 15)

- **Newsletter:** monthly, on the client's own site, written by the team from real information; the client's own subject, no separate newsletter site.
- **The holding's own sites (25-30):** each writes about clients of its sector in its newsletter and links to them; no charge, the holding's gesture <!-- CEO-OK: b84-own-sites-link-to-clients-2026-11-04 -->. The article and link are shown in the client's monthly report.
- **These sites are the office's first client:** the same team keeps their newsletters regular and does their SEO, because a link is worth as much as the site that gives it. Client and site lists are read from what the holding holds, at B84's rank.
- **Digital PR, step by step:** measure who links to the rival and not to the client; find something worth a story in real data; publish it on the client's site with charts and images; write a personal note to each editor; record what comes out in the monthly report.
- **The other roads:** the client's own news (a store opening, a collection, its story); real entries in sector guides and brand lists; finding articles that name the client without a link and asking the author to add it.
- **Press samples:** sent as a separate job, unconditional, never tied to a link.
- **Keywords:** what the client's customers search: real queries from Google Suggest, rising and falling interest from Trends, the rival's words as hints; these become the page and content plan and the newsletter's subjects. Real volumes come from the client's Search Console or paid data.
- **Market position:** the rival's links, popularity and Google rank set beside the client's, in the proposal and the monthly report; the direction is told, no exact rival traffic.
