### A job, end to end (his outletcity example)

1. The client speaks in their own words, gives a reference link, logo, photos — no prompt.
2. The master tears the reference down (REA, browser, both widths, video and motion frame by frame, measured).
3. The master writes the build brief; the client looks once.
4. Three sessions build the home page apart; the best is chosen and grafted (his choice at ≥ 1,000 $).
5. Images: the client's own first, the library next, the studio for the gaps; film from the Media Studio.
6. The winning session builds the other pages; WordPress/WooCommerce seat when a shop or WP is asked.
7. The closing look and Sol, one round; fixes.
8. The home page goes to the client first, then the whole; launch; the monthly package offered.
