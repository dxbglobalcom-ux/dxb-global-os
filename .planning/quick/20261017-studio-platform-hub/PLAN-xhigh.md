## A. Components (on what exists)

1. **Price engine** — `packages/shared/src/media-price.ts`. Pure and deterministic. Each route's price shape covers: per second × resolution, minimum billed seconds, audio, reference tokens, per image, per video, and a promo with its end date.
2. **Router** — `packages/shared/src/media-router.ts`. Pure: (shot, routes, quality cells, local ETA, now) → ranked candidates with reasons.
3. **Classifier** — Jev returns the shot class and its needs as schema JSON, through LiteLLM's `vercel_ai_gateway/` route (MODEL_ROUTING_SPEC.md:545-551). A schema failure or doubt goes to Sonnet 5.5 `medium`.
4. **External lane** — a third lane family beside the GPU and CPU lanes (`media-lanes.ts:12-23`), one loop per platform, capped at that platform's concurrency (fal starts at 2, R1). New kind `ext_render` is added to the CHECK at `20260903190000_b43_media_hands.sql:33`.

6. **dxb-mcp media group** gains three tools, registered in §9 (line 277):

## C. The router

1. **Input** — per shot, from the call sheet: text, seconds, size and aspect, speech language, references, deadline, deal value.
2. **Classify** — Jev, with Sonnet on doubt.
3. **Hard filters** — every `active` route on a `connected` platform is checked for: length (or a split the brief allows), resolution, audio, lip-sync language, references, video input.
4. **Quality gate, before price.**
   - Only cells inside `quality_tier` pass.
   - `top` means the set the evidence cannot separate from the leader. On a leaderboard that is the leader's confidence interval (R9: close ranks are statistically equal).
   - `ölçülmedi` is shown but never auto-chosen.
   - Deals of $1,000 or more lock `top` (COST_CONTROL A8: never a weaker route).
5. **Price.**
   - Engine price × `est_takes`. Until a rate is measured, `est_takes` defaults to the local 1.75 ⚠.
   - The promo applies only before `ends_at`.
   - A route with no price is "cost unknown", never 0 (A6).
   - The chosen route is confirmed with the vendor's estimate endpoint where one exists: fal `pricing/estimate`, WaveSpeed `model/price`, Higgsfield `/estimate` (R8 §4). Whether those calls are free ⚠.
6. **Choose.**
   - The local route comes first if it is eligible and its ETA (the queue × 625 or ~1,150 s per take) meets the deadline.
   - Otherwise, the lowest expected cost within the tier.
   - A tie goes to the faster route.
7. **Card** — one `approvals` row per job, using existing columns (APPROVAL_ENGINE §4): `risk_class=money_out`, `cost_estimate` = the ceiling, `alternatives` = the dial's three positions.
8. **Guard** — the DB refuses to claim `ext_render` without a decided approval, and refuses any spend above the ceiling.
9. **Dispatch** — submit with the privacy headers (fal `X-Fal-Store-IO: 0` and lifecycle, R1); download at once (Replicate keeps output 1 h, R8); checksum.
10. **QC**
    - automatic first: duration, size, audio, WER, identity, OCR
    - then the studio QC seat
    - then his eye (APPROVAL_ENGINE A9)
11. **Record**
    - billed seconds → `cost_ledger(source='media')`
    - failure billing follows each vendor's rule (fal 5xx free; Runware free; WaveSpeed refunds)
    - the verdict is appended to `media_quality`

Refusal or outage → the next candidate; above the ceiling → a new card.

## D. LiteLLM or an exception — decided

**Evidence** — LiteLLM docs, read through context7 on 2026-10-17:

- They live in the vault.
- They are read at call time inside the resident scheduler.
- They never sit in an environment. Every Agent SDK child inherits the `DXB_*` allowlist (`sdk-isolation.ts:129`, the O7 gap).
- They never appear in a prompt, a tool result or a log.

**Spend control**, in three layers:
1. the approved ceiling, enforced in code;
2. prepaid balances with auto-reload off;
3. `cost_ledger` rows feeding the monthly brake (COST_CONTROL §4bis).

At build, re-check LiteLLM 1.91.0 (STACK.md:26). A route that becomes supported moves into LiteLLM, and its exception line is deleted.

## G. The key-catcher

**Flow.**
1. The session starts `key-catcher --platform <p>` and takes no further screenshots.
2. The catcher runs `wl-paste --watch` for up to 180 s. Both `/usr/bin/wl-paste` and `wl-copy` are installed.
3. It makes one free authenticated read to validate the key:
   - fal `/v1/models/pricing`, WaveSpeed `/api/v3/models` and Replicate `/v1/models` all return 401 without a key (R8).
   - Runware, kie and MiniMax have no named call yet ⚠. Phase 1 measures one.
4. It writes the key atomically to the vault.
5. It clears both the clipboard and the primary selection.
6. It prints one status line, and no fragment of the key.
