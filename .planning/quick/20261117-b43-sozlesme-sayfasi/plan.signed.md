## 4. How it is verified

**a. On the draft, before anything moves**
- The map has no entry without an end; the auditor is handed the old file, the reading and the page and asked for a rule or an open leg that stands today and is on neither the page nor an owner.
- Every marker on the page is in the ledger, and beside each the ledger's "what" is quoted in the table — a registered id that says something else is a failure of this check, though the ruler would pass it.
- Nothing superseded came back, by meaning: besides searching for the seven old sentences, the auditor is given counter-examples that say an old rule in new words (e.g. "every accepted UGC is enlarged to full HD") and asked whether the page says any such thing.

**b. After the move**
- The archive is the old file: its `sha256sum` equals that of the file at the pinned commit (the hash written down before the move, not `HEAD`, which moves).
- The pointers reach the right content: for the board row and each file that pointed into the old file, the old target sentence and the new target are read side by side (one stale pointer is already known: a research page cites `B43.md:16`, an empty line today).
- `note-done.mjs` still works: run with both `--board` and `--rows-dir` on temporary copies; the note lands under `## Done notes`.
- The ruler's tests, written first, in pairs — red on the old ruler, green on the new: an unregistered claim in a row file; a registered claim; a dangling `OPEN` on a closed row; the same marked as history; a file under `archive/` with an unknown id, which must NOT be scanned.
- `ledger-truth` exits 0 with the row folder in scope; the records ruler and `tests/b43/records-truth.ts` pass.

## 5. The risks

- **A rule or a leg that stands is dropped in the shortening.** Held by the map over the whole file and the blind audit; the archive keeps everything, so a loss is repairable, never final.
- **A choice between two sentences that is not his.** One is known and asked (§2). Another met in the work is settled by the lead only when a later dated word of his plainly reaches it, in its scope; otherwise the work stops at it and it goes to him.
- **A scoped word of his is widened.** Each rule is written with the scope it was given (the UGC recipe, client videos, a single job).
- **The page narrows the mission.** The mission stands first and verbatim; nothing new is ruled.
- **The widened ruler stops other sessions' commits.** The 13 lines are put right in the commit that widens it; the ruler is run on the whole tree first.
- **The ruler still passes a registered marker that says something else** — older than this job and not repaired by it; on this page it is held by the table of step 2.
