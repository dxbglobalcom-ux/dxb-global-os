## C. Patterns across my rivals

1. **One door, named specialists behind it.** A single front agent routes to named role agents and speaks their names: 01 (Tom, Scout), 03 (Scout), 07 (CEO orchestrator plus six), 10 (NIMBUS plus nine). This is Hamza's shape. In 11/12 the owner is a department label ("RUN BY COO"), not a persona.
2. **The human sits at the outward-facing edge, not at every step.** 07 says "EVERY SEND IS YOURS". In 10 a diamond on the wire separates the risk officer from execution. In 03 the escalation bar is wired back into actions. In 11/12 a constraint is tethered to the one outward stage. In 01 a standing approval is reused. **Conflict:** 02 runs `Full access` with no gate (02:54, 365–367).
3. **Work runs on its own clock and reports itself.** 02 has Routines with "Ready ✓", 11/12 run at 08:00 and 16:00, 07 shows "WORKING 24/7" and "One daily brief", 10 publishes a daily brief. 08 differs: change-triggered (fingerprints), not timer-triggered.
4. **The report is pushed, even when empty.** 11/12 send "even on quiet days". 01 and 03 end with "What would you like me to handle first, sir?". 07 sends one daily brief. This matches W-C42-3.
5. **Every unit of work ends with a statement.** 07 uses `SENDER → RECIPIENT :: number`, 02 badges each unit with `Worked for 37s`, 11/12 promise `Every INBOX UID accounted for`. Our side: "0 of 1,543 events carry a finding" (07:138).
6. **The surface follows the work or the conversation.** 01 opens monitors per sentence, 09 draws the answer in ~3 s, 12's viewport moves to the stage named, 10 lights the speaking node, 04 speaks a sentence built from the board.
7. **Motion encodes state.** 10 dims idle nodes, 11/12 pulse in the direction of work with a visible rest. **Conflict:** P11-1/P12-1 rule 1 says "one pulse per real item… rest must be visible" (11:274–277). Ledger law 13 says a live link "flows always", bound to "up", not to a job (00:366–369). These are two different signals and the synthesis must keep both.
8. **Cheap determinism before paid judgement.** 12 sweeps trusted senders before any model runs (12:152, 179–184). 03 uses free Kokoro instead of per-reply credits (03:154–158).
9. **Outside data is what makes the briefing smart.** 01 and 03 read revenue and ads through connectors. 04 shows an outside-world board. We have zero connectors (03:295–296; 00:23–25).
10. **The decision record learns.** 10's LEDGER keeps "the bad ones", 08 keeps `decision_training_examples`, against our 68.7 % NULL outcome.

## D. What none of them solves that the holding needs

- **Concurrency at scale.** The largest roster is 9 agents (10), then 7 (07). None shows hundreds of agents working at once, queue depth, priority between departments, or several runs of one workflow side by side. We have 205 agents (10:190).
- **Depth of hierarchy.** Every system is flat: one orchestrator and specialists. None shows a department head who owns specialists and hands work across to another department, which is our 20+ departments with heads. "RUN BY COO" is a label, not a chain.
- **Checking the output, not the install.** Only 03 draws a supervisor loop, and as a diagram. 07's harness tests the setup, not the agents' work. 02 relies on the human spotting bugs. No rival shows a QA seat that verifies an agent's result against its brief before it reaches the owner. The closest checks are 11/12's accounting invariant and 08's exactly-once execution.
- **Cost per unit of work.** Only 08 has scoped budgets (code, not run). 03 mentions per-reply credits. No rival shows cost per run, per agent or per department on screen, or routes models by cost. Row 35's `COST & RUNTIME $4.31` is outside my share.
- **Approval at volume.** 07's "every send is yours" works for six agents. At 200+ agents it becomes the babysitting the CEO forbids. Only 01 (a standing approval reused) and row 30's ladder (outside my share) point toward earned autonomy. Our `autonomy_level` is 0 on all 205 agents (00:437).
- **The round trip through one door.** 01 shows delegation to Tom and a PR coming back. No rival shows the whole loop: intake → Hamza → owning employee → check → result back to the CEO, with state visible at each hop, across many requests in flight.
- **Recovery from stalls.** Only 08 has watchdogs and continuation (R). Every filmed system shows the happy path.
- **Constitutional boundaries inside the flow.** None has a fail-closed religious or ethical gate. Ours fires and is recorded (07:142, 244–246).
- **Unknown scheduler machinery.** No film settles it: 01:106–108, 11:198–199, 12:304–305 are all UNVERIFIED. Their clocks are shown, not explained.
