## 1. The principle

The foundation's six stages are the spine. Every row joins at the stage that gives it what it stands on, and not
before: a row is never built on the old system and then moved. Today's order was drawn on the old engine — its first
eighteen ranks repaired that engine and its database one defect at a time. Those repairs are no longer jobs of their
own: the foundation writes the part new and the defect goes with the old part, so the row closes with the stage's own
evidence. What stays outside the spine is small: the rows that stand on nothing the foundation gives (the backup, the
network wall, his own clicks and timings) and the drawing of his screens, which starts beside stage 0. The offices on
the board — the media studio, the web studio, the advertising seat, the revenue lines — join one at a time once the
gate, the lanes and the job stand, by PLAN §2 M. The board's own laws hold: one row after another, each part tried on
the bench and set aside, the key turned once.
