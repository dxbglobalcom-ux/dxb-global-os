# Astra — the blind audit of the amendment's second draft (2026-11-22)

GPT-6.2 Astra at high, one blind read-only pass over `blind/AMENDMENT.md`; the verdict as it wrote it. The lead's rewrite to these findings is `AMENDMENT.md`.

**REFUTED — The amendment is not ready for the CEO. An approved media job can take a path its price card did not authorize, a client delivery can miss his release, and an office handler can act beyond the request he approved.**  
**The smaller design is viable on paper. B84 can commission B43 on the site job’s existing desk and price card. Whether both offices can run without a foundation change remains UNVERIFIED.**

### Conditions recorded before opening the amendment

I used four tests from the brief. The same job must trace the client’s order through price, production, checked artifact, release, invoice, receipt and cost. Another office must be able to contribute without losing that chain. A retry, a second client at the same company, an external message or a new office must never enlarge anyone’s authority. Model choice and visible activity must reflect the work actually being done. Any additional mechanism must earn its place against something B84, B43 or B83 already needs.

This was one blind, read-only pass. I read the target plan, synthesis §§3–5, amendment and the cited owning records. I wrote no files. The verdicts below concern the design. No runtime, provider charge or installation was tested.
