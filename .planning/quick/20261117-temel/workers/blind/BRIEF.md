# B85 §4 — how a job is worked by the holding's workers: the brief

## The holding

This holding produces high technology, aims at large revenue and will found large companies; its goals are very
large. Its business lines' clients are small, medium and large, reaching up to the biggest clients in the world.

The holding's founding purposes are technological advancement and revenue generation. It is one AI-native operating
system for one holding: one human in it, the CEO, who states a goal and approves what the approval gate stops at him;
the system runs the companies end to end, 7/24, visibly alive. He is the owner, not an engineer: he gives the idea,
the engineers judge whether it works. Islamic boundaries are constitutional and his alone.

   how work is checked — so that the foundation's tables and engine (PLAN.md §2 D–I, §3) can be built for it.
2. Rest every element on tried knowledge with a result: the holding's own measured record, or the model maker's
   current documentation. Where neither exists, say so plainly and name the small measurement that will settle it —
   never cover an unproven idea with rules.
3. Take nothing from a single remark of his as a standing rule. What he asks of one job belongs to that job.
4. Decide the persona question as engineers, critically — his view is a lead, not the evidence.
5. Stay small: the foundation carries what every job type needs and no more.
6. Keep what already holds: the standing decisions (one Postgres, no second runtime or agent framework, the approval
