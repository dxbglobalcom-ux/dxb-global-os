# B85 §4 — how a job is worked (the section as proposed)

It replaces PLAN.md §4 whole. Each element names where it is proven: **[ours]** the holding's own record,
**[maker]** the model maker's current documentation, **[outside]** a published measurement on other models,
**[unproven]** — proven nowhere yet; such an element is measured inside stage 1 before anything stands on it. The
evidence, with its sources and grades, is `EVIDENCE.md` (sections A–H).

## The design

1. **One owner does the coupled work itself.** A job has one master. It holds the whole and makes what hangs
   together; decisions that bind each other stay in one context.
   - [ours] In four studies the single maker finished in half to a fifth of the team's time and at a quarter to a
     thirtieth of its cost, on the same brief (A1–A4). Each study is confounded; all point one way.
   - [maker] "domains that require all agents to share the same context or involve many dependencies between agents
     are not a good fit"; "Handing the same task to a subagent doesn't make the answer any better." (F)
   - What is not shown: that either way gives better work. No study of ours judged a working team blind (A6).

2. **A second worker is opened on four grounds and no other:** (a) parts that are independent and can run side by
   side; (b) a read or a search that would flood the master's context; (c) a tool or a permission the master does not
   hold; (d) a check that must be independent.
   - [maker] The stated benefits of a subagent are context isolation, parallelization, specialized instructions and
     tool restrictions; parallel teams "kept answer quality comparable to the single agent's while finishing
     considerably sooner" (F).
   - [ours] The construction runs this way every day (E).
   - **Three hands** is ground (a): variants built apart, one chosen, the others' good sides merged (B84 decision 4,
     his agreed plan). The foundation carries it — parts in parallel, a choice that waits on him, a merge part. Its
     record is one result, the merged olive page, which he closed as "fena değil … tamam" (A4); the product's own
     Ultra Code mode has never run here (A3). It is a mechanism that works, not yet a proven road to work he praises.

3. **A worker is a seat, not a character.** A seat is a few lines: what it does, the tools and data it may touch, its
   model and effort, the skill attached. The 213 persona files leave the work path and are archived whole.
   - [ours] No study shows persona seats improving a result (A6). The persona roads produced no praised work; both
     strictly praised works came from one session working the tools directly (B) — counts too small to prove harm.
   - [ours] The cost is measured: a persona file averages 20.9 KB and is read in every session of its seat (D); in the
     forced team run 233,397 of 312,470 output tokens were the specialists' (A1).
   - [ours] The construction's own seats are defined this way and carry its daily work (E).
   - [maker] A one-sentence role "focuses Claude's behavior and tone"; no measured effect is published and the newest
     model pages have no role section (F). [outside] Three papers find a persona does not raise accuracy — none on a
     Claude model (G).
   - The grade, said plainly: that personas help — no evidence anywhere; that they cost — measured; that they harm
     the work — not shown. The decision rests on the cost and on the absence of any benefit, and it can be undone
     because nothing is deleted.
   - **The persona text is not mined into checklists.** 81 % of it is role-specific method written by a model (D).
     [outside] Self-written skills score below no skills (G); [ours] 202 recipes written by helper agents gave twelve
     rejected samples out of twelve (B).
   - **Section 12 stays in force.** "Discipline DNA & Islamic conduct" is constitutional and reaches a worker today
     only through its persona file (D). It moves, word for word, into the standing layer every seat reads. Its text
     is not edited by this job.
   - The seats that own work keep a name and a one-sentence role: Hamza, Reception, the master of each job type, the
     checker, Hermes. Hamza's character is his face to the CEO and stays B64's.

4. **A job type's procedure is a skill, in the maker's own form** — a short page loaded when the job starts, files and
   scripts beside it opened on need. It is the mechanism the construction's doors already are (E), not a new one.
   - **Attached by the job type, in code** — never left to the worker to find. [outside] A skill left to be invoked
     was never invoked in 56 % of cases (G); [ours] a master left free called none of its six specialists (A6).
   - **Written by an engineer from proven material and audited blind before use,** as a door is changed today. The
     material: the reasons he rejected work of that type and the audit findings on it, both on record; the maker's
     rules for the tools; real reference material he chose; work he praised. [outside] Expert-written skills
     33.9 → 50.5 % (G); [ours] the blind audit finds real defects (C).
   - **Nothing a job produces changes a skill by itself.** A skill changes by a deliberate, audited edit. The earlier
     draft's lessons proposed at a job's close, book owners, rules for contradicting lessons and tool-fact cards are
     removed with the mechanism they patched. [outside] self-written skills harm (G).
   - **[unproven] That a skill makes OUR jobs better.** Stage 1 settles it: the one thin job is done by one master
     with its skill and without, the two deliveries are judged by him blind, the cost of each is recorded. If the
     skill does not help, job types run from their brief and pattern alone.

5. **What a job type always needs is a part its pattern creates, and the engine opens it** — the master's judgement
   adds parts, with a reason; it does not decide the mandatory ones.
   - Same proof as the attachment above (A6, G). [ours] The construction fixes its audit as a step of the flow, not
     as the lead's choice (E).

6. **The check is a separate worker with a fresh context that reads the brief and the artifact and verifies** — on
   the jobs AGENT_ORCHESTRATION A28 and A32 already name; a part of the pattern, never a standing instruction.
   - [ours] 15 audited jobs, with defects that would have reached him wrong; a second blind auditor found what the
     first had not in 2 jobs of 2 (C).
   - [maker] "A fresh reviewer does help, because it hasn't seen your reasoning"; a standalone evaluator is "worth the
     cost when the task sits beyond what the current model does reliably solo"; on the 5.x models a standing "use a
     subagent to verify" costs about a third more "with no change in quality" (F). "A reviewer that's asked to find
     problems will always find *something*" — so the check is against the brief (his rule of 2026-10-12).

7. **Work starts from his words and from real material.** The brief carries what he said, verbatim, and the real
   things the job is about — the product's own photographs, the references he chose, the brand's files; where such
   material exists the work does not start from text alone.
   - [ours] Both strictly praised works started so; all twelve text-only samples were rejected; storyboards built
     from the most-liked real ads' own prompts were liked the night those from a session's own ideas were rejected
     (B). Small counts, one direction.
   - **[unproven] How a one-line order becomes a full brief.** The failure is measured — on 18 Nov a master given one
     line took the safest reading: "nerede yaratıcılık" (A1). No remedy is measured. Stage 1 tries one: the job
     type's skill states what is collected and what is attempted before the work starts, the brief so produced is
     recorded, and his verdict on the delivery is the measure.

8. **His verdict is a recorded fact of the artifact:** praised, closed ("okey"), or rejected. Only praised work is
   offered as an example when a skill is written. What he asks of one job is in that job's brief and ends with it.
   (His words of 2026-11-22; a record, not a mechanism.)

9. **Context — PLAN.md §3 stands.** Further proof now on file: [maker] context rot is named; the remedies are
   compaction, structured notes and sub-agents; the newest models find state on the file system well; structured
   state is overwritten less than prose (F). [ours] a fresh session cost 5.62 $ where each resumed round of a giant
   one cost 8–16 $ (A5).

10. **Nothing more is built to answer the open questions.** Every run already records its seat, model, effort, time,
    tokens, cost, its check's result and his verdict (PLAN.md §2 D, G). Those rows are the measure.
