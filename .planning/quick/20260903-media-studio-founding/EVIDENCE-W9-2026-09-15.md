## 1. THE RULE CONFLICT I HIT, AND WHAT I DID ABOUT IT

The plan's step 2 says the compiler should emit "a per-employee profile = the home department's kit + the media
group". **As the compiler stood, that is impossible without breaking a constitutional rule**, and I did not
quietly choose:

```
HOLDING_LIBRARY_SPEC A4: "applied as INTERSECTION onto the policy profile
                          (PERMISSION_MODEL G2 — library can narrow, never widen).
                          Employees ... get <slug>.employee.mcp.json overlay profiles
                          (= dept surface ∩ their union)."
PERMISSION_MODEL G2:     "... en dar kesişim geçerli."
generate-profiles.ts:    const deptManifest = profiles.find(p => p.department === emp.department);
                         ... kept = allowed.filter(tool => granted.has(tool))
```

An overlay is the intersection with the HOME department's surface. Design and marketing carry no media tool, so
the intersection is empty and the grant would have done nothing.

**What I built instead, and why it does not loosen the rule:** the assignment raises the employee's **CEILING** to
the surfaces of every department it is a *recorded member of* — home plus assigned — while the cut stays an
intersection. The employee still cannot exceed the surface of a department it belongs to. **What widened is the
MEMBERSHIP, in the org record, not the library's power over policy.** That is exactly what an assignment IS.

**And a second decision, narrower than the plan needed:** the assigned department's own grants are **not** merged
into the employee's granted set. A borrowed seat adds only what it holds BY NAME. Otherwise it would silently
inherit every tool the host department ever gains. Measured both ways before choosing — with the wide version the
two seats came out at 28 tools, identical to the narrow version today, so nothing was lost by taking the narrow road.

Both are registered: `PERMISSION_MODEL.md` **G2-bis**, `HOLDING_LIBRARY_SPEC.md` **A4**, and in the code's own header.
