## Job 1 — a 10-second UGC video

**Client:** Halde, a small maker of reusable steel drinking bottles, selling online worldwide.
**Product:** the Halde 500 ml brushed-steel bottle with screw cap and steel straw. Real photographs of it are in
`urun/` (8 files). The bottle in the video must be this bottle — its shape, cap, finish — not a similar one.
**What they want:** one vertical video, 10 seconds, with sound, that looks like a real customer filmed it on a phone
and posted it: someone in an ordinary place showing the bottle and saying in one or two natural sentences, in English,
why they carry it every day. No studio look, no advertising voice. It will run as a paid social ad.
**Deliver:** `teslim/halde-ugc.mp4` (9:16), and `teslim/TESLIM.md` saying in a few lines what you made, how, and
anything you would tell the client about it.
**Terms:** no real, identifiable person is imitated. You decide everything else. Do not ask questions; where the
brief is silent, choose as a professional would and say what you chose in `TESLIM.md`.

## Job 2 — a set of product advertising stills

**Client:** Kestrel Goods, a leather workshop selling a snap-tab card wallet online worldwide.
**Product:** the Kestrel card wallet in brown leather with a small embossed "K". Real photographs of it are in `urun/`
(7 files). Every image must show this wallet — its leather, stitching, snap tab and emboss — not a similar one. The
small maker's text inside the wallet is not shown.
**What they want:** five finished advertising images for the launch: (1) a hero image for the shop's home page,
16:9; (2) a clean product image on a plain ground for the product page, 1:1; (3) a lifestyle image — the wallet in
someone's day, 4:5; (4) a close detail of the leather and stitching, 1:1; (5) a social post image with room for a
short headline, and the headline on it, 4:5. One look across all five, so they read as one campaign.
**Deliver:** `teslim/kestrel-01.png` … `kestrel-05.png`, and `teslim/TESLIM.md` saying in a few lines what you made,
how, and anything you would tell the client about it.
**Terms:** no real, identifiable person is imitated; no other brand's mark appears. You decide everything else. Do
not ask questions; where the brief is silent, choose as a professional would and say what you chose in `TESLIM.md`.

## Job 4 — a motion-graphics ad film made in code

**Client:** Kestrel Goods (the same workshop as job 2), for the wallet's launch.
**Product:** the same wallet; the same real photographs are in `urun/`.
**What they want:** one motion-graphics film, 15 seconds, 16:9, made in code and rendered to video — animated type,
shapes and the real product photographs moving together, the kind of launch film a design studio makes: an opening

**How:** in code, with a tool of your choice that you install yourself (Remotion is one; you may choose another).
**Deliver:** `teslim/kestrel-launch.mp4`, the project's source in `kaynak/`, and `teslim/TESLIM.md` saying in a few

  sound and music, VFX and post). The master is told the six are there to be called; how it uses them is its own.
- **Arm "Tek usta":** the folder's master is told in three lines that it is the studio's master for this job, holds
  B43's contract page as a file, and is free to work alone or to open subagents of its own making.
