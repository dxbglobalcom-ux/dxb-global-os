Making the Internet a Bit Less Sh\*t: Inside Resn’s Digital Experiences
=======================================================================

A look at how Resn combines brand, content, technology and a healthy disregard for boring websites to create digital experiences people actually want to explore.

[Studio/Agency Spotlight](https://tympanus.net/codrops/tag/studio-spotlight/)

[![](https://tympanus.net/codrops/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ResnWebsite_Interactive4_4x3.png?x95600)](https://resn.co.nz/ "Making the Internet a Bit Less Sh*t: Inside Resn’s Digital Experiences Demo")

[Visit](https://resn.co.nz/)

We are Resn, a boutique creative studio with harbour views and hot-and-cold running digital. Founded in 2004, we bring brand, content and digital experiences to life for global brands and emerging innovators. We are a global team spread across time zones.

We learned a while back that remote collaboration works just fine for us, so rather than commit to a single city, we’ve leaned into being reachable from wherever we happen to be.

We are made up of a few select human beings and hundreds of trillions of microorganisms. The humans do the work and the microorganisms keep the humans alive. It’s a beautiful relationship. Together, we like to create things that poke you in the brain with a surprising and delightful stick.

The Journey
-----------

Resn began over a drink in the pub in the highly exotic and mysterious country of Noo Zeeland. The World Wide Web was only about a decade old. YouTube didn’t even exist. Facebook was just a twinkle in Zuck’s then-human eye. And websites were designed around an aesthetic that we would describe as “not really designed at all”.

Enter a young Rik Campbell and equally young Steve Le Marquand. They bonded over a beer and a simple dream: to make the internet less shit. “Let’s call ourselves Resin,” they cried, enjoying the tactile stickiness of the word. But, it was already taken. So, they simply disem-vowelled it and became Resn instead. Gathering a posse of like-minded thinkers and creators, Resn went off into the unknown wilds of the internet to make its fortune.

It has been more than two decades now, which is quite a long time, especially in this industry. It is not something we think about very often, but every now and then, we look around and say, hey, we are still here, we are still independent and we are still producing work that we are proud of. So, we must be doing something right. That, or we’re so old and out of touch that we’ve lost all sense of taste and perspective. There’s just no way to tell.  
  
That said, the best way to get an idea of who we are is to look at the work we’ve created. So, let’s do a roundup of some of our recent projects.

Our Work
--------

### [Squarespace Foundations](https://brand.squarespace.com/)

Bringing the Squarespace brand to life through an interactive digital experience.

[](https://tympanus.net/codrops/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Awwwards_SquareSpace_1.mp4)

We usually go absolutely feral when there’s a really great brand guideline drop. There goes the weekend! But, we realise perusing brand bibles and style guidelines is not everyone’s cup of tea. So, when Squarespace asked us to help bring their brand guidelines online and turn them into something people would actually want to explore, we were pretty excited. Squarespace Foundations is a journey through the building blocks of the Squarespace brand. Logo, Typography, Colour, Motion, Photography and Campaign each become their own chapter, complete with custom interactions, playful motion, and enough visual polish to make a style guide feel surprisingly, even suspiciously, entertaining. Makes you wonder what the video is interlaced with.

### [Savor](https://savor.it/)

Creating a rich, sensory experience celebrating science, sustainability and the joy of good food.

[](https://tympanus.net/codrops/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Highlight-1-main-scroll.mp4)

Savor makes butter, but not as you know it. Normal butter is made from cow udder secretions. Yeah, sounds pretty weird when you put it like that. Instead, Savor takes carbon from the atmosphere and turns it directly into fat, removing the cow, and its greenhouse emissions, from the equation. Named one of Time Magazine’s Best Inventions of 2025, Savor has produced ingredients that have been used in Michelin-starred restaurants and even high-end beauty products. Savor came to us with a big story to tell and a challenge to make it feel luxurious, natural and delicious. We created a platform that, while still a little bit science-y, puts nature at the heart of the story. The result is a rich, tactile experience reinforcing the sensory pleasure of food made from real fat. We guarantee it will be one of the most delicious websites you visit today, so what are you waiting for?

### [Breakthrough Energy](https://www.breakthroughenergy.org/)

A global platform to accelerate clean energy innovation and build the industries of the future.

[](https://tympanus.net/codrops/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Video-01.mp4)

We think everyone would agree that the planet is going through it right now. We’re not going to list all of our issues, but it’s fair to say energy is one of the biggest. Our appetite for it only seems to grow, and we need to figure out how to meet that demand without cooking ourselves in the process. Breakthrough Energy was founded ten years ago as an incubator fund for green energy initiatives. It has grown into a driving force behind a portfolio of companies developing the technologies and industries that could transform our energy systems. We partnered with Breakthrough Energy to reimagine its digital home around that opportunity. Rather than focusing on what needs to be reduced, we focused on what can be built. While there’s no giant “fix everything” button (trust us, we looked), we were happy to support Breakthrough Energy by creating a platform that’s as bold as its mission. Check out the website. We hope it inspires a little optimism.

### [Tracing Art](https://www.getty.edu/tracingart/)

Uncovering the hidden stories behind how artworks travel through time.

[](https://tympanus.net/codrops/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/element-3.mp4)

Before this project, we had no idea what went on behind closed doors in art archives. Let’s be honest, pretty much no one did. While the world wasn’t exactly crying out to know, Getty was like, “hold my stock images”, and came to us with the idea for an immersive, interactive experience that goes behind the scenes in this hidden world. Tracing Art gives you a fascinating peek into the human stories hidden behind the art, aimed at anyone curious to understand how artworks travel through time. Basically, we took an enormous pile of historical records and asked ourselves, “Can we make this feel less like data and more like uncovering a historical mystery?” It turns out, yes. Yes, we can. We wish we had known this years ago. See, Art History is a career, Mom!

Recognition
-----------

Awards, we like to say, aren’t important to us right up until we win one, at which point there are tears and group hugs, and then we go back to pretending none of it matters. The folks at FWA and Awwwards played a real role in getting us taken seriously early on, and that recognition compounding over two decades is part of why we’re still standing. Our favourite clients tend to have three things in common: real trust in what we do, realistic budgets, and a willingness to collaborate.

What’s Next
-----------

Twenty years in, the goal hasn’t changed in the slightest: to make the internet a bit less shit than we found it. The only real difference is that our patience for mediocrity is at an all time low. So, if your brand, content, or website is feeling a bit “same-sy” and needs a shakeup like a digital snowglobe, then hit us up at: [newbusiness@resn.co.nz](mailto:newbusiness@resn.co.nz). We can help.
