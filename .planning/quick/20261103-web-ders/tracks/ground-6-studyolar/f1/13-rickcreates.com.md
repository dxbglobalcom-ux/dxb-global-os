Site of the Year Teardowns
==========================

Each year Awwwards names one Site of the Year and the FWA names one FWA of the Year. This section tears down those winners year by year. Each report examines the concept, visual design, interaction and front-end build behind the award, with performance and asset data measured in a real browser.

* ![Black Mustang on red desert sand under a giant red planet with the title FUEL FROM THE AIR](/reports/2021-05-04-prometheus-fuels/card.webp?v=400a6497)

  2021Awwwards**8.40**FWA**88 points**SOTYFOTY

  [Prometheus FuelsPrometheus Fuels](/r/2021-05-04-prometheus-fuels)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------

  Prometheus Fuels is an American startup that turns carbon dioxide from the air into gasoline, diesel and jet fuel, and this is the website it launched in 2021, which tells the company's process as a 3D road trip. As the visitor scrolls, a black Mustang drives across a red desert. The camera follows it through a field of fuel forges, into a giant fan and down among the molecules that become fuel, and finally stops at a gas station. The whole world is drawn like an old travel poster, and at three points along the road the site asks the visitor to hold the space bar and do the step themselves.

* ![Home page with a dark box full of blue, white and black 3D cross-shaped blocks](/reports/2023-10-02-lusion-v3/card.webp?v=005aef51)

  2023Awwwards**8.25**FWA**89 points**SOTYFOTY

  [Lusion v3Lusion v3](/r/2023-10-02-lusion-v3)
  ---------------------------------------------

  Lusion v3 is the third version of the portfolio site of Lusion, a British 3D and interactive studio, and it shows what the studio can do with 3D storytelling and interactive web work. A 3D picture runs through the whole home page, and as visitors scroll down, the picture moves forward one stretch at a time. The home page starts with a pile of white and dark grey cross-shaped blocks, a few of them in a bright accent colour. The cursor pushes them away as it comes near, and a click changes the accent colour. At the end of the home page, an astronaut flies through a glowing tunnel to a closing screen reading Let's work together.

* ![The cursor reveals Lando Norris's gold McLaren helmet across the opening portrait](/reports/2025-11-20-lando-norris/card.webp?v=7df4e756)

  2025Awwwards**8.18**FWA**85 points**SOTY

  [Lando NorrisLando Norris](/r/2025-11-20-lando-norris)
  ------------------------------------------------------

  Lando Norris is the personal website of the McLaren F1 driver Lando Norris, and it uses the driver's racing helmet as the thread that runs from the opening portrait through a hall of fame of helmets to the footer. At the start, a front-on portrait of Lando Norris fills the screen, and wherever the cursor passes over the face, pieces of a painted helmet show through as if the cursor were stirring liquid. Resting the cursor on the NEXT RACE card pulls the whole helmet up over the head. As the visitor scrolls down, the portrait shrinks into a card and a neon-green signature writes itself across the photo stroke by stroke.

* ![Opening screen: a corn plant lit by a beam of light, with the headline CORN. REVOLUTIONIZED.](/reports/2020-07-08-pioneer-corn-revolutionized/card.webp?v=da1bceaa)

  2020Awwwards**8.18**FWA**88 points**SOTY

  [Pioneer - Corn RevolutionizedPioneer - Corn Revolutionized](/r/2020-07-08-pioneer-corn-revolutionized)
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Pioneer - Corn Revolutionized is a promotional site for the American seed brand Pioneer that turns a new corn seed's journey from lab to farm into one unbroken 3D camera move driven by scrolling. At the start, the screen shows a corn plant. As the visitor scrolls down, the plant becomes a glowing DNA strand and the strand scatters into points of light across the screen. The points become seeds dropping into a pot, the seeds grow into a seedling, and the camera rises high to look down on a grid of test plots. Once the story ends, the camera returns to the first corn plant, and the visitor can keep scrolling forever.

  CinematicScroll
* ![Home page with a purple-haired Keeper character and KEEP. PROTECT. REIMAGINE.](/reports/2022-12-27-kpr/card.webp?v=564fea02)

  2022Awwwards**7.98**FWA**85 points**SOTY

  [KPRKPR](/r/2022-12-27-kpr)
  ---------------------------

  KPR is the website of a digital-collectibles brand that released ten thousand illustrated characters called Keepers, and it turns their world into a set of concept art that visitors leaf through as they scroll. At the start, a purple-haired Keeper fills the screen, with KEEP. PROTECT. REIMAGINE. in three lines at the lower right. As the visitor scrolls down, the illustration of the Keep shrinks into a card with a folded tab and slides off screen, and the Factions illustration rises from below to fill the picture. Holding the mouse down on the Factions and World illustrations shows the objects hidden in each picture through the view of a scanner.

* ![An igloo among snowy mountains, its ice blocks lifting apart with glowing edges](/reports/2024-07-23-igloo-inc/card.webp?v=9961e357)

  2024Awwwards**7.92**FWA**86 points**SOTY

  [Igloo IncIgloo Inc](/r/2024-07-23-igloo-inc)
  ---------------------------------------------

  Igloo Inc is the website of Igloo, a crypto consumer-brand company. The site freezes the company and its brands into a field of ice, and scrolling down sends the camera from an igloo on a snowy mountain deep into the ice. At the start, the screen shows that igloo, and when the cursor comes near, its ice blocks lift and glow one by one. The camera then passes through an ice fog with rainbow edges, past ice blocks holding two brands and a glowing ring, and stops in front of a penguin made of well over a hundred thousand particles. When the visitor keeps scrolling, the camera returns to the igloo.

  CinematicScroll
