People
======

We’re creating a space where creatives truly belong. Where arts and crafts are part of everyday life, and where enthusiasts can create without limits. By merging content and technology, we’re pushing the boundaries of what’s possible to shape the future of the web.

We keep it fresh, collaborative, and never too serious (except when it matters). Big ideas, sharp execution, and a team that’s always striving for more. That’s how we do it.

Our Values
----------

Fueled by Passion

![](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2F9syto90m%2Fproduction%2F4be7bd27ba78a78bde9372bdd1252f15ec7349c3-1674x1620.webp&w=2560&q=75)

We are fortunate to have the opportunity to do what we love. Recognizing this, we commit ourselves to being grateful and respectful towards one another. We are a group of curious, optimistic, and enthusiastic individuals who deeply care about all of our initiatives. Feeling proud of what we do is the energy that fuels us, that keeps us motivated and excited.

Push Together as a Team

![](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2F9syto90m%2Fproduction%2F4bcc67cab37d7e93c97aaf955e712ebf8e11fb26-1674x1620.webp&w=2560&q=75)

We are characterized by our freshness and good sense of humor. We enjoy sharing, having fun, and celebrating moments together. However, we also take the time to reflect and identify what we have learned, as well as acknowledge our mistakes when things do not go well. We strive to act as one, always together.

Improve by 1% Every Day

![](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2F9syto90m%2Fproduction%2Ff7d8d95259b5f4ed33e248850e1ea1c843317387-1674x1620.webp&w=2560&q=75)

We believe that recognizing our own limitations helps us identify opportunities and encourages teamwork. Each of us is a specialist and shines in our unique way. We welcome challenges, persist through obstacles, and learn from our mistakes. This motivates us to ask for help when needed and be accountable to each other.
