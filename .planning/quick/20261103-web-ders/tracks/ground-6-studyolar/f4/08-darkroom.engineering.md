darkroom.engineering — design and development studio
----------------------------------------------------

darkroom.engineering is a global design and development studio that turns ambitious digital ideas into reliable products. Our team works across design, front-end and back-end engineering, creative development, motion, WebGL, headless commerce, and API integrations. We partner with teams that care about craft, speed, accessibility, and systems that remain maintainable after launch. The result is polished work that loads quickly, scales cleanly, and gives product teams the confidence to keep shipping.

Studio work and capabilities
----------------------------

Our selected work spans interactive brand experiences, product interfaces, web games, commerce, and open-source tooling. We combine design fluency with engineering discipline, then document the system so the people who inherit it can understand and extend it. Learn more about the studio on our [about page](/about), review examples on the [work page](/work), or [contact us](/contact) to discuss a project.

Developer resources
-------------------

Developers and software agents can inspect our [developer documentation](/developers), read the machine-readable [OpenAPI specification](/openapi.json), and use [llms.txt](/llms.txt) to discover public resources. These documents describe the available endpoints, response formats, and integration guidance without requiring client-side JavaScript.

01. who we are

where things get developed.
---------------------------

you might've seen our work in the wild — [ibi.cash](https://ibi.cash), [looped](https://looped.poly.ai), [badomensofficial.com](https://badomensofficial.com), [orderful.com](https://orderful.com). we're the team behind the curtain, making sure it loads fast, looks sharp, and holds up when it matters most.

02. Mission

build software that works.
--------------------------

we make software that doesn't break, doesn't flake, and doesn't waste your time. sometimes it's a website, sometimes it's a product, sometimes it's an installation in buenos aires. we design, code, and launch in a tight loop — with just enough process to keep momentum without killing creativity. if it runs on code and needs to work well, we're probably a good fit.

03. Invitation

let's talk.
-----------

if you're looking for a team that genuinely cares about your product, your users, and your constraints — we're here for that. we don't do flashy pitches or empty promises. we do the work, with you in the loop, building something that actually solves the thing you hired us for. if that sounds like what you need, let's talk.
