To start with

Dogstudio is a multidisciplinary creative studio at the intersection of art, design and technology. We are on a mission to explore, create meaning and provoke emotions through design and storytelling.

![]()

Soo... What?

We believe in the power of leveraging design, stories and emotions to help small & major brands, organisations and businesses engage human beings on any kind of medium.

Are you tired of reading the same descriptions about those company claiming they’re different from another but really they’re not?  
 Fine! Because we’re tired too, so why don’t we directly skip to the part where you understand how we can help you?

* Brand Strategy
* Branding & Art Direction
* Digital Products
* Emerging Technologies  
  (You know... AR, VR, AI etc...)
* Interactive Installations
* Visual Design
* Animation & Illustration

To start with

Our goal is to deliver amazing experiences that make people talk, and build strategic value for brands, tech, entertainment, arts & culture.

Dog is in the details
---------------------

Eleanor said, “Be flexible, but stick to your principles”, and she was damn right.  
 Over the years, we built our own mindset, born from our past experiences, both in success and failures, but all inspired by the same backbone : the more you get in the details of every layer of a brand, product or experience, and the more you’re likely to leave a strong mark on your audience. Peace.

![]()![]()

This is how we roll
-------------------

We know our business, but we surely don’t know yours, but it’s great, because we also strongly believe amazing work comes from great relationships and we’re convinced our full potential can only be released when we all work together, inside and outside the company (or during buzzed late-night discussions)

Before collaborating on anything, we always start with getting to know each other.

Your needs, your goals, your expectations, your story, your team, and even, hopefully maybe your taste in Belgian beers.

Now, you’re looking for a team to produce whatever you want without taking the time to get to know us, and thoroughly tell us more about your situation? Well...here’s the way out.
