What Makes an AI Web Design Company Different?
----------------------------------------------

Real AI expertise goes beyond using tools to generate layouts. A strong agency has a clear process, uses human review to protect quality, and understands how AI features affect the user experience. It should also build websites with readable content, useful headings, mobile performance, and accessible interactions.

Look for real project examples, clear deliverables, client reviews, and a documented process. Ask how the agency uses AI, who makes final design decisions, and how it tests the finished website.

How Much Does AI Web Design Cost?
---------------------------------

The source article gives these estimated project ranges:

* Landing page: $1,500–$3,000
* Small business website: $3,000–$7,000
* Multi-page corporate website: $7,000–$15,000
* Website with custom AI features: $10,000–$50,000
* SaaS or web app design: $30,000–$100,000+

Design Monks lists starting prices from $1,800. Final costs depend on page count, custom features, platform, content, integrations, testing, and ongoing support. Treat these figures as planning estimates, not fixed quotes.

How to Choose the Right Agency
------------------------------

Start with clear goals, a defined budget, and a realistic launch date. Prepare your brand assets, content, and examples of websites you like.

Before hiring, ask about AI tools, human oversight, accessibility, mobile speed, ownership of files and code, copyright risks, revisions, and post-launch support.

Final Thoughts
--------------

The best agency for your business depends on what you need. Some teams need a conversion-focused website, while others need custom AI features or complex product experiences.   
Compare each agency’s process, proof, pricing, and technical skills before making your decision.
